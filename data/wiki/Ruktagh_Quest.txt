==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "I see your menu has a selection of goblin food, including ruktagh. How are you getting fresh dill out here?! Well, I'm sure the dill isn't fresh. But the meal had better be!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
I see your menu has a selection of goblin food, including ruktagh. How are you getting fresh dill out here?! Well, I'm sure the dill isn't fresh. But the meal had better be!

===Midway===
Where is my ruktagh?!

===Requirements===
* Craft Ruktagh
* (Keep Ruktagh)
* Talk to Ailred
