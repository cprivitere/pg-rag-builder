==Activity Quest==
[[Horse Thieves (Eltibule)]] is an [[Activity Quests|Activity Quest]] which does not appear in the quest log. It shares the displayed name '''Horses Rescued''' with all other Horse Thieves quests.

===Prerequisites===
This [[Activity Quests|Activity Quest]] is automatically completed when the Horse Thieves in [[Eltibule]] are defeated.
