==Summary==
Ragabir in Red Wing Casino said, "So much for dwarves being great miners. I need a little bit of paladium, and you'd think with all these idiot dwarves around that'd be easy. Nope. Not easy. Are you any better than a dwarf? I just need a few chunks! I'll pay well!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ragabir]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
