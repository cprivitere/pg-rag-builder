==Summary==
Velkort has told me the frequency for the giant red crystal. All I have to do is touch it while thinking the right word. Once I'm past the crystal, I should try to figure out who's behind this and why.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Velkort]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. The [[Unlocking the Crystal Lattice]] quest must be completed first.

===Preface===
{{Quote|You forgot something again: you're a simpleton. Of course it's a frequency! Now go visit the giant red crystal on the lake, touch it while thinking Alawambat, and you'll slip past it to whatever is underneath.}}

===Requirements===
* Enter the cave beneath the crystal
* Get to the bottom of this
* Talk to Velkort
