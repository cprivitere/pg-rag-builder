==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "Where the heck were you all this time?! No matter. How are your vexfish lettuce wraps? Fresh, I hope? I'll take that! And please make it snappy. I'm famished from waiting!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
Where the heck were you all this time?! No matter. How are your vexfish lettuce wraps? Fresh, I hope? I'll take that! And please make it snappy. I'm famished from waiting!

===Midway===
Yes, I ordered the vexfish lettuce wraps? What's the hold up?

===Requirements===
* Craft Vexfish Lettuce Wraps
* (Keep Vexfish Lettuce Wraps)
* Talk to Ailred
