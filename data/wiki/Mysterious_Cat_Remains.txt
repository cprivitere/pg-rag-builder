==Summary==
Grakelsput in Povus said, "Once, long ago, giant cats roamed Povus. But we've only managed to resurrect a few specimens! We're so early in our research of this creature that we don't even want to study their behavior yet: I just need bones to study! If you find giant cat bones, please bring them to me."

This Quest can be repeated after 5 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Grakelsput]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor. 

===Preface===
Once, long ago, giant cats roamed Povus. But we've only managed to resurrect a few specimens! We're so early in our research of this creature that we don't even want to study their behavior yet: I just need bones to study! If you find giant cat bones, please bring them to me.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Ancient Cat Jawbone
* Obtain Bone Tail Assembly
* Talk to Grakelsput
