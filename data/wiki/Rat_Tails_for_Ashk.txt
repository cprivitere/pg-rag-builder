==Summary==
Ashk the Answerer in Rahu said, "I like to keep a handful of stock ingredients on hand, and I am almost out of giant rat tails. Fortunately the sewer beneath our feet is teeming with them! I will pay a very reasonable sum if you bring me some. Oh, they needn't be from the Rahu sewers. Any rat tails will do."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ashk]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
