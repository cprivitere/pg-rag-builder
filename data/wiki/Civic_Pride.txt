== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Civic Pride]] encourages participation in community-benefiting activities.
=== In-Game Description ===
Represents your willingness to tackle the small, mundane tasks in your community, and your understanding of why that's important. This skill is especially valued by humans.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
==== Oil Lamps ====
There are oil lamps in several zones that can be tended for [[Civic Pride]] XP in three ways:
* Refilling with {{Item|Fuel Oil}}. Gains 2 XP.
* Lighting after 7 pm (map-time). Gains 20 XP.
: - Only the first person to light a lamp will gain experience from that lamp.
* Snuffing after 5 am (map-time). Gains 20 XP.
: - Only the first person to extinguish a lamp will gain experience from that lamp.

==== Torches ====
Torches may also be tended for xp:
* Refilling with {{Item|Oak Wood Chips}} or other wood chips
* Lighting after 7 pm (map-time).
: - Only the first person to light a torch will gain experience from that lamp.
* Snuffing after 5 am (map-time).
: - Only the first person to extinguish a torch will gain experience from that lamp.
==== Item Disposal ====
There are a few locations where you may dispose of items in exchange for [[Civic Pride]] XP.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Zones ==
==== [[Serbule Hills]] Civic Pride ====
* 7 Oil Lamps near the Inn
: - Experience for refueling starts at 10xp and decreases as you level
: - Lighting Oil Lamps is worth 20xp
: - Snuffing Oil Lamps is worth 20xp
==== [[Serbule]] Civic Pride ====
* 38 Oil Lamps in and around [[Serbule Keep]]
: - Experience for refueling starts at 20, then reduces to 1xp as you level the skill.
: - Lighting Oil Lamps is worth 20xp
: - Snuffing Oil Lamps is worth 20xp
* [[Velkort]]'s [[Sentient Flame]] will consume items and return xp
==== [[Eltibule]] Civic Pride ====
* There are torches in both [[Eltibule Keep]] (8) and [[Hogan's Keep]] (16)
: - Refueling gives 2xp
: - Lighting Torches gives 10xp
: - Snuffing Torches gives 10xp
==== [[Red Wing Casino]] Civic Pride ====
* Disposing of trash in trash cans in the [[Red Wing Casino]].
: - Experience granted is based on <kbd> (Item Value * Amount Disposed) / 200 = Civic Pride xp</kbd>.
: - To receive experience, you must deposit at least 2 items worth a total of 200 councils or more. The system will round down.
: - Example: 40 {{Item|Potato Seedling}} deposited = <kbd> (5 * 40) / 200 = 1 xp Civic Pride </kbd>.
==== [[Rahu]] Civic Pride ====
* 8 Oil Lamps in the neutral part of town, 21 in and around the hostile part, and 1 by the Ilmari portal

Note: At this time, there is no Civic Pride XP awarded for filling the Rahu zone lamps or turning on or off.

===Equipment===
* {{Item|Fuel Oil}} can be purchased from [[Fainor]] in the Serbule Inn, and most other cooking vendors.
* {{Item|Oak Wood Chips}} and other chips are crafted through [[Carpentry]].

== Trivia ==
* [[Civic Pride]] was added to Project Gorgon as part of the August 15, 2015 patch.
* One In-Game hour passes every five minutes. Midnight/noon in-game is the top of each even/odd hour, respectively, server time (Eastern Standard/Daylight).
* [[Civic Pride]] XP has been a reward during the [[Suspicious Lemons]] events.
* The [[Civil Service]] event awards large amounts of [[Civic Pride]] XP as bonuses for completing event quests.
