==<b>May 24 Hotfix</b>==
This is a hotfix, not a "real" client update. '''So some bugs (such as camera issues and selection problems) cannot be fixed today; they will be fixed in a few days with a real update.'''
:Bugs fixed in this hotfix:
::- "Winter Fae Blood" is supposed to allow you to carry 6 at a time, but was only letting you carry 1 at a time
::- The new boss [[Claudia]] did not dispel her curse on death
::- Turned off the sound effect for major heals (it will be back after the client update, but was very annoying in the mean time)
::- Vendors charged double for stock items
::- Treasure effects that boosted healing were not working
::- The guild system was not correctly tracking who was online; as a side-effect there was a scenario where not all guild members would receive guild credits from completing a guild quest
:The following bugs were fixed overnight via a server-side patch:
::- Sometimes completing a player work-order doesn't send the item to the requester
::- Quests can't be completed
::- The specific guild quest "Afraid of the Circus" had to have all its objectives completed in order
