==Summary==
Therese has asked for some dinosaur scales. She thinks there are dinosaurs living deep in the crypt beneath Serbule.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Therese]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I've been working on a new fertilizer using some dinosaur scales I had delivered from Statehelm. It's not working yet, but I think I'm getting closer! But I need more scales.

Say, I heard someone say there were dinosaurs in the crypt! You know, the crypt at the back of the graveyard? I only ever saw horrible spiders, but I didn't go in very far. Could you look?}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain 5x {{Item|Small Dinosaur Scale}}
* Talk to Therese
