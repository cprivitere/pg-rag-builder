== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Rakshasa Phrenology]] is a Sub-Skill of [[Phrenology]] based on the study of the skull of [[Rakshasa]].

=== In-Game Description ===
An understanding of how rakshasa think and feel, based on careful study of the shape of their skulls. (Phrenology skills provide critical-hit chances to all Psychology and Mentalism attacks against the corresponding type of creature. Phrenology also has more esoteric uses.)

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Glajur]] in [[Kur Tower]] can train the introductory recipe for this skill.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Skull Extraction ===
Skulls can be extracted from a corpse using a [[Skull Extractor]] or similar tool. Various types of [[Undead]] can also drop skulls.

* {{Item|Ring of Mind Insight}}
* {{Item|Multipurpose Knife}}
* {{Item|Yogzi's Butcher Knife}}
=== Skull Study ===
Once extracted, skulls can be studied using the corresponding [[Phrenology]] recipe and Craniometer.
* {{Item|Craniometer}}
* {{Item|Precision Craniometer}}
* {{Item|Surgical Craniometer}}
