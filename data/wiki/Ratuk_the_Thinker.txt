'''Ratuk the Thinker''' is an Orc mage who isolated himself to conduct research.  Ratuk teaches advanced teleportation techniques, which are quite valuable to players.

Ratuk spends his time researching [[Teleportation]] techniques, but his isolation has left him unable to maintain proper supplies. Ratuk claims he is capable of killing an opponent using teleportation.  Recently, Ratuk has reached out to [[Landri the Cold]] for help to create a wormhole into another plane of existence.
