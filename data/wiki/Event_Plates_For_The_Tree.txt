==Summary==
[[Spot]] in [[Animal Town]] said, "We need round things for the tree! Round... round... like plates. Plates! Get some plates. This many. <i>[Spot holds up a varying number of fingers, changing her mind every few seconds. You decide that a half-dozen plates will do.]</i>"

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Spot]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. This quest is only available to long time animals.
