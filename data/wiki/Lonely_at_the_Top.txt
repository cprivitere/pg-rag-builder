==Summary==
Ferris Blueheart in Vidaria said, "We've studied the minions and the Behemoths pretty exhaustively, but there's still a lot we don't know about the highest ranks: the Overseers and the Justiciars. I would love to observe their inner workings and see how they stack up to the others. You know what to do.""

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ferris Blueheart]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. The quest [[Lesser Of Two Evils]] must be completed first.
