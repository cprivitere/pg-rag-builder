==Summary==
Barnaby in the Sacred Warden Grotto said, "We're having some trouble with the "drawing with ink" lessons: Rubi refuses to get her paws inked up. I think I need something less messy to help them build paw-mouth dexterity. Help me get the raw materials for a little sewing project."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Barnaby]]''' in the '''[[Sacred Grotto]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. You must have previously completed [[Fox Drawing Lessons]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
We're having some trouble with the "drawing with ink" lessons: Rubi refuses to get her paws inked up. I think I need something less messy to help them build paw-mouth dexterity. Help me get the raw materials for a little sewing project.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Simple Blankets x2
* Obtain Coarse Cotton Yarn x2
* Obtain Feathers x4
* Talk to Barnaby
