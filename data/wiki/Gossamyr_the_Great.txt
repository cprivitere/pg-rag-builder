[[Gossamyr the Great]] is a respected fairy fortune teller currently living in [[Statehelm]].

== Favor ==
[[Gossamyr the Great]] will not share his preferences with those of '''{{favor|Neutral}}''' favor. Paying Gossamyr for a fortune increases favor by 25 points.
