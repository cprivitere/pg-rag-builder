==October 27th #2, 2014==

* Fixed more Halloween invasion issues
* Fixed a bug that caused golems to stick around after their owner disconnects


Also some unrelated bug fixes that happen to be in this update due to reasons:

* You no longer learn [[Words of Power|Word of Power]] #2 automatically from lore; you learn Word of Power #1 automatically. The level 2 version is now discoverable on random loot scrolls.

* Fixed issue with collecting ambient magic sponges in the sewers [[Carpal Tunnels|under the giant hand]]

==October 27th, 2014==

* Fixed some issues that caused the Halloween invasion boss to get out of sync with his minions.
* Reduced the number of minions in the invasion down a bit, but buffed up the boss a good bit.
* Changed the respawn point in [[Serbule]] to be away from the entrances, so that invading monsters won't camp respawners.
* Fixed bug that caused all new shields to be displayed as "Legacy Items".

Remember that (as the little info window says) ghosts are nigh-invulnerable until stunned! Once stunned, they go from yellow to white and become easy to kill. Unarmed skill gets a stun at level 3, and Shield skill gets a stun at level 1.
