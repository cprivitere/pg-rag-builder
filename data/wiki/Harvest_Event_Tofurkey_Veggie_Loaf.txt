==Summary==
Ricatu in Povus said, "Kelim was telling me about a turkey meatloaf he ate recently, and it sounded really good. Think you can do the same thing with tofurkey? You'll have to find the recipe yourself."
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ricatu]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''.  The quest is available during the [[Fall Harvest]]. The quest [[Harvest Event: Tofurkey!]] must be completed.
