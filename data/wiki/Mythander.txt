== Services == 
==== Stable Master====
[[Mythander]] offers access to the stable menu starting at '''{{favor|Neutral}}''' favor.

He also offers adventurers the opportunity to learn [[Riding]] and obtain a horse. 

Riding skill can be obtained by doing [[Learning the Ways of Horses]], or paying Mythander 3000 Councils, or by completing Mythander's [[Mystery Rocks]] quest. The horse always costs an additional 3000 Councils. Basic riding tack and gear can be obtained from player [[Saddlery]] crafters or from [[Pasha]] in [[Ilmari]].

==== Horse Glamorizer ====
Mythander has a golem who offers a painless process of turning a special horse into a glamor for other horses. This destroys the original horse and is only available for now-deprecated special horses acquired from activities that now directly give the glamor. Ordinary Wild Horses cannot be made into a glamor.
