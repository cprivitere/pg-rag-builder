==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] sent me into a vampire safehouse beneath [[Statehelm]] to retrieve several items he needs.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[Realigning the Planar Planner]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|I've used Khyrulek's journal to map a way into one of the vampire safehouses beneath Statehelm. There's a working entrance in... Vidaria! It's in the old Woodburr Farm common building. Look for a... windmill... nearby. Once you find the Tuvillic Rune, you should be able to... defeat the guardian... and enter the safehouse.

Once inside, search for these items -- I will write them down for you. You may keep anything else you find.}}

===Requirements===
* Touch Vampire Rune in Woodburr Farmhouse, [[Vidaria]]
* Obtain {{Item|Refined Hematic Platelets}}
* Obtain {{Item|Elongated Darkness}}
* Obtain {{Item|Shadowslip's Guts}}
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]

===Rewards===

As a reward, I shall teach you one of the... secrets... of the vampire. Be grateful, for it is quite... powerful.}}

===Notes===
The final room contains an NPC and a lever which will cure your vampirism. This resets your favor and quest progress with Truxartis back to a state prior to completing any favors with him. You will be able to go and repeat the quests performed to initially contract Vampirism from him once again if desired. 

Please note: Curing Vampirism will reset your Vampirism level and undo any quests you have done. This means that you will need to re-purchase level cap unlocks if they have already been purchased, and quests to unlock skills will need to be completed all over again.
