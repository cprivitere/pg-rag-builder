==Summary==
A concerned scapegoat has shown up at the bridge North of Serbule Keep, in search of something important to her. Search for her lost family, Miss Guided, Miss Take, Miss Fire, Miss Behaved, and Miss Fits, then return to her once you've found them all.

This Quest can be repeated after 15 days.

You must have previously completed [[Event: Lost Scapegoats]] in order to undertake this quest. This quest is only available during a Lost Scapegoats event. 

===Preface===
Hello! I've been wandering around for a while now, and decided to rest here. You see, I can't seem to find my family anywhere. I've searched everywhere, but no matter how hard I look I can't find them. Will you help me?

===Requirements===
* Find Miss Guided
* Find Miss Take
* Find Miss Fire
* Find Miss Behaved
* Find Miss Fits
* Speak with Miss Chievous
