==Summary==
Deville in Animal Town asked for help gathering the ingredients for a potion that should transform him back into a human.

He said the most difficult ingredient would be the heart of [[Vurrkan]], a deadly drakeworm living in Sun Vale.

This Quest can be repeated after 60 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Brandon Mellus]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]''' and then talk to '''[[Deville]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Troll Flesh}}
* Obtain {{Item|Fairy Wing}}
* Obtain {{Item|Rubbery Tongue}} x3
* Obtain {{Item|Vurrkan's Heart}}
* Talk to Deville
