==Summary==
Echur in Serbule has asked you to obtain a dose of Cranium Powder. He believes you can buy it from Azalak the mantis if you're friendly enough with him.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Echur]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|So, um, our buddy Azalak the mantis owes me some cranium powder. I just haven't gotten around to picking it up. Can you get that for me? Oh, but, uh, don't tell him you're getting it for me. Don't mention me at all! Just buy it from him. I'll pay you back! Eventually.

You might have to get on his good side before he's willing to sell it to you. Just be nice to him, he's a great guy.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain some {{Item|Cranium Powder}}
* Talk to Echur
