[[Ferris Blueheart]] is an Assistant to the Council Mages found in the western council camp of Vidaria.  If you contract [[Hellfire]], he can provide you with a {{Item|Hellfreeze}} once every six days for free, in addition to selling additional cures and providing training to create them.

Completing Ferris Blueheart's quest chain unlocks his '''[[Daily Quests]]'''
== Location ==
; [[Vidaria]]

== Shopkeeper ==
[[Ferris Blueheart]] sells a selection of Hellfire and Lesser Hellfire cures for Vidaria Renown.
