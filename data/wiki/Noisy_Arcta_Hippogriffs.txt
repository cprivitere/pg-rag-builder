==Summary==
Ensign Cricket aboard the Wintertide said, "The loudest things aboard are definitely the hippogriffs. At first it was neat hearing them shriek all night, but it's gotten a little repetitive. I think any official invasion party would really need to kill those! Thanks!"

This Quest can be repeated after 30 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ensign Cricket]]''' in '''[[The Wintertide]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor. 

===Preface===
The loudest things aboard are definitely the hippogriffs. At first it was neat hearing them shriek all night, but it's gotten a little repetitive. I think any official invasion party would really need to kill those! Thanks!

===Requirements===
* Kill Arcta Hippogriffs x10
* Talk to Ensign Cricket
