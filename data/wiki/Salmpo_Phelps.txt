[[Salmpo Phelps]] is a human dockworker who lives in the city of [[Statehelm]]. 
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Salmpo Phelps]] may be found wandering the docks of Statehelm during the day. At night, they can be sleeping in a room on the second floor of one of the larger buildings at the docks.
