==Summary==
You're slaying goblins that hinder the road to Sedgewick Forest.

This Quest can be repeated after 0 hours.



===Preface===
<b>Hunting: Goblins</b>
While the goblins sent to patrol the roads here are not nearly as dangerous as the ones in Sedgewick Forest, they are still very deadly. If trade is ever to return to normal, their blockade must be destroyed.

===Requirements===
* Kill Goblins in Serbule Hills x10
