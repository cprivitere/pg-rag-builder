==Summary==
Makara in the Ilmari Desert asked you to find an odd crystal. She said it could be found in yeti caves deep beneath the Kur Mountains. The crystal is a very rare ground spawn that will likely take a long time to find.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Makara]]''' in '''[[Ilmari Desert]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Prisms are useful for a few special crafting tasks. I can make ordinary prisms, but I've heard of a strange prism made by an ancient yeti master. I wish to study it! Find it for me?

It would be in some cave in the Kur Mountains, I presume. I doubt it would be easy to find, since I've only heard vague rumors of it. It's probably in some sack deep down in a hole somewhere.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Archaic Prism}}
* Talk to Makara
