[[Event: Garden Invasion]] is a [[Seasonal Event]] that usually occurs in early Spring, and sometimes a second time in mid-Fall. Alongside [[Event: Bun-Fu Training]], the Garden Invasion is a source for {{Item|Bunny Juice (Brown Fur)}}, allowing players to transform into the [[Rabbit]] beast form.
{{Quote|source=[[Sie Antry]]|
This is insane! I can't kill 'em fast enough! Rabbits are everywhere! And Jesina is no help at all. "Oh they're so cute!" No. They are NOT cute! They're eating my crops!

You don't look like you're doin' anything important. Help me kill these vermin!
; I guess I can, if it's necessary...
Great. Get to it! There's gotta be hundreds of 'em hiding in the bushes around here. Just try planting some potatoes or onions or... anything, really, and they'll run right to your crops!

Oh, and one other thing: try not to let Jesina see you do the deed. She gets real upset.

}}
==Summary==
[[Sie Antry]] in [[Eltibule]] asked for your help in getting rid of hungry rabbits. Just grow some plants in any garden area, and they'll come running!

==Prerequisites==
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Sie Antry]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available during the Garden Invasion Weekend Event. This quest is repeatable every 6 hours during the event.
