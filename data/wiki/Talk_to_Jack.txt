==Summary==
Charles Thompson, a vendor in a tent outside of Serbule, asked you to talk to Mushroom Jack about the giant mushrooms near Serbule.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Charles Thompson]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Before I was rudely kicked out of Serbule, I was trying to get in touch with a fanciful fellow that I'd heard lives there. He's an Elf, so who knows if he's trustworthy, but his name is "Mushroom Jack." Delightful, isn't it? Anyway, I heard he knows about those gigantic mushrooms on the hill nearby. Could you ask him what medicinal properties they have? I'm mostly worried that they're poisonous.}}

===Requirements===
* Talk to '''[[Mushroom Jack]]'''
* Talk to '''[[Charles Thompson]]'''

{{Spoiler|Response to player|{{Quote|Who? Charles Thompson? Never heard of him. But yes, I know what those giant mushrooms are for: they're psychic antennae for the Myconians! They use those mushrooms as beacons to control the thoughts of weak-willed people.

No, I don't have proof. Fuck your proof, just open your ears! You stay around those mushrooms long enough and you'll hear the voices, too!

Oh, and tell him not to eat the giant mushrooms. They're not only poisonous, they're also as hard as stone. Not a fun way to spend a weekend.}} }}
