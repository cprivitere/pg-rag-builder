== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Druid]] is a combat skill earned by individuals seeking a deeper connection with nature, and willing to put their life on the line to defend it. In order to obtain the skill, one must venture to the [[Druid Camp]] at the Northern tip of [[Sun Vale]]. An altar just outside a druid encampment will accept your pledge. You must have at least one combat skill at level 50 to use the altar. [[Silvia]], the druid trainer, can be found near the shrine if a druid wishes to learn new abilities.

* [[Parella]] in [[Statehelm]] offers the unlock of Druid Skill Levels 81-90, 91-100, and [[Prodigy Potential]].
* [[Rixie]] in [[The Yellow Room]] will teach a travel form after completing the second level of her mini-game.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Druidic Emergencies ===
{{Quote|source=[[Game_updates/2016-07-23]]|
:Druids can finally fulfill their druidic obligations! "Druid events" are calls to action for the Druids. One hour prior to the event, druids will receive a premonition of pending danger (in the form of a special effect icon). When the event occurs, druids are obligated to go and help with whatever disaster is occurring. You will receive a special quest explaining the details and tracking your progress.

: Once the event starts, druids can't earn XP. This is to encourage druids to do their goddamned jobs. But druids can still get loot, and when the event ends, Dreva will send 'blessings' to druids that helped. Dreva's Blessings can be used to purchase abilities and items from the druid pedestal in Sun Vale.

: Non-druids can help fight back the plagues of evil, too. Anyone who defeats the necessary monsters will advance the special event, whether they're druids or not.

: There are several different 'sizes' of each event. When the premonition begins, the number of active players online is checked, and an appropriate difficulty is chosen based on that number. (There are three versions of each event. We'll add more versions down the road.) 
}}
==== Intermittency ====
Each day the system decides between 0-3 druid events.

==== Druidic Storage ====
[[Druid]]s with at least 4 levels in [[Teleportation]] are able to purchase two useful recipes by spending Dreva's Blessings at the Druid altar. These recipes give access to a special storage many adventurers find useful for stowing their [[Druid]] equipment or [[Haste|Sprint boosting potions]]. 
* '''Summon Druidic Storage (12-slot)''' - Summon a special druidic storage container. Must be near a teleportation platform. This can be used during a druidic emergency and up to an hour before or after the emergency.
* '''Summon Druidic Storage (20-slot)''' - Summon a special druidic storage container. Must be near a teleportation platform. This can be used during a druidic emergency and up to an hour before or after the emergency.

== Trivia ==
[[Category:Skills]][[Category:Combat Skills]]
Certain Druid animal forms (raven, duck, owl) do not count as animal for purposes of Animal Town NPCs. You will get a message "you still smell like a human/elf/rakshasa," but even after 3 hours, they will not talk with you.

This is no longer the case, but when [[Riding]] was first added, level 50 Riding counted for the level 50 combat skill prerequisite to unlock Druid.
