==Summary==
The Suspicious Cow in Eltibule said, "Finally! I did the summoning ritual hours ago! Why do you Wardens even bother with a summoning ritual if you're just going to ignore me for HOURS while terrible things happen?!

It's the gnashers! They usually keep their distance and stay northwest of here, but now they've been spreading towards us. They killed a bull this morning! It was a bit north of here. And... they ate him! They ATE him! And nobody in that castle over there cares one bit! Please hurry. I know I'm delicious. It's only a matter of time!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 day.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Suspicious Cow]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor. This quest is only available for Wardens. This quest is only available during an event in the area.
