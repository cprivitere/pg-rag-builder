==Summary==
Kill the Scion of Norala, a huge wolf deep within the wolf cave in Kur Mountains.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Wolf Altar]]''' in '''[[Wolf Cave]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

To begin this quest you must have level 1 [[Lycanthropy]].

This quest is only available around the Full Moon (+/- 1 day).

===Preface===
{{Quote|Norala believes in the rule of might. If you can kill the Scion of Norala, then you should do so. If you die, then you were too weak to live.}}

===Requirements===
* Kill Scion of Norala
* Talk to Wolf Altar
