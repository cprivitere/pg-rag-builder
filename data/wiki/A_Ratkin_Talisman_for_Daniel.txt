==Summary==
Daniel Murderdark in Rahu said, "Hey, thanks for teaching me that little ratkin-skeleton trick! It's not as amazing as the trick I taught you... but it's still pretty good! But, um... in my excitement I forgot something. I don't have one of those summoning talismans! Can you give me yours? I'll give you a lifetime of riches for it!"

To start this quest, talk to '''[[Daniel Murderdark]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. This quest is only available after completing a hangout.
