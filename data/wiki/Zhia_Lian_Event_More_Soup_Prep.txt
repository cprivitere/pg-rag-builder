==Summary==
Brandon Mellus in the Red Wing Casino said, "I think we need a backup soup in case the eyeball soup doesn't go over well. Can you get these ingredients?"

This Quest can be repeated after 60 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Brandon Mellus]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. You must have previously completed [[Halloween Event: Soup Prep]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
I think we need a backup soup in case the eyeball soup doesn't go over well. Can you get these ingredients?

===Requirements===
* Obtain Maggoty Ham x3
* Obtain Carrots x3
* Obtain Muntok Peppercorns x3
* Talk to Brandon Mellus
