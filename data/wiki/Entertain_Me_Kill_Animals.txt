==Summary==
Pennoc in Serbule told you to kill some animals. Not monsters: just animals.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pennoc]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Go and kill some animals! Pigs, deer, sheep, whatever. But don't do it in town, you'll upset everybody. And don't kill monsters -- just animals! I don't want you getting hurt on my behalf.}}

===Requirements===
* Kill Animals
* Talk to Pennoc
