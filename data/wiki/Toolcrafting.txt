== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Toolcrafting]] lets the player craft all sorts of useful gear, from armor to better autopsy/skinning/butchering tools to single-skill [[Belt|Lucky Belts]].

=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in the creation of advanced tools and unusual equipment. Far more than just creating hand tools, an expert Toolcrafter is a master of repurposing found materials, and can create belts, armors, and even weapons from unusual materials. This equipment is usually designed to meet very specialized needs.

Some tools need to be crafted at a forge. For a list of forge locations, see [[Blacksmithing]].

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* [[Yogzi]] in [[Sun Vale]] teaches Toolcrafting if your [[Blacksmithing]] or [[Carpentry]] is level 20. (Yogzi's dialog in-game indicates both Carpentry and Blacksmithing must be 20, but having only one of the two is sufficient). Yogzi also trains most of the main recipes below 50.
* [[Sie Antry]] in [[Eltibule]] will teach you how to make all kinds of gear from the body parts of vanquished Snails starting at [[Toolcrafting]] level 30.
* [[Amutasa]] in [[Rahu]] will unlock levels 51-60, 61-70, and teaches level 51+ recipes, as well as the mid-level hammer recipes (Shaping Hammer, Tack Hammer, and Delicate Tack Hammer).
* [[Yagreet]] at the [[Great Tree]] in [[Sun Vale]] will teach Amazing First Aid Kit, and [[Mox Warcut]] Astounding First Aid Kit.
* [[Corey The Croaker]] in the [[Winter Nexus]] will teach how to craft Spring Fairy equipment.
* [[Felmer]] in the [[Fae Realm]] will unlock levels 71-80 and teach 80+ recipes.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] in the [[Nightmare Caves]] deep beneath [[Povus]].

(For forge locations, see the [[Blacksmithing]] page.) [[Sun Vale]] does have a tanning rack, however, located in [[Animal Town]].
