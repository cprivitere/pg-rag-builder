== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
Graffiti is criminal activity in Statehelm, and will result in demerits if a citizen should catch you at it.

As you level, the length of the messages you can leave increases. At level 17, the length is 32.

There is a set of quests that trigger automatically as you use the skill, each one rewarding some Lore XP and a new symbol for use in {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}}:
