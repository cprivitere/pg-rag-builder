==Summary==
Truxartis asked me to find his butler [[Grasuul]] at a cursed lake in [[Vidaria]] and convince him to return. Completing this quest unlocks the daily Blood Oath quests.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[The Stolen Lens]] and receiving the Vampirism skill from Truxartis.

===Preface===
{{Quote|First and foremost, you should find my... butler. He was once a ghoul. And I suppose he is still a ghoul, of a sort. But he is ... more. He can sense hatred in the air. It is MOST valuable.

Grasuul ran away when I was forced into repose. But I know where he would go: I spied a cursed lake near the ruby wall. He would no doubt be there, reveling in anguish. Grasuul is... not well.

Tell Grasuul that I have awoken. He will come back.}}

===Requirements===
* Convince Grasuul to Return (speak to Grasuul at the cursed lake near the ruby wall in Vidaria)
* Talk to Truxartis

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Grasuul the Butler|
{{Quote|Grasuul has returned! Now we can find the best sources of blood. Excellent.}}

* 50 {{Favor|favor}}
* 10 Blood Oaths
* Unlocks the Grasuul NPC
* Unlocks all four daily Blood Oath quests
}}

===Notes===
{{Spoiler|Notes|
After completing this quest, [[Grasuul]] can tell you the current blood target (the target type for the current real-world day, resetting at midnight EST) and the current in-game weather.
}}
