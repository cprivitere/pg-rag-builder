==Summary==
[[Lisi]] in [[Sun Vale]] said, "There are many seashells on the beaches here. I like the pointy ones, and the round shiny ones ... But I really like the shell fragments. I can use them to make little mosaics. It gives me something to do while my wing heals."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lisi]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. This quest is only available to [[Fairy|Fae]].
