== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Fire Magic]] is a common magical combat skill learned by many across [[Alharth]].

=== In-Game Description ===
Using Fire to destroy your enemies. This is a very potent offensive skill, including long-range and area-effect attacks, and even some Cold attacks for versatility. However, there are drawbacks: Fire Magic is extremely Power-intensive; all creatures generate extra Rage when they are burned; and many creatures have some resistance to fire.

=== Research Mechanics '''VERY IMPORTANT''' ===
Fire Magic, [[Ice Magic]], and [[Weather Witching]] are research-based skills. This means rather than directly learning spells, you learn a research research recipe for a certain level of spells, and when completing that research you get a random spell of that level. '''Outside of the first level of a spell, you can only research a level that you have already researched the previous level of. This means if you research all spells you want at a certain level, you can stop researching at that level, and the unwanted spells will not be acquired in future tiers of research. This can save a significant amount of materials in later tiers while you acquire your desired spells, as research becomes very costly later on.'''

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Velkort]], an elf living in [[Serbule]], is always willing to teach [[Fire Magic]] to interested travelers.
* [[The Sand Seer]], a Rakshasa in [[Ilmari]], unlocks levels 51-70 and teaches equivalent level recipes.
* [[Landri the Cold]] is willing to teach Defensive Chill to [[Fire Magic|Fire Mages]].
* [[Bendith the Banished]] unlocks levels 71-80 and teaches the equivalent recipes.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Reinath]] deep beneath [[Povus]] or can be bought by [[Guzor Harrison]] in [[Statehelm]] after completing his quests.
* Levels 91 - 100 are unlocked by [[Guzor Harrison]] in [[Statehelm]] and can be either bought with money or paid for with [[Statehelm Renown]]. Must be enrolled as a student at the [[Statehelm School of Magic]] for training options!

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
* [[Fire Magic]] is completely useless underwater!
=== Research Ingredients ===
[[Fire Magic]] is a research based magic, and abilities are unlocked through combining specific ingredients using recipes. The skill can be expensive due to the required ingredients used in spell research: {{Item|Fire Dust}}, {{Item|Sulfur}}, {{Item|Saltpeter}},  {{Item|Explosive Runestone}}. {{Item|Flame Nib}}, {{Item|Guano}}, {{Item|Phoenix Beak}}, {{Item|Phoenix Feather}}, {{Item|Phoenix Egg}}. Please see the pages for those ingredients for more information on how to gather them. Researching is ''not'' a lottery, each recipe can only be completed a number of times so the amount of ingredients required is fixed. The research mechanic simply adds a little randomness to the order in which you receive the abilities.
* The abilities [[Spontaneous Fireball]] and [[Spontaneous Warmthball]] are not classified as [[Fire Magic]] abilities, but can be learned when completing the appropriate level research recipes.

=== Fire Wall ===
An interesting mechanic is that [[Fire Magic]] has a “Fire Wall” pet that just stands around and burns things. It’s implemented as a pet internally, but since it’s a wall, it can’t follow you around. It’s more like a trap. It can also be killed by enemies.

Fire wall has several variants that can be learned at level 65 or higher. These include [[Wall of Healing Flame]], [[Summon Magmasphere]], [[Wall of Coldfire]], and [[Wall of Smarmy Flame]]. You can learn the recipe to research these spells from the training orb at [[Reinath]] in the [[Nightmare Caves]], or from [[Guzor Harrison]] in [[Statehelm]] after completing his quest to join the academy.
