[[Tremmond Blackhammer]] is an Armorsmith in the city of [[Povus]].

== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Tremmond Blackhammer]] can be found in his workshop near the forge in the Old Town section of Povus.  He's only available as a trainer and vendor during the in-game day.
