==Summary==
Truxartis asked me to kill the manticore Lythorax and recover the Missing Hematic Lens.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[The Man In The Coffin]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|I need to re-open this Blood Well ... right away. But a very rude... manticore...stole the lens while I was... resting. He is called Lythorax. You will need to kill him. He's sentient... but not ''very'' sentient.

You will need...assistance... most likely.}}

===Requirements===
* Kill Lythorax and obtain the {{Item|Missing Hematic Lens}}
* Return the lens to Truxartis

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for The Stolen Lens|
{{Quote|This is it! Now... Yes! The blood well is reopened!}}

* 50 {{Favor|favor}}
* 10,000 Councils
* Truxartis can now grant Vampirism
}}
