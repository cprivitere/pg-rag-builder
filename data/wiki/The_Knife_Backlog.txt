==Summary==
Amutasa needed someone to help her fill an order for six Good Skinning Knives.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Amutasa]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

To begin this quest you must have level 15 [[Toolcrafting]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|I am overwhelmed with work requests! This is a good thing, but I am just not able to keep up! I must learn to say 'no', I suppose. But it is too late for that now. Are you capable of making tools? I could use your help in filling a simpler order: I need six good skinning knives.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain Good Skinning Knives (6)
* Talk to Amutasa
