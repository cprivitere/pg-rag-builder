==Summary==
Fluffikins in Gazluk said, "I'm supposed to be in charge of the campsite, which is why the jealous humans <i>constantly</i> slack off to make me look bad. I keep telling them to repair these stalls so they're ready for the heavier snows to come. So they head off to gather wood, and then come back empty-handed. "Chased by a bear" or "bit by a slime"... always some excuse so they don't have to work. Can you help me? We need spruce."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Fluffikins]]''' in '''[[Gazluk Plateau]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
