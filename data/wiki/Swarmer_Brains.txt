==Summary==
Ser Lorentz in Vidaria said, "I knew I forgot something! When I asked you to kill those swarmers, you were supposed to scoop up their brains and bring them back. The mages have a 'theory'. I know this wasn't your fault, so I'm happy to overlook this incident if you can head back out into the field and get those brains."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ser Lorentz]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. The quest [[Testing Your Demon-Killing Mettle]] must be completed first.
