[[Sir Johnson]] is a Council military leader, a skilled [[Shield]] expert, and a treasure-seeker currently stationed in [[Amulna]] to watch the movements of the Rakshasa of [[Rahu]]. [[Sir Johnson]] discovered [[Weaponized Xedrite]] in the sands under [[Ilmari]], and has been recruiting adventurers to help recover it from [[War Caches]].

== Conversations ==
{{Quote|source=Sir Johnson|
Hmm. You'll do. Ready to start? 
; Excuse me, start what?
Oh, you're not here about the job opportunity? Sorry. You look the type. And we could use some more help, so if you're interested in fabulous treasure...
; Tell me about this job opportunity.
I'm hiring people to explore some tombs for me. I'll tell you right up front, it's incredibly dangerous work. I've had rakkie bandits come into town and take the job, thinking it'd be easy, but they usually go back to banditry... or die. 
; That's a hell of a sales pitch. 
I'm just trying not to send people to their deaths, that's all!

Are you still interested?
; Yes. What's the job?
There's a bunch of bunkers in this desert, buried beneath the sand. We call 'em "War Caches." They were made by the Crone Hegemony forces during the Rahu wars, and lots of 'em are still full of military equipment. 

The caches are usually hidden, but sometimes the sandstorms expose one for a bit. I've hired a bunch of bandits to search for 'em. When they find a location, I buy the intel from them. Then my Seer gets to work.
; Your Seer?
Yeah. Sorry, I'm not supposed to use her real name. She's just "the Sand Seer", all mysterious. Anyway, when we get a location, she does some scrying for me, looking for a special kind of metal called xedrite. Not normal garbage xedrite, though: I'm looking for Weaponized Xedrite. Metal with incredible magical properties. 

So that's the job: when we find a location with Weaponized Xedrite, we send you in to get it. 
; What's the pay? 
Well, that's a bit complicated. See, I'm collecting this metal on behalf of the Council, but I didn't really receive much of a budget. It's a long story. 

So I can't pay much in Councils, but I've got some top-notch magical equipment! For each successful mission, I'll give you credit in the commissary, and you can spend it on whatever you want. 
; The Commissary?
Yeah. My assistant Drima runs a little shop. There's weapons, armor, magic jewelry, and other things you might find useful.
