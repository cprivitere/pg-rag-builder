==Summary==
Velkort in Serbule mentioned some talking mantises in a field, and asked you to kill some and get their claws.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Velkort]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Those strange talking mantises out in the fields have weird runes on their claws. Someone drew those on them! Can you gather some specimens for me? I want to know more about them.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Augmented Mantis Claw}}s x5
* Talk to Velkort
