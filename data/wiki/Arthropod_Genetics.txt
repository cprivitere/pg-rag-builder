== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Unlocking Arthropod Genetics ===
Unlocking [[Arthropod Genetics]] requires completion of a series of tasks centered around the [[Povus]] region. 
{{Spoiler| Step 1 - Obtain the Ghostly Letter|
The first step of the process is to obtain a {{Item|Ghostly Letter}} by defeating [[Tumak the Betrayer]] in Povus. He must be defeated between the in-game times of 9am and 10am.}}
{{Spoiler| Step 2 - Obtain the Ghostly Key|
With the Ghostly Letter in-hand, visit the City of Povus nightly until [[Kimeta]] spawns. [[Kimeta]]'s ghost will only appear under specific conditions.
* It must be nighttime in Povus. She will not spawn before 8:00pm.
* Ghosts must be spawning in the Sprawl district of the city.
* The light outside her house must be off. If the light is turned on she will despawn.
* If the light is not turned on, she will despawn at 1:15am.
The {{Item|Ghostly Letter}} must be given to [[Kimeta]]. In return, she will bestow the {{Item|Ghostly Key}}.}}
{{Spoiler| Step 3 - Questing for Gerrux|
Gift the {{Item|Ghostly Key}} to [[Gerrux]] as if giving an item for favor. Start the quest [[Kimeta's Key (Quest)]].
* Find [[Errruka the Benefactor]] and ask about a lost key.
* Wait one in-game day, then return to [[Errruka the Benefactor|Errruka]].
* Obtain {{Item|Kimeta's Key}} from [[Errruka the Benefactor|Errruka]] by trading for either 25 {{Item|Frog}} or 1 {{Item|Black Pearl}}.
* Return to [[Gerrux]] with {{Item|Kimeta's Key}} to complete the quest. 
* Read the rewarded book {{Item|Secrets of Arthropod Genetics}}.
}}
