==Summary==
Mu the Myconian asked you to collect the heartshrooms of any Myconians you're forced to kill.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mu]]''' in '''[[Myconian Caverns]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|If you travel in these caves you will meet violent myconians. Either you or they shall die. If it is they who die, please bring back any intact heartshrooms you find. We can regrow myconians from them.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Heartshroom}}s x5
* Talk to Mu
