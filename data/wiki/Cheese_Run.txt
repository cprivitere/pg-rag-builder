Nelson Ballard in Serbule has asked you to obtain some cheese he ordered from a man in Eltibule Keep.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Nelson Ballard]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
