[[Shirogin]] is a [[Tailoring]] expert living in the City of [[Rahu]].

He dreams of being invited to the [[Formal Execution]], an event thrown by the Rakshasa Generals towards the end of a season. He wishes to attend the event, but has not been invited for the past three years due to an unfortunate situation involving [[General Tremal]].
