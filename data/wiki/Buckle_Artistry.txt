== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Buckle Artistry''' is a skill based around the creation of belt buckles. These buckles are not magical in any way. Only the individual who crafts a buckle may equip it. Over time, belt buckles will decay and break.
=== In-Game Description ===

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Jake the Buckler]], a Human living in [[Sun Vale]], will teach the skill to anyone willing to help him complete a few tasks (specifically the quest [[Lemony Dye]]). Jake can be found near the Druid Camp.
* [[Jake the Buckler]] can also raise the [[Buckle Artistry]] level cap to Level 60.

== Connected Skills ==

=====Related Skills:=====
* [[Toolcrafting]] - Toolcrafters can create belts.
* [[Shamanic Infusion]] - Belts can also be created by those proficient in Shamanic Infusion.

=== Buckle Decay===
{{Quote| source ="Citan the [[Developers|Developer]]" - [[Game_updates/2015-12-14]]|
Crafted belt buckles get damaged in combat and eventually break, requiring you to make a new one. The rate of decay is going to have to be experimented with. Theoretically the buckles will last for an average of 5 hours of combat (that is, being "in combat" for 18,000 seconds) before they break.
