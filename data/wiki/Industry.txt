== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Industry]] is a skill leveled by completing crafting or collection tasks for the Work Order system. Across Alharth, merchants post [[Work Orders]] for explorers and crafters to complete. 
=== In-Game Description ===
Competence in completing tasks to specification. Highly industrious workers can juggle more tasks at once, and earn slightly higher rewards for their hard work.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
Training [[Industry]] primarily occurs when NPC [[Work Orders]] are completed. 
* [[Decoction of Industry Knowledge]] is a consumable, granting +250 Industry XP. {{Hint|Sold by Qatik in Red Wing Casino.}}
* [[Sheyna]] in [[Rahu]] offers the unlocks of levels 51-60 and 61-70.
* [[Tryaka]] in [[Povus]] offers the unlock of levels 71-80. (Note: Povus town's level must be 8+ for Tryaka to arrive).
* [[Korbok]] in [[Vidaria]] offers the unlock of levels 81-90.

Work Orders can be obtained from:
* Interacting with a Work Order board in a town.
* Receiving a Work Order scroll in monster loot or in a dungeon.
* Buying a Work Order from an NPC's Buy Used tab. {{Hint|Players often sell unwanted work orders to NPCs like Hulon and Velkort.}}

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
===Work Order System===
====What is a Work Order====
[[Work Orders]] are scroll items that ask for a certain number of items to be delivered to a specific NPC. In return for turning in those items the player will receive the price that was indicated on the Work Order.

Once a player has learned (or even completed) a certain Work Order for an item, other identical Work Orders will be shown with a green text to indicate that the player has 'already memorized' that Work Order. After one month the 'memorized' status will be reset for the work order;  The player can then once again learn the Work Order and turn in the required items.

Many players will begin by completing Work Orders in [[Serbule]] before moving to other zones. Work Orders in higher level zones request items that require higher levels of crafting skills to complete, but also offer a higher reward. The completion of high level Work Orders is a common way to earn many Councils. As [[Industry]] is leveled, the maximum number of active Work Orders a character can claim is increased (up to 13).

====How are Player Work Orders different from NPC Work Orders====
While most towns have a sign offering NPC Work Orders, Player Work Order signs can sometimes be found nearby. Player Work Orders do not give experience towards leveling [[Industry]], and must be completed while interacting with the sign. Player Work Orders cannot be bought, sold, dropped, or traded.

At the Player Work Order sign, a character can:
* Create a new Work Order (choose the desired item, specify the number requested and set the price).
* browse the posted Player Work orders.
* Fill a Work Order (if the required items are held in inventory).
=== Obtaining Work Orders ===
==== Work Order Types ====
* [[Serbule]] : Items obtained through Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Cooking, Fishing, Fletching, Foraging, Leatheworking, Surveying, and monster trophies obtained as loot.
* [[Eltibule]] : Items obtained through Blacksmithing, Cheesemaking, Dye Making, Gardening, Fletching, Textile Creation, Leatherworking, Skinning, Surveying, Blacksmithing and monster trophies obtained as loot.
* [[Sun Vale]] : Items obtained through Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Surveying, and Toolcrafting
* [[Kur Mountains]] : Items obtained through Cooking
* [[Ilmari]] : Items obtained through Leatherworking, Tailoring and Toolcrafting (snail gear)
* [[Rahu]] : Items obtained through Blacksmithing (werewolf gear), Carpentry, Cooking, Flower Arrangement, Surveying, Tailoring, Toolcrafting, Leatherworking, Monster Trophies
* [[Gazluk]] : There is no work order board in Gazluk.
* [[Red Wing Casino]] : Items obtained through Alchemy (boost potions), Brewing, Carpentry, Cooking, Dye making, Mining, Tailoring, Monster Trophies. 
* [[Fae Realm]] : Items obtained through Cooking, Sushi Preparation, Ice Conjuration, Cheesemaking, Gardening, Fletching, Carpentry, Jewelry Crafting, Blacksmithing, and monster trophies obtained as loot.
* [[Vidaria]] : Items obtained through Flower Arrangement,???, and various item orders (Ultra Lac, Spruce Wood).
