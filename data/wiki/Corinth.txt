{{Quote|source=[[Corinth]]|...and here bardic magic works similarly to augmentation, for success is very much a matter of restraint. Even in... oh!}}
[[Corinth]] is the Head Augmenter of the [[Augmenter's Guild of Statehelm]], a guild of craftsman in [[Statehelm]] dedicated to mastering [[Augmentation]] and helping the willing use Augments. 

He wrote the widely distributed instructional book [[Augments for Everyone]].
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Corinth]] can be found in an office at the Bard College.

== Conversations ==
{{Quote|source=[[Corinth]]|
Hello. I am Corinth, the Head of Augmentation for the Statehelm School of Magic.

'''Do you teach any courses?'''

Here at the Bard school? Oh no no, I'm not a bard. I'm just visiting from the School of Magic.

Although I'm also not teaching any courses there at the moment either. But I have taken on tutelage roles in the past. 

But you must understand that my time is precious, so I charge quite high fees, and can work only with the truly wise.

If there's nothing else? I'm sorry to be brusque; I simply have so much work to do!

'''I won't keep you. Goodbye.'''}}
{{Quote|source=[[Corinth]]|
We all know that intent can directly invoke a simple attuned enchantment. But what if I told you that you could convert wisdom itself into power? No, no, too enticing. The first sentence of a book should be dull so that it scares away the unworthy.

I'm sorry, I'm working on my new book. Do you need something?}}
{{Quote|source=[[Corinth]]|
To imbue an instrument with magic is to amplify the voice of the soul that wields it. That soul must, of course, be worthy of the power they try to wield.

Sadly, weak souls can only command weak enchantments. A weak soul is not a mark of shame. It's simply a matter of fate.}}
