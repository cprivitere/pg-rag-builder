==Summary==
[[Way]] asked you to be on the lookout for his [[Way's Lucky Yo-Yo|yo-yo]], which he believes is in the posession of a [[sentinel spider]].

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Way]]''' in '''[[Myconian Caverns]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|A few moons back, an elf visited us and gave me an exotic item called a 'yo-yo'. It has a string. It goes up and down via magic. I loved it!

Unfortunately I left it lying about, and one of the sentinel spiders took it to their nest. If you happen upon it, please return it.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Way's Lucky Yo-Yo}}
* Talk to [[Way]]
