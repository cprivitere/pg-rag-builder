== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Pig''' is one of the many [[Beast Forms]], and possibly one of the easiest to obtain. As with the other [[Beast Forms]], the effect is "permanent until cured". If you get tired of rolling in the mud, you can always buy an [[Un-Pig Juice]]. Be warned that Un-Pig Juice is very costly, and can be difficult to find.

For more information on how to navigate being turned into an animal visit the [[Beast Forms]] page for advice.

=== In-Game Description ===
The art of living and fighting as a pig.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* [[Pig Juice]] drops from all manner of [[Non-Ruminant Ungulate|Non-Ruminant Ungulates]].
* [[Norbert]] in [[Sun Vale]]'s [[Animal Town]] will unlock levels 51-70 and teach level 51-70 abilities.
* [[Backfat]] in the [[Fae Realm]] is your source of level 71-80 oinking.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Big Footed Jim]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Hamilton]] in the [[Statehelm Sewers]] unlocks 81-90, 91-100, and [[Prodigy Potential]].

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Truffle Sniff ===
Pigs can use Truffle Sniff to dig up random mushrooms.  You can get Myconic XP from Truffle Sniff mushrooms.
*Truffle Sniff 1 -- {{Item|Parasol Mushroom}}
*Truffle Sniff 2 -- {{Item|Strange Dirt}}, {{Item|Parasol Mushroom}}, {{Item|Mycena Mushroom}}
*Truffle Sniff 3 -- {{Item|Strange Dirt}}, {{Item|Parasol Mushroom}}, {{Item|Mycena Mushroom}}, {{Item|Boletus Mushroom}}

=== Riding ===
As a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]], Pigs are not able to use the traditional Horse mounts offered through [[Riding]]. Instead, [[Norbert]] in [[Animal Town]] teaches mount-form to Pig players who are at least level 25. They also teach an ability that increases both land and water speed temporarily.
===Equipment===
Crafters producing equipment for use by [[Pig]]s should obtain the [[Sigil Scripting]] recipe '''Pig Inscription,''' which allows for the creation of {{Item|Pig Malachite}}. 

This attuned gemstone can be used in any recipe which calls for the use of [[:Category:Items/Crystal|Crystals]] to create equipment with [[Pig]] Treasure Effects.
