== Quests ==
[[Michael Irasce]] offers adventurers with '''{{favor|Neutral}} favor''' a quest ([[The Lost Travels of Romast]]) asking for the retrieval of a rare book from "behind a gilded chest" in a cave in [[Povus]].
{{Spoiler|Book Location|The book is located in the alcove behind the gilded chest next to [[Slimey]] the snail in the northern [[Forthragarian Caves]].}} 

Completing this first quest unlocks an inventory management tool and library-science technique. (An inventory-window button which allows filtering of viewed items by Equipment, Consumable, Quest, or Other.)

[[Michael Irasce]] will also begin to offer five more quests to retrieve various books.
