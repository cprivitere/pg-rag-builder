==Summary==
There's an undecorated tree in Rahu near Zhao's shop. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Zhao]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Get Vagabond Ears for the Tree
* Get Lemons for the Tree
* Get N Cores for the Tree Lights
* Get Spruce for the Tree-Topper
* Talk to [[Zhao]]
