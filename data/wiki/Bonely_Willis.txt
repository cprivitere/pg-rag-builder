[[Bonely Willis]] is a human sailor living at the [[Statehelm]] docks until their ship is ready to depart. 
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Bonely Willis]] may be found wandering the docks of Statehelm during the day. They might also be at the bottom of the bay. At night, they can be found sleeping in a room on the second floor of one of the larger buildings at the docks.
