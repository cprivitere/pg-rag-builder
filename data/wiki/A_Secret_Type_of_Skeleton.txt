==Summary==
Daniel Murderdark in Rahu said, "Pst... a little bird told me the most exciting rumor: there's some weird kind of skeletons in a cave under Rahu... and they're easily controllable by necromancers! I bet they're the restless undead from that rakkie war! Those rakkies could supposedly shapeshift... imagine a shapeshifting skeleton! Orrrr... they say there were dragons during the war... imagine a dragon skeleton at our command!

Well, I don't care what it is, really... I have to see one! If you find it, you've gotta show me. Promise me!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Daniel Murderdark]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. This quest is available at [[Necromancy]] level 25.

===Solution (contains spoilers!!)===
{{Spoiler|Click Expand to view a hint (minimal spoiler)|
You do not need to fetch an item to solve this quest. It is tied to an ability.}}

{{Spoiler|Click Expand to view a bigger hint (medium spoiler)|
You need a higher necromancy level to solve this quest. You will eventually learn an ability that allows you to show him an unusual skeleton as found deep in a cave under Rahu (the sewers connect to caves).}}

{{Spoiler|Click Expand to view solution (full spoiler)|
Reach Necromancy level 64 and learn [[Raise Skeletal Ratkin Mage 5]] from [[Bogaku]] in [[Rahu Sewer]] Goblin Encampment. Once you have learned the spell and obtained a [[Ratkin Necromancer's Talisman]], you need to summon one of those Skeletal Ratkin Mages and talk to [[Daniel Murderdark]] with the skeleton in tow.}}
