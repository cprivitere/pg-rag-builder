==Summary==
Irkima in Red Wing Casino said, "I have been instructed to sell more 'baubles and trinkets' to appeal to drunken bipeds with lots of cash. They often ask for lucky tokens, so I wish to procure some rabbit feet. If you bring me three, I shall pay 1200 Councils for them, plus some Red Wing Tokens."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Irkima]]''' in '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
