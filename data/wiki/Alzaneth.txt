== Conversations ==
{{Quote|source=[[Alzaneth]]|
Ah, a fellow student of the arcane. I am Alzeneth. I'm visiting from [[Synelon]]. It's a lovely city you have here.

'''I've not heard of Synelon, where is that?'''

Oh! You haven't heard of it. I see. Well, it's a famous school of magic in [[Verta]]. Given that it's the closest school to the [[Council Lands]], I would have imagined the magical crowd in Statehelm would know about us.

I... suppose we haven't made much effort to branch out recently. We have been in mourning over the loss of [[Dwyndarre]].

'''I heard about the destruction of Dwyndarre. You have my condolences. Is Synelon near Dwyndarre?'''

Yes, our main tree was a pleasant hike away from Dwyndarre. We came when we saw the smoke, but of course the damage was done at that point. There were corpses everywhere...

I'm sorry. I know you have important work to attend. I won't keep you!}}
{{Quote|source=[[Alzaneth]]|
Lady Irasce's library has quite the reputation in my homeland. I had to come see it for myself, and I am not disappointed. It's a waste, in some ways, to keep these treasures here of all places.}}
{{Quote|source=[[Alzaneth]]|
This college's copy of "Non-Primal Abjuration" has a chapter that is missing from our copy at Synelon. I have yet to decide if this chapter was written by the same person, but it is inarguably brilliant.}}
{{Quote|source=[[Alzaneth]]|
'''Can you train me in Weather Witching?'''

You want me to train you in Weather Witching? Well... I'm flattered, but I'm hardly the most qualified instructor. Those I suppose if even MY meager skills are above yours, I should help you out of the doldrums of mediocrity. But it will take time, and I'm only visiting for a short while.

Why don't you run an errand for me? That will free up some time to give you lessons.

I've heard there's an old gale-chanting spot. I'm not sure where it is exactly, but it's on a hill overlooking a lake. There can't be that many lakes, right? You'll know you're in the right spot by the breeze you feel.

I've heard that if you summon a sandstorm while standing there, it will attract a young breeze elemental right to you! Most aren't native to this realm, but something about the winds in the Ilmari allow them to be born. Quite unusual! So anyway, it will find you in seconds. Kill it and get its heart. Don't worry; most wind elementals that grow up here end up insane anyway, so you're doing it a favor, maybe.

After you get that heart, you should definitely leave very quickly, though. Larger wind elementals will be attracted too, and no one can fight a hurricane.}}
