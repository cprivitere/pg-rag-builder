==Summary==
[[Leah Bowman]] in [[Povus]] said, "When the town was healthier, we used to put prettied-up mushrooms on the tree. Fly amanita grows everywhere around here, so that's not a problem. But I don't know how to make the dye! We need a nice tomato red dye to cover the stems."

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Leah Bowman]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''.
===Preface===
When the town was healthier, we used to put prettied-up mushrooms on the tree. Fly amanita grows everywhere around here, so that's not a problem. But I don't know how to make the dye! We need a nice tomato red dye to cover the stems.
===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Fly Amanita}} x20
* Obtain {{Item|Tomato Red Dye}} x1
* Talk to [[Leah Bowman]]
