[[Voo]] is a [[Myconian]] located in the [[Myconian Cave]].  [[Voo]] likes to experiment with [[Alchemy]]. 

Note: [[Voo]] requires you to be slightly more fluent in [[Myconic]] compared to the other friendly Myconians in the cave. You need 11 [[Myconic]] to speak to [[Voo]].
