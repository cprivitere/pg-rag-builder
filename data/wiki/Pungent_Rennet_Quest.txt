==Summary==
Umarak in Povus said, "I want to make my mate's favorite cheese for her, but it requires extremely pungent rennet. And, of course, I have nothing to make that with!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Umarak]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
I want to make my mate's favorite cheese for her, but it requires extremely pungent rennet. And, of course, I have nothing to make that with!

===Midway===
If I had some pungent rennet, I could make my favorite cheese again!

===Requirements===
* Obtain Pungent Rennet
* Talk to Umarak
