==Summary==
You've found Miss Behaved! She seemed rather hungry when you found her and asked that you make her favorite food dish for her trip home.

This Quest can be repeated after 15 days.

You must have previously completed [[Event: Lost Scapegoats]] in order to undertake this quest. This quest is only available during a Lost Scapegoats event. 

===Requirements===
* Obtain 5 Boring Fruit Cocktails
* Speak with Miss Behaved
