==Summary==
Lincha in Povus said, "I used to have a great little meditation stool which I used while working in my garden. The orcs stole it, of course. I would pay well for a replacement... but only if I know the crafter. I don't want to accidentally support slave labor!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lincha]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
I used to have a great little meditation stool which I used while working in my garden. The orcs stole it, of course. I would pay well for a replacement... but only if I know the crafter. I don't want to accidentally support slave labor!

===Midway===
I wish I had a great meditation stool...

===Requirements===
* Craft Great Meditation Stool
* (Keep Great Meditation Stool)
* Talk to Lincha
