{{Quote|source=[[Ricatu]]|
Don't you love this time of year? Smell that crisp air! Ignore the undead stench, and just breath it in! This is a great time to get together with friends, share a feast, and show off your cooking prowess. And I did that last year, but...

I've been thinking about morality, and what it means to have a moral compass. And in the end I just don't think it's right to kill living creatures and eat their flesh. Maybe, maybe if it was a sentient animal who longed for death. I could see that. And I'd be happy to help! But you can't ask non-sentient animals if they mind sacrificing their life for a plump turkey dish or a side of good meaty gravy.

I don't want to miss out, and I don't want my friends making fun of me. So I need you to help me find some viable --and tasty -- alternatives.
}}
[[Ricatu]] is traveling Rakshasa with questionable morals. He offers tasks during the [[Fall Harvest]].
== Location ==
; [[Povus]] 
: [[Ricatu]] can be found in the northwest Inn of Povus, in the city's Old Town district, during the [[Fall Harvest]]. 
== Favor ==
[[Ricatu]] does not use the favor system.
== Quests ==
During the [[Fall Harvest]], [[Ricatu]] offers a variety of quests which require various levels of [[Cooking]] and [[Cheesemaking]] to complete. Level 35 Cheesemaking is required to complete the first quest offered, [[Harvest Event: Not Quite Milk]].
