[[Vendor Fox]] is an [[Industry]]-trained Wizard familiar magically stored in a box. When released, the [[Vendor Fox]] will offer to purchase items from anyone that speaks to them for five minutes. The [[Vendor Fox]] can be summoned every two hours using {{Item|Event: Vendor Fox in a Box}}
== Location ==
; Anywhere
: The [[Vendor Fox]] can be summoned anywhere.
== Shopkeeper ==
[[Vendor Fox]] sell a variety of useful items, and will buy anything. Adventurers can purchase items from the ''Buy Used'' tab of a [[Vendor Fox]]. The ''Buy Used'' tab is shared between summoned foxes.

== Favor ==
A summoned [[Vendor Fox]] will only build favor with its summoner. 
* Favor is raised every time the [[Vendor Fox]] is summoned.
* It takes 300 summons, or 600 hours to reach '''{{favor|Soul Mates}}'''
===Favor Rewards=== 
[[Vendor Fox]] reward their summoner upon an increase in their '''{{favor}}''' with higher Spending Limits in its shop.
