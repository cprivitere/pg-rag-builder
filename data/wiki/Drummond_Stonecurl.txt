[[Drummond Stonecurl]] is an NPC in the eastern end of Vidaria. He trains Cooking, Foraging, and Geology.

== Location ==
; [[Vidaria]] <!-- List the Zone the NPC is found in. Be sure this matches the infobox! -->

== Shopkeeper ==
[[Drummond Stonecurl]] sells a rotating selection of seeds, one type per day.
