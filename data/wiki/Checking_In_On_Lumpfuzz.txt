==Summary==
Tangle in the Sacred Warden Grotto said, "Ever been to the Ilmari Desert? Well, there's a town in the desert called Amulna. In this town is a wolf. Well, more specifically, he's a werewolf. But he's one of the good ones. He has some great merchant contacts and gets us a lot of supplies. This care package includes some money we owe him."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Tangle]] in the [[Sacred Grotto]]. The quest is available at {{Favor|Tolerated}} favor.
