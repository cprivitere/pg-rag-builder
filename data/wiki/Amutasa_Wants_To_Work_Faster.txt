==Summary==
Amutasa requested some Enhanced Cranium Powder so that she can work faster.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Amutasa]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|No matter how much I work, I cannot seem to stop being overwhelmed by work requests. Perhaps a different approach is needed. A visiting elf once invited me to a "dance party" which ended up having very little music and a lot more nakedness than I was expecting. But the highlight of the evening was a powder he brought with him, called 'Enhanced Cranium Powder'. Perhaps if I had some of this... I could finish this work more efficiently! Do you know how to get some?}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Enhanced Cranium Powder}} x3
* Talk to Amutasa
