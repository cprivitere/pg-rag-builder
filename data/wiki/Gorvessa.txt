[[Gorvessa]] is a former warrior (turned harem girl!) who now cooks stew for the band of goblins led by [[Malvol]]. Fluency in [[Goblinese]] (30) is required to talk to her.
== Location ==
; [[Hogan's Basement]]
: Found standing near her stew pot.
