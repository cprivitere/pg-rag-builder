==Summary==
Yasinda in Hogan's Keep asked you to find proof that her daughter is dead. She said, "In the note, the sage told her to find his lean-to under a giant boulder. He said he could just hear the sounds of a waterfall."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Yasinda]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|It's been almost a decade since I heard from my daughter. Last I heard she'd taken up with filthy human "sage" who promised to teach her secret human knowledge. Ha! Human knowledge! Anyway, the sage traveled here and then contacted Sinda and told her to come find him. I'm sure they're both dead by now. But I want to know. Please bring me proof of Sinda's death.

In the note, he told her to find his lean-to under a giant boulder. He said he could just hear the sounds of a waterfall.}}

===Requirements===
{{Spoiler|Find Evidence of Sinda's Death|
Head to the large waterfall west of Hogan's Keep, then keep going southwest along the rocky outcroppings of the map until you find a lean-to against the rocks with Sinda's skeleton.  A nearby backpack contains {{Item|Sinda's Tail Ring}}, which you will return to [[Yasinda]] as proof of her daughter's death.

It may be easier to reach the lean-to by starting at the Red Wing Casino and heading north along the west edge of the map.
