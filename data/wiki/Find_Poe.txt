==Summary==
[[Mu]] asked you to locate [[Poe]], a myconian that may not entirely be under [[Tidal|Tidal's]] influence, and deliver a [[Mu's Message Spore|message-spore]].

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mu]]''' in '''[[Myconian Caverns]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Most myconians have turned away from my counsel. But Poe -- loyal Poe is out there somewhere. I don't really believe Poe has turned away from me. Find Poe and deliver this message-spore. And if Poe needs help, please do whatever is necessary.}}

===Requirements===
* Deliver {{Item|Mu's Message Spore}} to Poe
* Talk to [[Mu]]

{{Spoiler|Response to player|{{Quote|This message spore is from Mu? I see.
