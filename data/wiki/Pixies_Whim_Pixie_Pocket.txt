==Summary==
Uriana in the Fae Realm said, "I remember a thing! It is called a Pixie Pocket. So named by cruel mortals! Or... perhaps named by cruel fairies... I do not remember. But I think I remember the formula! Here: my notes. Make the Pixie Pocket. Tell me if my formula works."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Uriana]] in the [[Fae Realm]]. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.
