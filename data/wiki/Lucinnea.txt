[[Lucinnea]] is a sentient cow capable of conversation. She is a Class 4 Nature Spirit who can be summoned by sacrificing an {{Item|Amazing Steak}} at a rib cage (Ribs) under a small stone overhang, which seemingly acts as an altar. 

== Location ==
; [[Vidaria]]
: [[Lucinnea]] can be summoned north of the Western Council Camp, south of the large Hellfire pit.
