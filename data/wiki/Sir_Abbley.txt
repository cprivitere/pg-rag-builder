[[Sir Abbley]] is [[Statehelm]]'s stablemaster. He offers access to stables and [[Animal Handling]] Training. 
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Sir Abbley]] wanders in and around the stables in [[Statehelm]].
