== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Fae (Race Skill)]] is a skill granted to [[Fairy]] characters.

Fairy is a unique race that is required to use this skill. The fairy race must be unlocked via the [[Free Tortured Fairies]] quest on your current server in order to be seen in character creation.

=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in things that only fairies can do.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Fae (Race Skill)]] is automatically granted to Fairy characters.

=== Fae Energy ===
'''Fae Energy''' is consumed when abilities such as [[Free-Flutter]] are used, or when certain [[Alchemy]] recipes are crafted. 
===== Restoring Fae Energy =====
* {{Item|Foamy Fae Energy Potion}} - ''For fairies: restores up to 40 fae energy, but cannot not raise fae energy above 40.''
* {{Item|Gooey Fae Energy Potion}} - ''For fairies: restores up to 30 fae energy, but cannot not raise fae energy above 30.''
* Randomly recovered when crafting [[Fae (Race Skill)]] recipes.
* Defeating Enemies: +1 Fae Energy is regenerated for every kill, as long as the enemy is of higher  "level" than your total energy. Defeating a level 30 enemy will raise your '''Fae Energy''' from 28 to 29, but if you have 30 '''Fae Energy''' when you defeat it, the energy level will not raise to 31.
===Earning XP===
There are two principal ways to earn experience in [[Fae (Race Skill)]]:
* Many of the starting [[:Category:Quests/Fae_Quests|Fairy Quests]] reward [[Fae (Race Skill)]] XP. This should be enough to get a fairy to somewhere in the level 20-25 range.
* The twelve repeatable (every 5 days) [[Fairy#Help the Hive Quests|Help the Hive Quests]] award 200 [[Fae (Race Skill)]] experience each.
In addition [[Midge the Apothecary]] and [[Crelpin]] each offer faeries a few hangouts which award [[Fae (Race Skill)]] XP.
