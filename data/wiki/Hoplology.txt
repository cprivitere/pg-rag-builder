== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Hoplology]] is secondary non-combat skill for [[Staff]] fighting.
=== In-Game Description ===
The study of weapons and armor, with a focus on the benefits and weaknesses of different kinds of equipment. As the skill increases, Staff users will find that their combat abilities also restore Armor.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Learned from reading a skill-book on the ground in [[Timothy Elerimon]]'s part of [[Wolf Cave]].

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Leveling Hoplology ===
* [[Hoplology]] is leveled when a new base item classified as equipment is identified using the skill. After successfully studying a new item, there is a five minute cooldown on the recipe.
* Treasure effects are ignored by [[Hoplology]]. A <span class="extimage32px">{{Item icon|Basic Sword}}</span> '''Basic Sword of Many Cuts''' is the same item as a <span class="extimage32px">{{Item icon|Basic Sword}}</span> '''Basic Sword of  the Wind.''' Both are classified as a {{Item|Basic Sword}}.
