==Summary==
[[Kelim]] in [[Amulna]] said, "The tree smells horrible, doesn't it? All... piney. I think we should douse it with Telka's Tail. And hang the perfume bottles in the tree as decorations! I have lots of empty bottles, but no full ones. Can you get me two?"

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kelim]]''' in the '''[[Ilmari Desert]]'''.

===Preface===
The tree smells horrible, doesn't it? All... piney. I think we should douse it with Telka's Tail. And hang the perfume bottles in the tree as decorations! I have lots of empty bottles, but no full ones. Can you get me two?

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Telka's Tail}} x2
* Talk to [[Kelim]]
