== Shopkeeper ==
{{Vendor items sold}}
== Favor ==
As favor is earned with [[Kohan]], favor for [[Ufkar]] is reduced.

For an easy way to get favor with him, you can simply buy the [[Life or Death Potion]]'s from the nearby [[Ichin the Ice Master]] provided you have his favor level requirement. Alternatively, you can shop the used tab on him for exotic potions players have sold.

Most [[Carpentry]] vendor's used tabs will have various carpentry items players have sold to them that you can purchase for favor too.
