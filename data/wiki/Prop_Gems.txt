==Summary==
Barnaby in the Sacred Warden Grotto said, "Young foxes love colorful gems! I could use a selection of nice colors to use as teaching props."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Barnaby]]''' in the '''[[Sacred Grotto]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. You must have previously completed [[Fox Sewing Lessons]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
Young foxes love colorful gems! I could use a selection of nice colors to use as teaching props.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Onyx x3
* Obtain Carnelians x3
* Obtain Tourmalines x3
* Obtain Topazes x3
* Talk to Barnaby
