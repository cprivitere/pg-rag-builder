==Summary==
Kalaba in Eltibule taught you the basics of [[Transmutation]]. He asked you to find weak magical items ("Uncommon" rarity items, with a green label) and turn them into Phlogiston. You learned a Transmutation recipe to do this -- it's in your Recipes menu.
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kalaba]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
===Preface===
{{Quote|
Since I already taught you Transmutation, you're ready to go find weak magical items and turn them into phlogiston for me. Bring me some low-grade packets of Phlogiston.

As a Transmutation beginner, you'll need to find Uncommon (green-label) magic items that are no higher than level 30. Use the Transmutation recipe you learned to distill these items to Phlogiston.
}}

===Requirements===
* Distill Simple Magic Items Into Phlogiston x4
* Collect Some of the Resulting Phlogiston x7
* Talk to Kalaba
