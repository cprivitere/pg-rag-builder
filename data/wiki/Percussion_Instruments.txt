== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Percussion Instruments]]is one of three musical instrument Sub-skills of [[Performance]].
=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in performing with percussion instruments such as drums, bells, and dulcimers.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Percussion Instruments]] is automatically unlocked when an instrument associated with the skill that has a Level requirement of 0 is equipped.
* {{Item|Hand Drum}} unlocks the skill at Level 0.
* {{Item|Mini-Dulcimer}} unlocks Level 11-20 abilities.
* {{Item|Fairy Chimes}} unlock Level 21-30 abilities.
== Connected Skills ==
===== Sub-Skills: =====
* None.
===== Secondary Skills: =====
* [[Performance]] - [[Percussion Instruments]] is a Sub-skill of [[Performance]].
* [[Stringed Instruments]] - Skill in performing with stringed instruments such as harps, lutes, and guitars.
* [[Wind Instruments]] - Skill in performing with brass and woodwind instruments such as trumpets, ocarinas, and flutes.
===== Related Skills: =====
* [[Interpretive Dance]] - Skill in dancing to music in a way that entertains or moves others.
* [[Oratory]] - The ability to speak in a public way that moves your audience.
* [[Bard]] - A combat ability that uses musical instruments.
===== Synergy Levels: =====
There are no Bonus [[Synergy Levels]] for [[Percussion Instruments]].

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Music ===
Playing music grants buffs which can boost combat experience gained, out-of-combat armor regeneration, and sometimes other effects if a group of players gathers.
=== Gardening ===
Playing music while [[Gardening]] can increase the chances for a larger crop harvest.
=== Finding Instruments ===
*{{Item|Hand Drum}} - Often drops from Orcs in [[Kur Mountains]] or [[Gazluk]].
*{{Item|Mini-Dulcimer}} - Rarely drops from Yeti in [[Yeti Cave]].
*{{Item|Fairy Chimes}} - Earned by completing a quest for [[Felmer]].
