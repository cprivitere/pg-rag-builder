[[Urglemarg]] is a [[Ranalon]] encountered underwater in [[Sun Vale]].

== Favor ==
[[Urglemarg]] does not use the favor system.

== Services ==
==== Bartering ====
[[Urglemarg]] barters items (of random rarity<sup>needs confirmation</sup>) which boost underwater survivability and mobility, in exchange for [[Sand Dollars]].

Standard items possess level '''35''' enchantments, '''fancy''' versions have level '''40''' enchantments, while '''extra-fancy''' gear comes with enchantments of level '''45'''.
