==Summary==
Yogzi in Sun Vale wants you to make him a bunch of simple skinning knives.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Yogzi]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

To begin this quest you must have level 1 [[Toolcrafting]].
