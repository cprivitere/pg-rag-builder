== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Priest]] is a combat skill. Despite [[Gloria Stonecurl]] saying by agreeing to be a priest, you will be expected to help save the world from a cataclysm, Priest does not have any equivalent mechanics to [[Druid|Druids]] or Druidic Emergencies. 
=== In-Game Description ===
Officially the 'Priest of Arisetsu' skill, this represents your attunement with Arisetsu, the goddess of Warmth and Hope. When fighting alongside allies, you can bring hope to even the most impossible battles... but you cannot do it alone. You must be wearing a holy symbol -- and NOT wearing a Necromancy Gem -- to use Priest abilities. Holy symbols are most often found as jewelry, but can also be found on armor and weapons.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Gloria Stonecurl]] in [[Kur Mountains]] teaches [[Priest]]. You must have either 25 Levels of [[Compassion]] or [[Psychology]] to learn the skill, {{favor|Comfortable}} status with Gloria, and pay a fee of 5,000 councils.
* [[Aralorn]] in [[Gazluk]] unlocks levels 51-60, 61-70.
* [[Raina]] unlocks 71-80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Reinath]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Steven Poplar]] in [[Statehelm]] unlocks levels 81-90, 91-100, and [[Prodigy Potential]].
