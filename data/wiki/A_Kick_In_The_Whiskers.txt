==Summary==
Dachak in Povus said, "The ratkin used to be friendly. When I was younger, they would come into town and I'd sell 'em vegetables! But after the fall of Povus they attack on sight. I had to give up mining because of them. I wish somebody would teach 'em a lesson for me! Hint, hint."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Dachak the Digger]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. 

===Preface===
The ratkin used to be friendly. When I was younger, they would come into town and I'd sell 'em vegetables! But after the fall of Povus they attack on sight. I had to give up mining because of them. I wish somebody would teach 'em a lesson for me! Hint, hint.

===Requirements===
* Kill Ratkin in Povus x15 (Ratkin Shock Assistants appear not to count)
* Talk to Dachak
