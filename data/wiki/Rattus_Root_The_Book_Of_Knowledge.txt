==Summary==
Bogaku in the Rahu Sewers said, "Rattus root is a favorite snack of these barbaric vermin. I'm sure to a mutant like you it just looks like a root, right? But to a gobthropologist, it's like an open book! A book of secrets! So go get me my secret books! I mean rattus roots!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Bogaku]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
