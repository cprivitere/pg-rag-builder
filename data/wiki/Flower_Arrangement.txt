== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Flower Arrangement''' is used to craft arrangements to help with foraging, fishing, etc.

=== In-Game Description ===
The art of arranging flowers so that they evoke strong emotions.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Taught by [[Zhao]] in [[Rahu]], and requires [[Gardening]] level 50. To begin with '''Flower Arrangement''', one must pay 30,000 councils, or bring 150 of any flower seeds{{Hint|Can be a mixture of different seeds}} to [[Zhao]]. As a note, the flower seed turn in will take the lowest level seeds in your inventory, regardless of folder placement. Move any seeds you intend to keep to your saddlebag.
* [[Landri the Cold]] teaches cleanliness recipes
* [[Rohina]] teaches mycologist recipes as well as [[Flower Arrangement]] level 50-60.
* [[Floxie]] unlocks level 61-70 and 71-80.  She teaches standard recipes of those levels, as well as the Bee Lovers' Bouquet.
* [[The Floriculturist]] unlocks level 81-90 and they teaches standard recipes of those levels

== Connected Skills ==
===== Related Skills: =====
* [[Foraging]] - You need to forage the seeds for the flowers, to make the bouquets.
* [[Gardening]] - You need to grow the seeds for the flowers, to make the bouquets, using this skill.
* [[Nature Appreciation]] - You need levels in this to appreciate most of the flower arrangements.
