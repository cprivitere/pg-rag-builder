==Summary==
Daisy in Sun Vale's Animal Town asked for some eyestalks. They come from Watchers, which are terrible monsters generally found deep within dungeons.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Daisy_(NPC)|Daisy]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
