==Summary==
Merriana in Serbule Hills said, "I have expanded my search and have come into stone-contact with a human engineer near here who claims to know what I need. However, he won't tell me over the aether. Please locate this man and retrieve his letter. He is to be found "In the Serbule Sewers." His name is Arif."
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Merriana]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Tolerated}} favor. The quest [[Merriana's Second Request]] must be completed first.
