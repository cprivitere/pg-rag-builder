==Summary==
Yurra the hag suggested that you might find [[Dalvos]] somewhere in Serbule. She said that an elf in town had been inspecting the giant red crystals that [[Dalvos]] is known to make. Find this elf and see if she can help you.

==Prerequisites==
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Yurra]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''.

==Preface==
