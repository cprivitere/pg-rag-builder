== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Sigil Scripting''' is a kind of arcane trap-making skill taught by [[Sir Arif]] once you have 20 [[Calligraphy]]. The skill also includes some utility and beneficial symbols (also refered as "runes") such as one that knocks back enemies, and a restorative rune that heals nearby allies. Sigil Scripting does NOT go on one of your two main combat bars. Instead, all abilities can be placed on your sidebar.

All Sigils require consumables, typically Inks. Sir Arif sells all of them, but they're also craftable through [[Alchemy]].

Sigil Scripting also involves etching runes onto gems, or specifically, Malachite. This attunes Malachite to particular animal form, allowing you to craft gear for it. Such recipes are sold by [[Preta]].

Note hands are required to place sigils.

=== In-Game Description ===
Using magical inks and arcane rituals to create areas of power.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* [[Sir Arif]] teach Sigil Scripting

* [[Kohan]] will unlock levels 51-60 and 51+ Sigils.
* Level 60-70 is unlocked by defeating a fabled dragon of yore.
* Levels 71-80 are unlocked by [[Serendipity Cheddar]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
