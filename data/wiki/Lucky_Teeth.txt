==Summary==
Bogaku in the Rahu Sewers said, "Have you found that these ratkin are superstitious? Most are! They even carry good-luck charms, the pathetic things. Hmm, go and find me some of their charms."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Bogaku]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. You must have completed [[Ratkin Gaming Habits]].

===Preface===
Have you found that these ratkin are superstitious? Most are! They even carry good-luck charms, the pathetic things. Hmm, go and find me some of their charms.
