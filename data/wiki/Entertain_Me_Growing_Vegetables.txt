__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Pennoc in Serbule told you to grow some fresh vegetables. He wasn't particular about the type -- just that you grow them yourself. 

(Fun fact: Getting vegetables from Ranalon Food Bowls in the [[Ranalon Den]] counts as growing vegetables for this quest. That means you can do this entirely without gardening if you really want to.)

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pennoc]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Why don't you go grow some vegetables for me? Yes. Grow a dozen fresh vegetables. How bored am I? I'm bored enough to watch plants grow -- THAT'S how bored!}}

===Requirements===
* Grow Vegetables
* Talk to Pennoc
