==Summary==
Lakrea told you to work the word 'maladaptive' into your next conversation.

Maladaptive: adjective: not providing adequate or appropriate adjustment to the environment or situation.

(Note: if you use the word out of context, the game won't know, but <i>you'll</i> know that you lost 10,000 Roleplay Points.)

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lakrea]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Holistic Wellness: 'Beleaguered']] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|You need to expand your useful vocabulary. Not your total vocabulary, your <i>useful</i> vocabulary. You know thousands of words that you never think to use in daily life. Am I right? Of course I'm right. Here's a word to use: 'maladaptive'.

<i>[ Maladaptive: adjective: not providing adequate or appropriate adjustment to the environment or situation. ]</i>

 Just find a place to work that into your next conversation. But don't just throw it out there! Use it in a conversation!}}

===Requirements===
* Use 'Maladaptive' In a Conversation
* Talk to Lakrea
