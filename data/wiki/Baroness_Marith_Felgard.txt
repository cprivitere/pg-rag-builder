[[Baroness Marith Felgard]] is a baroness living in her castle in [[Serbule Hills]].

She was appointed baroness by [[Lord Serbule]] before his disappearance and sees herself as the rightful leader of Serbule Hills. However, [[The Council]] has since taken charge of [[Serbule]] and the surrounding areas, so her official title is nothing more than a title.

She has disliked [[Nelson Ballard]] ever since she requested assistance with Serbule Hills' [[ranalon]] problem and he simply told her to evacuate to [[Serbule Keep]]. It is possible he would have requested assistance from the [[Council Army]], but they are currently preoccupied fighting demons in [[Vidaria]].

Nowadays, she is still the most powerful person in Serbule Hills, since the Council does not enforce their laws much in Serbule, let alone the sparsely-populated hills south of Serbule. She despises [[ranalon]]s and would like to see the [[Serbule Hills]] ranalons killed off or driven away.

She seems to dislike the fact that many displaced elves have fled into Serbule.

== Shopkeeper ==
[[Baroness Marith Felgard]] sells a selection of supplies and seedlings used in [[Gardening]]. She also sells Tannin Powder, a Basic Fertilizer Recipe, [[Handsaw|Handsaws]] and [[Bottle of Water|Bottles of Water]].
