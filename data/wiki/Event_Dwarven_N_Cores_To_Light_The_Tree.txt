[[Zhao]] in [[Rahu]] said, "The tree came with a complex system of wires and sparking mechanisms. Apparently they can make the tree glow. I've asked Ufkar to reverse-engineer it, and he says we can make it work with a power source. A few dwarven N cores ought to work."
