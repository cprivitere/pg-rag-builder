[[Justin Fargold]] is a human merchant who currently runs an [[Alchemy]] shop in the City of Povus. Before the death of the city's previous alchemist, [[Justin Fargold|Justin]] ran an art gallery. 
== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Justin Fargold]] can be found in a shop south of the forge, when the Povus Town Level is at least 10.
== Favor ==
[[Justin Fargold]] does not use the favor system.
== Services == 
==== Bartering ==== 
Justin Fargold offers to barter alchemical potions for {{Item|Xogrite Chunk}}. The selection of barters changes at 8am each in-game day. Prices and quantities are also variable.
