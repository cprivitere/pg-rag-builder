Moxie said, "Hey could I get you to do me a personal favor? I'm really low on supplies and I'm in dire need of [[Expert-Quality Metal Slab]]s. Lots of them! I promise I'll only ask this of you once - never again! If you can deliver me 25 Expert-Quality Metal Slabs, I'll show you what I use them for!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Moxie]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.
