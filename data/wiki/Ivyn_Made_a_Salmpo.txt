==Summary==
Ivyn in Serbule asked you to kill the Salmpo, a plant creature he accidentally grew. It's south of town.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ivyn]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Close Friends}} favor.
