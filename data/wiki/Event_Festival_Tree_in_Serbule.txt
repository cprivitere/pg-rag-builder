==Summary==
There's a barren festival tree near Echur in Serbule. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Echur]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Get Apples for Decorations
* Get Gingerbread Humans for Decoration
* Get Decorations from Nelson Ballard
* Get Tree-Topper from Mr. Squeaky
* Talk to [[Echur]]
