==Summary==
Malgath in the werewolf den asked you to obtain some wolfsbane for him.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Malgath]]''' in '''[[Wolf Cave]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
