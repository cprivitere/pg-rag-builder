==Summary==
Chirrra in  the Serbule Hills Ranalon Den said, "Another mission already? Um... just go kill some ranalons! Whichever. Bring me some of their tacky jewelry to prove you've done your job."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chirrra]]''' in '''[[Ranalon Den]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

This quest requires the previous completion of a [[:Category:Quests/Chirrra Tier 1|Tier 1]] quest from Chirrra.

This is a solo quest from Chirrra. You may have one solo quest from Chirrra at a time. You can take on a new solo quest 15 minutes after completing or canceling this quest.
