==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "Oh, waiter, finally! Yes, I want to know what makes your beetle skewer a "Deluxe" bettle skewer? Oh, don't tell me, I want to be surprised!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
Oh, waiter, finally! Yes, I want to know what makes your beetle skewer a "Deluxe" bettle skewer? Oh, don't tell me, I want to be surprised!

===Midway===
Where's my deluxe beetle skewer?

* Craft Deluxe Beetle Skewer
* (Keep Deluxe Beetle Skewer)
* Talk to Ailred
