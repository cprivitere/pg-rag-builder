[[Gleanfeckle]] is a fairy refugee who lives in the city of [[Statehelm]]. 
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Gleanfeckle]] may be found wandering the city of Statehelm during the day. They are often found in the southwest parts of the city. Sometimes they can be found inside the first floor classroom of the Bard College.
