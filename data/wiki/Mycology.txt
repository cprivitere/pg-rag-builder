== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Mycology]] is a skill that is raised by gathering mushrooms. It gives access to a variety of recipes for the creation of Spore Bombs, poisons, and potions. Mushrooms are also useful in [[Cooking]] and [[Alchemy]] recipes.

Mycology is usually acquired early on around [[Anagoge]] Island by gathering a patch of Parasol Mushrooms. The skill continues to raise afterwards as you gather various kinds of mushrooms. New players will likely encounter higher tiers of Mushrooms first in the town of Serbule. There are a few patches around the barn which will require higher skill level.
=== In-Game Description ===
Mastery of fungus in all its forms
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Mycology]] is often first learned on [[Anagoge]] or in [[Serbule]] by picking mushrooms or piles of dirt off of the ground. The first recipe, [[Parasol Mushroom Flakes]], is learned by studying an Alchemy book [[Basics of Alchemy]].  The book may be found on [[Elmetaph]]'s bookcase in the lighthouse, or on the second level of the [[Anagoge Records Facility]].

* Another [[Mycology]] recipe is also found in the [[Anagoge Records Facility]], on the first level. On a pedestal in a room containing several mushrooms, rest the '''[[Proctor's Journals]]'''.  If the player chooses to '''Study the Details''' they can learn the recipe for [[Basic Spore Bomb]]s.
* [[Mushroom Jack]] and [[Charles Thompson]] both sell [[Mycology]] recipes and abilities from their homes in [[Serbule]].
* [[Voo]] and [[Jaw]] in the [[Myconian Cave]] sell [[Mycology]] recipes and abilities.
* [[Yagreet]] in [[Sun Vale]] sells [[Mycology]] potion recipes.
* [[Hemmit Magmagrip]], a traveling mycologist studying in the [[New Prestonbule Cave]], unlocks levels 51-60 and 61-70 of [[Mycology]].
* [[Backfat]] in the [[Fae Realm]] can sniff out levels 71-80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]] or [[Strom Farblood]] in Statehelm.
* Levels 91-100 are unlocked by [[Strom Farblood]].
