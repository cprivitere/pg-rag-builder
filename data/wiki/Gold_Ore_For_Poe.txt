==Summary==
Poe the Myconian asked for a bunch of unrefined gold ore.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Poe]]''' in '''[[Myconian Caverns]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

To begin this quest you must have level 1 [[Jewelry Augmentation]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|I must ask you again for aid in preparing my special augments. I will need many such items to fund my travels. Gold works well for creating the augments. Unrefined gold ore is best. I've noticed that overworlders often keep gold on hand. Do you happen to have any?}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain Unrefined {{Item|Gold Ore}} x4
* Talk to Poe
