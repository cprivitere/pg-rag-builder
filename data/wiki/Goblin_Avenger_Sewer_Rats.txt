==Summary==
Glortok in the Rahu Sewers said, "The ratkin just keep... surviving! How is it possible?! Perhaps we need to broaden our approach. The sewer rat may seem harmless... but it's wily and dangerous and probably somehow the secret crux of our problem!"

This Quest can be repeated after 66 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chief Glortok]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Goblin Avenger: Accursed Hippos]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
The ratkin just keep... surviving! How is it possible?! Perhaps we need to broaden our approach. The sewer rat may seem harmless... but it's wily and dangerous and probably somehow the secret crux of our problem!

===Requirements===
* Kill Sewer Rats x15
* Talk to Glortok
