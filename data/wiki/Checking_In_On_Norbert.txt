==Summary==
Tangle in the Sacred Warden Grotto said, "Check in on Norbert for me, will you? I know you've met him already in Sun Vale. He's a good pig. Used to run missions with us, but some of his friends died in the big massacre, and he's been a lot more distant since then. Take this stuff to show we still care, that sort of thing."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Tangle]] in the [[Sacred Grotto]]. The quest is available at {{Favor|Tolerated}} favor.
