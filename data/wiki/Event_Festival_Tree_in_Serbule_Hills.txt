==Summary==
There's a barren festival tree at the Baroness's house in Serbule Hills. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Baroness Marith Felgard]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Get Goblin Cards for Decorations
* Get Grapes for Decorations
* Get Decorations from Nelson Ballard
* Get Tree-Topper from Ranalon
* Talk to Marith Felgard
