==Summary==
In a cave beneath Povus, you came upon a strange door that could only be opened if you find all the "lost penguins." Presumably they are near the door?

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to the Penguin Gate in '''[[Nightmare Caves]]'''.

===Preface===
{{Quote|The door doesn't budge. A cloying voice says, "Where are my penguins?! I won't go anywhere without ALL of my penguins!"}}

===Requirements===
* Find the Lost Penguins
* Return to the Door

===Other Notes===
{{Spoiler|Non-specific penguin hint|

}}
