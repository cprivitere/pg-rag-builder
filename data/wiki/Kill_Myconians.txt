==Summary==
Mushroom Jack in Serbule wants you to find and kill a dozen Myconians. He says he'll know when you've done it.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mushroom Jack]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I don't know if you believe me or not about these Myconians. But I need you to believe! Go out there and find yourself some. And kill them! Kill a dozen! Then come back. I'll know you did the deed. The stink will be all over you. Go now. Do it.}}

===Requirements===
* Kill Myconians {{Hint|You have to talk to a Large Mushroom (NW section of map) and have 8 Myconian to enter the dungeon}}
* Talk to Mushroom Jack
