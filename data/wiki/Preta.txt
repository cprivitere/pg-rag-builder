[[Preta]] is a [[Summer Court]] fairy living at the base of the giant tree in [[Sun Vale]]. She specializes in sigils and runes.  When she lived in the fae realm, Preta ran a library that cataloged the [[Words of Power]] of her realm. She is afraid of the torture cells her kin resurrect in when killed.

== Services ==
==== Consignment ====
; Preta offers [[Consignment]] at {{Favor|Friends}}.
: Mark-up fee of 10%, stocking fee of 100 Councils.
: Max 2 active consignments (Books, scrolls, recipes, parchment).
: Max 4 consignments per week.

==== Augmentation ====
[[Preta]] can install Level 0-50 Augments for 350 Councils. You must be {{favor|Close Friends}} or better.
