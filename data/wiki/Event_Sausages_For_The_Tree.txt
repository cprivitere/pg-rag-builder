==Summary==
[[Spot]] in [[Animal Town]] said, "We need stringy things. Like... um, string. No, string is ugly. Sausages! Sausages are stringy! Get some!

But please <i>please</i> don't tell Norbert because he yells at me when I say I like sausage. Please? Okay good."

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Spot]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. This quest is only available to long time animals.

===Preface===
We need stringy things. Like... um, string. No, string is ugly. Sausages! Sausages are stringy! Get some!

But please <i>please</i> don't tell Norbert because he yells at me when I say I like sausage. Please? Okay good.

* Obtain {{Item|Sausage}} x6
