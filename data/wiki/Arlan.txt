[[Arlan]] is a male [[Elven|Elf]] living in a small outpost in the [[Ilmari]] Desert. Around twenty years ago, [[Arlan]] and his wife Bonnie tried to open a boutique near a small oasis. It was planned to be called "Bonnie's Hot Springs." He claims that the resident [[Rakshasa]] went insane and killed Bonnie. For these past twenty years, [[Arlan]] has been wandering the world, only returning to the boutique recently.
