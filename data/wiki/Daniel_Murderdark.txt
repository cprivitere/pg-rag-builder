== Conversations ==
{{Quote|source=[[Daniel Murderdark]], First conversation|
: Welcome, guy! The darkness awaits us all. Do you dare accept your fate?
: ''' Sure, we've all got to go some time, I guess.'''
: How right you are! I sense you know much about the ways of death! Perhaps, with me as your guide, you could go beyond the veil of darkness.
: ''' Why are you talking like that? '''
: Look, I was just trying to ... ''Daniel Murderdark sighs.''

: What do you want?
: ''' Don't change the subject, what were you trying to do?'''
: I'm trying to drum up business, okay?  The rakkies... err, rakshasa, sorry... don't really know about necromancy. They think it's mysterious and interesting.
: ''' Is that why you call yourself 'Daniel Murderdark'?'''
: Yes. And also because it's an awesome name.
: ''' Right. Anyway... so you teach necromancy? '''
: I'm still working on that. I'm having a really hard time explaining it in a way the novice can understand. But if you already know the basics, I can teach you some fancy tricks!
: ''' Then where can people learn the basics? '''
: Nowhere, okay? ... I don't know! My father taught me, but he's not exactly accepting new students. So I'm trying to teach people, but ... just get off my back, guy, I'm doing my best!
: ''' Okay, okay. I didn't mean to upset you.'''
: Sorry for getting stressed out. Hustling in this town is hard, but I'm trying to make it work. Look, if you don't know Necromancy, maybe I can interest you in some anti-Necromancy potions! Just check out my skeletal assistant there. If you have to fight an evil necromancer, you want one of my darkness resistance potions!
: ''' Thanks, I'll take a look.'''
}}
