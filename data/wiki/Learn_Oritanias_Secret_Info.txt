==Summary==
Oritania in Eltibule has information about Dalvos's whereabouts. But she won't tell you unless you help her with some errands first. Get that info!
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Oritania]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. The quest [[Chasing Dalvos to Eltibule]] must be completed first.
