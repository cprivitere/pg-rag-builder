[[Strom Farblood]] is a dwarf from [[New Aufghel]] living in the city of [[Statehelm]]. He works as a guest professor for the [[Statehelm School of Magic]].
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Strom Farblood]] may be found in his laboratory upstairs at the School of Magic.

== Quests ==
[[Strom Farblood]] will offer a quest to those who have joined the [[Statehelm School of Magic]] and reached level 55 in [[Alchemy]].

Upon completing his quest, [[Strom Farblood]] will offer training for higher levels of [[Alchemy]], [[Battle Chemistry]], and [[Mycology]].
== Training ==
[[Alchemy]] levels 51-60, 61-70, 71-80 and some recipes.

[[Battle Chemistry]] levels 81-90, 91-100 and associated skills, plus the golem skills.

[[Mycology]] levels 81-90, 91-100 and some recipes.
