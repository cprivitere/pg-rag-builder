==Summary==
Braigon in Eltibule said, "I'm working on some mozzarella right now. Bring me some whey and we'll talk about the tofu."
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Braigon]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''.  The quest is available during the [[Fall Harvest]]. The quest [[Harvest Event: Learn About Tofu]] must be completed first.

===Preface===

}}

===Requirements===
* Collect {{Item|Bottle of Whey}} x5
* Collect {{Item|Bottle of Sweet Whey}} x5
* Talk to Braigon
