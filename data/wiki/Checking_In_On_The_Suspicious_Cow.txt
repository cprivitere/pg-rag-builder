==Summary==
Tangle in the Sacred Warden Grotto said, "We need to check in on some of our spy network members. These are animals in distant lands that work with us. They aren't all members of the Wardens, but they're entrusted with our secret communication ritual. Start by checking in with a cow in Eltibule. I don't know her name... she's never given us one. But she says she's the only talking cow in her herd, so... see what you can do. Take this care package to her. It's some stuff she asked for a while back."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Tangle]] in the [[Sacred Grotto]]. The quest is available at {{Favor|Tolerated}} favor.
