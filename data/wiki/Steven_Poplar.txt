{{Quote|source=[[Steven Poplar]] (Complete Hang Out: Learn this "secret technique" that Steven is so excited about)|
'''About that "special technique" you taught me...'''

We spent hours going over this. Don't tell me you lost your memories AGAIN?!

No, no, I' sorry. It's fine! Here's what you need to know.

I taught you a technique to harvest excess negative emotions from undead corpses. Check the almanac in the church to see what types of undead are most overflowing right now; those should be your target.

You don't have to be a priest or use priestly powers to make this work; you just need to wear a symbol of Arisetsu while you kill the undead. Then when you check their corpses, you may spot blobs of excess emotion, so called "pearls."

Bring those pearls back to me and I can use the Muradrake Mirror to invert them into wholesome emotional energy. And then I can use THAT energy to heal people! Among other things.

'''You're using undead energy to heal people? Is this really safe?'''

I'm NOT using undead energy to heal people! That's a terrible thought.

See the mirror over there? That's the Muradrake Mirror. Created by Steven Muradrake, who was one of the best and noblest necromancers humanity has ever seen. I can use the mirror to invert the emotional context of dark emotion pearls. Doing so creates a tiny magical spark of hopeful energy, which I can harness.

Now SOME may say that using a necromantic artifact goes against Arisetsu's will, but Arisetsu hates undeath, not necromancy per se. I did a bunch of research to confirm this. Personally I believe Arisetsu would be pleased to see us conjuring negative emotions into positivity.

But please don't tell other priests of Arisetsu. Especially the elven priests -- they're very old-fashioned and any new idea scares them.
