==Summary==
Glortok in the Rahu Sewers said, "Those creepy red-eyed rats must be a source of their power. Destroy them! No mercy!"

This Quest can be repeated after 66 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chief Glortok]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Goblin Avenger: Undying Ratkin Miners]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
Those creepy red-eyed rats must be a source of their power. Destroy them! No mercy!

===Requirements===
* Kill Necro Rats x15
* Talk to Glortok
