==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] sent me to find a Tuvillic Rune along the outer wall of [[Povus]] and obtain a Droach Pineal Gland. Completing this quest rewards the [[Willbreaker]] ability.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[The Mind Made Anew]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|The final ingredient I need to recover my mental powers is... an ancient pineal gland. I know where you can find exactly... What I need. Search for a Tuvillic Rune in Povus. I believe it is somewhere along the outer wall.}}

===Requirements===
* Touch Vampire Rune on Wall of [[Povus]]
* Obtain {{Item|Droach Pineal Gland}} x1
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]
