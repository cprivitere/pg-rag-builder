==Summary==
<i>Zhia Lian:</i> The undead are extra active right now, so you need to slay them! Start by killing skeletons. You can find them in a graveyard. Or lots of other places, really.

Oh, and if you're freaked out about murder, don't worry: it doesn't count as murder when you kill the undead! You can trust me. I'm a god.

This Quest can be repeated after 60 days.



===Requirements===
* Kill skeletons x5
* Loot a bone from a skeleton
