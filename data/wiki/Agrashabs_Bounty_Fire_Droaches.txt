==Summary==
Agrashab in the Fae Realm said, "The so-called 'firebird mountain' south of here is home to an irritating phoenix population, which terrify my subjects, but at least they don't typically hunt trolls for food! Droaches, unfortunately, do. Please head to 'Firebird Mountain' and eliminate the fire droach threat."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Agrashab/Fae Realm|Agrashab]] in the [[Fae Realm]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.
