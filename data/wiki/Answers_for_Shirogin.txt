==Summary==
Shirogin in Rahu asked you to confront Ichin the Ice-Master. Ichin has an invitation to the formal execution, and Shirogin wants to know why he didn't get one.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Shirogin]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|The formal execution is a very important social event for the upper crust of Rahu. I used to go every time... but this is the third time in a row that I didn't get invited.

I need to know what I did. Ask my friend Ichin why I'm not invited. I know Ichin plans to go to the execution... he must know what's going on! I would confront him myself, but... I'm not really the confrontational type.}}

===Requirements===
* Talk to Ichin the Ice Master
* Talk to Shirogin

{{Spoiler|Response to player|{{Quote|What? Shirogin sent you? Hahaha! This is <i>exactly</i> why he wasn't invited! He skulks! He has others do his dirty work. He connives. He worries. He pleads.

Shirogin is something between a master schemer and a desperate fawning kitten. And I say that as Shirogin's closest friend!

Tell him what I told you. There was an incident at a previous execution which got him banned... he'll know which one.

Tell him that I care for him, and that as his friend, I am telling him that he needs to drop this.}} }}
