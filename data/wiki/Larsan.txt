[[Larsan]] is a [[Dwyndarre]] refugee living in [[Serbule]] that trades in jewelry. Like many of his fellow Elven shopkeepers, he greatly dislikes the Taxes imposed upon him.
{{Quote|
Did you hear the humans want to collect taxes from us? Tax! Just for running a shop! That's absurd. There's barely any security here, so what would I be paying for? If you want to collect tax, you'd better... sorry. Sorry! Council customs make me a little upset.
}}
