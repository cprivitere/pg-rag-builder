[[Flia]] is a fairy serving the [[Winter Court]].  She is a spy, who has successfully located the sisters [[Jesina]] and [[Sarina]].

When Favor with Flia reaches "Like Family" she suggests that she could transform you into a fairy, but it would take eons of torture.
== Location ==
; [[Serbule]]
: Stands south of the altar that is east of Serbule Keep, and north of the Standing Stones.
