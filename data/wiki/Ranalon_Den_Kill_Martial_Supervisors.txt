==Summary==
Chirrra in  the Serbule Hills Ranalon Den said, "You need to focus on the adults that oversee the leglings! Strike them down to weaken the den. Head into the legling training area. Go past the gardening practice area. When the path forks, go left to find the clubbers. A few adults are there. Strike them down!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chirrra]]''' in '''[[Ranalon Den]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

This is a solo quest from Chirrra. You may have one solo quest from Chirrra at a time. You can take on a new solo quest 15 minutes after completing or canceling this quest.
