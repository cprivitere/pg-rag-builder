==Summary==
Azalak the mantis needs you to collect a bunch of intact brains from sentient creatures. Brain Bugs occasionally drop intact brains, as do several other creatures, such as Nightmare Creatures and Psychic Mantises.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Azalak]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Zeratak and I wish to make a living selling our special alchemical brain elixirs. These are traditional psi-mantis recipes, but I am attempting to hone them to be less toxic to other races. I believe that the lobes from the brain bugs are too potent... instead, I wish to grind up their entire brains. Or any fully intact brains will do, actually. I am a scientist. I am not picky.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain Intact {{Item|Brain}}s x3
* Talk to Azalak
