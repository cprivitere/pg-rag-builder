==Update Notes: March 27, 2020==
This is another quick bug-fix update. We'll be back with the next regular update in just a few weeks!

*Fixed situation where monsters could "see" players through walls
*Fixed issue with a handful of loot items that didn't automatically stack when looted from certain monsters
*Removed all levels of Fae racial skill for non-Fae
*Fixed bug that prevented certain delayed debuffs from applying in certain situations. (This includes the debuffs that turn you into a cow or bat after a certain amount of time.)
*Addressed problems with some old game accounts that could not awaken the fairies in the "Free Tortured Fairies" quest
