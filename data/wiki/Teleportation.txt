== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Teleportation''' is the main method for players to quickly move around the world. It works like this: you Bind to a specific circle, and then have the ability to teleport to that Circle in the future. There are other recipes, like the ability to recall to the beginning of any zone, or to have 2 circles bound. 

=== In-Game Description ===
The magical art of suddenly being somewhere else.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* [[Elmetaph]] teaches Teleportation on [[Anagoge|Anagoge Island]]. It is possible to acquire the necessary amethyst gems on the island by searching through items on the ground, especially bags near the teleporation circle on the south side of the island (and the broken teleportation circle on the north side of the island).

* [[Sem]] in the Gaz zone can train you in Mushroom Teleportation.

* Teleportation can alternatively be learned through the book {{Item|Teleportation For Beginners}}, which is sold by [[Flia]] and [[Jesina]]. However, it is rare for players to get off the island without learning it.

* [[Tyler Green]] offers a {{Item|Teleportation For Beginners}} book as the reward for his [[Rubbery Tongues]] quest.

* [[Ratuk the Thinker]] also teaches specialized Teleportation spells such as '''Bind Alternate Circle''' and '''Recall Alternate Circle'''. Even a teleportation ability that can be used in combat: '''Organ Displacement'''!

* [[Sem]] supposedly teaches Mushroom-Circle Bind in [[Gazluk Shadow Cave]] difficulty level 60.  Mushroom teleportation can be performed only at [[Mushroom Circle]]s.

* For Fairy players, you must level up your [[Fae (Race Skill)]] in order to obtain the respective skills shown in the recipes table below. See the [[Fae (Race Skill)]] for more information.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Experience ===
The first time you use (bind to or recall from) a Teleportation Circle, you're rewarded 200 bonus Teleportation XP. You can click a Teleportation Circle icon on your map to see how many times you have used that circle and if it is eligible for the bonus.

You can see how many unique teleportation circles you've already bound to in each zone by going to the Skills pane -> Teleportation -> List Used Teleport Locations. You can also see your currently bound Circle at Skills -> Teleportation -> Get Binding Info.

=== Teleportation Machine ===
The [[Teleportation Machine]] is a device used to facilitate long-distance teleportation by Council members, controlled through enchanted obelisks and powered through crystalline catalysts. A single operable [[Teleportation Machine]] can be found in the [[Windy View Cave]], although a dilapidated setup is still present on [[Anagoge Island]].
