== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Tailoring''' is the craft that turns raw textiles into cloth equipment. You can also give certain enhancements to cloth and leather gear, such as pockets (extra inventory space) and elemental damages.

=== In-Game Description ===
Tailoring has two aspects: first, you use it to turn textiles into clothing. Second, you can use tailoring to make clothing and leather armor more comfortable, more useful, or more stylish.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* [[Thimble Pete]] will teach you Tailoring
* [[Legs]] will teach Tailoring recipes to players in animal form for Spider Casings. She also teaches textile recipes for various spider silk.
* [[Shirogin]] will unlock levels 51-60 and levels 61-70 and teaches 51+ Tailoring recipes.
* [[Elaina]] can also unlock levels 51-60 and 61-70, as well as teach many Tailoring recipes.
* [[Gurki]] can teach you how to make camo clothing and add pockets to clothes.
* [[Eveline Rastin]] can teach you how to make nimble clothing.
* [[Felmer]] will unlock levels 71-80 and teaches 80+ Tailoring recipes.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Jerasen]] in Statehelm will unlock levels 91-100 and teaches 90+ Tailoring recipes.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Ending up with extra textiles ===
Because multiple Textile Creation recipes create and use odd amounts of fabric (Cotton Thread creates 4 every time, but most recipes using it only require 1), you'll often end up with extra materials while Tailoring. For most equipment recipes, the magic number is 8: every 8 pieces of gear, you'll have no materials left.
