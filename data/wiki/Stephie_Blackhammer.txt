[[Stephie Blackhammer]] is a dwarf working as a blacksmithing assistant to her uncle, [[Tremmond Blackhammer]].
== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Stephie Blackhammer]] can be found outside her uncle's shop in the Old City district of Povus.

== Favor ==
[[Stephie Blackhammer]] does not use the favor system.

== Services == 
==== Quests ====
