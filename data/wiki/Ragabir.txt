[[Ragabir]] is a goblin vendor in a side room of the arena area within the Red Wing Casino. He also teaches some Alchemy recipes. He requires 30 [[Goblinese]] to understand.
== Location ==
; [[Red Wing Casino]]
: [[Ragabir]] is a room on the southside of the Arena area  
== Shopkeeper ==
[[Ragabir]] sells some Extreme armor, health and power potions.
