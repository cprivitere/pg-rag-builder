==Summary==
Poe the Myconian asked you to retrieve some ectoplasm. It can often be found on ghosts.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Poe]]''' in '''[[Myconian Caverns]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|My special art requires a very uncommon ingredient. In fact, it is so rare that I have never obtained it directly myself; I have only ever received it from others. Can you fulfill this role now? I require ectoplasm. It is the give-off of ghosts, banshees, wailing spirits, and other incorporeal former-meatlings.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Ectoplasm}} x3
* Talk to Poe
