'''Malgath''' is the [[Weapon Augmentation]] trainer currently hiding out in [[Wolf Cave]]. After the [[Shadow Keepers]] began hunting for [[Nightshade]]'s guild, Malgath fled to the Kur Mountains. While out hunting one day, he became infected with Lycanthropy. To protect his friends, he retreated into the [[Wolf Cave]]. He's searching for a cure, and for some way to fight off his bestial urges. 

In the past, Malgath sold weapons door-to-door.

==Location==
; [[Wolf Cave]]
: On the leftmost path, with many enemies along the way.
