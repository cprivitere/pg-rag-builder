[[Kalaba]] is a [[Rakshasa]] Blacksmith from the city of [[Povus]]. He is not particularly fond of dealing with unfamiliar faces, and will not offer merchant related services to anyone who has not proved themselves worthy of his sturdy equipment. Once a few favors are completed ([[Kill Panthers]] and [[Kill Fey Panthers]]), the services he offers will open. Kalbala will be willing to purchase weapons and armor, and begins selling armor. The prices he offers are generally better than what Yetta will pay for the same item. He will also offer a conversation to teach [[Transmutation]] at this time.
== Location ==
; [[Eltibule]]
: In the Eltibule Keep courtyard (the castle in the center of the zone).
== Shopkeeper ==
Kalaba sells a set of "sturdy" weapons and armor, which appears to be between "basic" available from [[Joeh]], and "quality" available from [[Helena Veilmoor]].

==== Interpretive Dance Variant ====
[[Kalaba]] is the teacher for [[Interpretive Dance|Dance 2 (variant C)]]. At least three musicians must be playing instruments near [[Kalaba]] to convince them to demonstrate their dance.

== Services ==
==== Consignment ====
; Kalaba offers [[Consignment]] at {{Favor|Close Friends}}.
: Mark-up fee of 10%, stocking fee of 50 Councils.
: Max 3 active consignments (Equipment).
: Max 5 consignments per week.

==== Augmentation ====
[[Kalaba]] can install Level 21-40 Augments for 250 Councils.
