==Summary==
There's an undecorated tree in the New Prestonbule cave. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Rick Snapley]]''' in '''[[New Prestonbule Cave]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Get Red Gems for the Tree
* Get Stencils for the Tree
* Get Potions for the Tree
* Get Tea Lights for the Tree
* Talk to [[Rick Snapley]]
