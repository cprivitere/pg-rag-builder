==Summary==
Torana said, "BACON! Who doesn't like bacon? Well, maybe those crazy vegans don't. If I can't have bacon while we're out on the road, I can at least take the smell of it with me! I'm still waiting on my stipend from Orran to go buy supplies to make my own. Could you craft and bring me 10 [[Nice Bacon Candle]]s? I'll trade some of my knowledge for it!"

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Torana]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Candle Making]] level 50. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event. 

===Preface===
Torana said, "BACON! Who doesn't like bacon? Well, maybe those crazy vegans don't. If I can't have bacon while we're out on the road, I can at least take the smell of it with me! ===Requirements===
* Make 10 Nice Bacon Candles
* Possess 10 Nice Bacon Candles
* Talk to Torana
