==Summary==
Gasu'um in New Prestonbule said, "To repair my necklace, I need pyrite. Obtain for me the finest pyrite! I will reward you."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Gasu'um]]''' in '''[[New Prestonbule Cave]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Sentimental Jewelry]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|To repair my necklace, I need pyrite. Obtain for me the finest pyrite! I will reward you.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Pyrite}} Ore x5
* Talk to Gasu'um
