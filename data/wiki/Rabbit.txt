== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Rabbit''' is a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]] with little combat power. Rabbits are stealthy, and their abilities often make them undetected.

The Bun-Fu Training special weekend event provides a quest line yielding rabbit experience and several Bunny and Un-Bunny juices. It occurs once a year.

[[The Errana Bunny's Egg Hunt]] event occurs yearly around Easter, allowing players to acquire several Bunny and Un-bunny juice and find eggs for an extra reward. 

[[Cinnamon the rabbit]] in [[Sun Vale]] and [[Riston]] in the [[Red Wing Casino]] sell Bunny and Un-Bunny juices year round for Live Event Credits. 

[[Zazzamath]] will turn the player into a bunny on speaking with them if the player has no combat skills at 80+.

=== In-Game Description ===
The art of living and fighting as a rabbit or hare. Note: rabbits are not known for their ferocity, so sentient rabbits should carefully consider their choice of secondary skill.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Cinnamon the rabbit]] in [[Sun Vale]] unlocks levels 51-60 and levels 61-70.
* [[Noita the Green]] in the [[Fae Realm]] unlocks 71-80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Big Footed Jim]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Fuzzlebun]] in the [[Statehelm Sewers]] unlocks 81-90, 91-100, and [[Prodigy Potential]].

== Trivia ==
The Rabbit skill was introduced in June 2017

The '''Rabbit''' skill was first obtained by completing Therese's quest during the special weekend event on 10th June 2017. The reward for that quest was a [[Bunny Juice]] and an [[Un-Bunny Juice]]. After steam release these item's were raffled multiple times by guides in game. 

The Bun-Fu Training special weekend event was first added in early November 2019 and now occurs yearly. 

Rabbits are small enough to use the Fae Realm to Sun Vale portal.
