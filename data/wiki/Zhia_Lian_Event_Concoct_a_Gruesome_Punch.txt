==Summary==
<i>Zhia Lian:</i> You should understand your enemy better. I think you should drink from the foul undead concoction known as the Gruesome Spooky Punch. Normally only an alchemist could make the stuff, but if you collect the ingredients, I'll mix you up a batch... plus you'll get the recipe, so you can make more later.

This Quest can be repeated after 60 days.
