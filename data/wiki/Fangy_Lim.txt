[['Fangy' Lim]] was the last mayor of [[Povus]] until his death, after serving for 20 years. He is responsible for giving the name [[Tentakiller]] to the offspring of the dwvon. While his spirt remains tethered to Povus as a ghost, it will only attack if sufficiently angered. During battle, one to three [[Loyal Patron]] will appear to attack alongside him.
{{Spoiler|Spawning 'Fangy' Lim|
Six characters must knock on his gravestone at once. (During Zhia Lian event)}}
