===Preface===
<b>Hunting: Kuvou</b>
Kuvou -- supposedly named after the sound they make while belching -- came with the first rakshasa settlers, who even today use them as a food source. Their vomit is also prized. Unfortunately they lack the docility of other herbivores and must be trained from birth in order to be kept as livestock.
