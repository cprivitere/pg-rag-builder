==Summary==
Kib the Unkillable in Red Wing Casino said, "My second-favorite sword needs to be repaired. I've searched the countryside near the casino and found nothing appropriate. I need metal of expert quality! I've seen armor made of amazing metal, so I know it must exist somewhere in this land..."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kib the Unkillable]]''' in '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

}}
