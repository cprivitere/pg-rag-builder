[[Liamu the Watchful]] is a Rakshasa scholar currently studying the Manticores passing through [[Vidaria]]. 
== Location ==
; [[Vidaria]]
: [[Liamu the Watchful]] can be found at a small tented campsite on a slope in northeastern Vidaria, between the Rubywall and the lower farmland.
