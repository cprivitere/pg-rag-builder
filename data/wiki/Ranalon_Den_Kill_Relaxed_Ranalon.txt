==Summary==
Chirrra in  the Serbule Hills Ranalon Den said, "Why don't you assassinate the ranalon that are off duty? They'll be in the lounge and resting areas. And they definitely won't be expecting you!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chirrra]]''' in '''[[Ranalon Den]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

This is a solo quest from Chirrra. You may have one solo quest from Chirrra at a time. You can take on a new solo quest 15 minutes after completing or canceling this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Why don't you assassinate the ranalon that are off duty? They'll be in the lounge and resting areas. And they definitely won't be expecting you!}}

This is a [[:Category:Quests/Chirrra Tier 1|Tier 1]] quest from Chirrra.

===Requirements===
* Kill Relaxed Ranalon
* Talk to Chirrra
