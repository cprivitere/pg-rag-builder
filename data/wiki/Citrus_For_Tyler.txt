==Summary==
Tyler Green asked you to get him some oranges or lemons so his teeth don't all fall out.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Tyler Green]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
