==Summary==
Torana said, "I need to figure out how to get these fools more into nature. I'm still waiting on my stipend from Orran to go buy supplies. Could you craft and bring me 10 [[Rough Woodland Candle]]s? I'll trade some of my knowledge for it!"

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Torana]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Candle Making]] level 16. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event. 

===Preface===
I need to figure out how to get these fools more into nature. I'm still waiting on my stipend from Orran to go buy supplies. Could you craft and bring me 10 [[Rough Woodland Candle]]s? I'll trade some of my knowledge for it!

===Requirements===
* Make 10 Rough Woodland Candles
* Possess 10 Rough Woodland Candles
* Talk to Torana
