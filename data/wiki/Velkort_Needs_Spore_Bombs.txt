==Summary==
Velkort in Serbule wants you to obtain some Basic Spore Bombs for him. They can be made with a certain Mycology crafting recipe.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Velkort]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|In addition to being an expert fire mage, I'm also quite the alchemist. Mycology, however, is beneath me. Mushrooms are ... dirty. But I have need of some simple explosive mushroom bombs. Perhaps you know how to make those?}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Basic Spore Bomb}}s x5
* Talk to Velkort
