{{Quote|
This golem provides a service for Alpha players: if one or more of your items has become Legacy due to gameplay changes, the golem can replace it with a new randomly-generated item.

The new item will be the same model and level as the Legacy item, and will have the same rarity, but it will have entirely randomly-generated treasure effects.

Other features of the old item, such as augments, attunement, inscription, and crafting bonus effects, will not be replaced.
}}

The '''Legacy Item Replacer'' is an automaton that can either replace a Legacy item, or inform you why the item was marked Legacy.
