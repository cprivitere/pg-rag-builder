==Summary==
Merriana in Serbule Hills said, "Somewhere south of here, nearish Serbule Keep, is a massive door. This traditional showy dwarf door leads to the long-dead city of Borghild. No dwarves live there, but it's infested with undead.

You will ... How do I put this politely? You are quite formidable, but you would be wise to bring some allies with you into Borghild. It is full of traps.

Do this for me and I shall bequeath upon you one of my grandest weather wands."
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Merriana]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Tolerated}} favor. The quest [[Under The Hand Is An Orb]] must be completed first.
