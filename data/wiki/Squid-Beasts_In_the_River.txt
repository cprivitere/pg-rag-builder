==Summary==
Dog Ear in Povus said, "Since you're a Warden, maybe you can help me fix an imbalance. Ever since the rakshasa fishermen stopped coming to the river, the squid-beasts are getting out of control. They're even climbing out onto land now. They attack anything that comes near! It's making it hard for animals to get water. Can you remove some of the squids?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Dog Ear]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. This quest is only available after an interaction. 

===Preface===
