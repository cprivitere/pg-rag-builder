==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] sent me to find a Tuvillic Rune in the walls of [[Eltibule Keep]] and retrieve a stored ointment. Chain C is independent of Chains A and B and can be started as soon as you receive Vampirism.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[The Stolen Lens]] and receiving the Vampirism skill from [[Truxartis]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|While I am still... more powerful than you could ever dream to be, I... have been robbed of much of my... power. But do not worry. You shall help me recover.

Not too long ago I had a thrall hide supplies in an abandoned keep in Eltibule. Find a way into the walls and find a rune of Tuvillus.
Defeat the primitive guardian and... bring me the ointment it guards. Hurry.}}
