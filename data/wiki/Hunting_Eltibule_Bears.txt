==Summary==
You're killing bears in Eltibule.

This Quest can be repeated after 0 hours.

===Preface===
<b>Hunting: Eltibule Bears</b>
The bears in Eltibule are odd. First, some of them are green. Second, they don't seem to act like bears should. They tend to gather in large aimless groups. It's not clear why they do this, but it's clearly not good.

===Requirements===
* Kill Bears in Eltibule x10
