==Summary==
Uriana in the Fae Realm said, "Pixies hunt; fairies hunt; panthers hunt! But one is not worthy! NOT WORTHY! They are not like us! They seethe with mortality! Can you not smell the mortality of the panthers?! Show them their mortality! SHOW THEM!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Uriana]] in the [[Fae Realm]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.
