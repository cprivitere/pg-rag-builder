==Summary==
Kinira said, "Hello! We have been on the road for quite some time, so rations and cooking supplies got thin. If these guys have to eat beets again, they might riot! They need meat! Fish! I hear the Fae have a great recipe for vexfish! Bring me 20 [[Vexfish Lettuce Wraps]], and maybe I can help you out somehow."

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kinira]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Cooking]] level 70. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Hello! We have been on the road for quite some time, so rations and cooking supplies got thin. If these guys have to eat beets again, they might riot! They need meat! Fish! I hear the Fae have a great recipe for vexfish! Bring me 20 [[Vexfish Lettuce Wraps]], and maybe I can help you out somehow.

===Requirements===
* Prepare 10 Vexfish Lettuce Wraps
* Obtain 20 Vexfish Lettuce Wraps
* Talk to Kinira
