==Summary==
Bring 10 mushroom to Mushroom Jack.

==Prerequisites==
To start this quest, talk to [[Mushroom Jack]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

==Preface==
{{Quote|

;:You're trying my patience.

==Requirements==
*Collect 10 {{ItemCategory|Mushroom}} (of any kind) {{Hint|Often, there are mushrooms right around his shack. Otherwise, try in the Serbule Sewers, or far east Serbule, near where you arrived from Anagoge Island.}}
*Give mushrooms to [[Mushroom Jack]].
