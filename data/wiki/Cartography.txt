== Cartography Overview==
[[Cartography]] is a useful skill that quantifies your exploration of zones and dungeons. Leveling cartography will give you many more inventory slots. 

=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in accurately mapping your surroundings. You earn Cartography XP by clearing the 'fog' from game maps, which happens automatically as you walk around.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* A character automatically earns the [[Cartography]] skill as they begin walking around whichever map they first spawned in. Each zone and dungeon provides cartography xp from exploration.
