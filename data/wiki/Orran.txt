[[Orran]] is the Leader of the [[Crafting Caravan]]. [[Orran]] offers barter options for{{Item|Crafter's Guild Token}}.
== Location ==
; [[Red Wing Casino]]
: [[Orran]] can be encountered during the [[Crafting Caravan]] event.

==Bartering==
The player may barter a number of times per period, depending on favor: once at '''{{Favor|Comfortable}}''', twice at '''{{Favor|Friends}}''', three times at '''{{Favor|Like Family}}''', and four at '''{{Favor|Soul Mates}}'''.

Additionally, those who reach '''{{favor|Soul Mates}}''' with [[Orran]] will be given permission to barter for a [[Cosmetic Pet]].
