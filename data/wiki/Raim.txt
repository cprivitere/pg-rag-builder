[[Raim]] is the Tailor of the [[Crafting Caravan]]. The repeatable quests [[Raim]] offers give {{Item|Crafter's Guild Token}} as rewards. These tokens are bartered with [[Orran]] during the event.
== Location ==
; [[Red Wing Casino]]
: [[Raim]] can be encountered during the [[Crafting Caravan]] event.

==Special Bartering==
After completing [[Event: Bonus - Raim Needs String]], the player can give Raim an exceptional (red) or better {{Item|Windstep Shoes}} and receive {{Item|Raim's Advanced Crafting Token}}. The player may then barter this advanced crafting token along with a number of {{Item|Crafter's Guild Token}} to receive a higher level {{Item|Enhanced Windstep Shoes}}.  The number of Crafter's Guild Tokens that must be given is equal to half the level of the new Windstep Shoes (30 tokens for level 60, 35 for level 70, or 40 for level 80).  The new windstep shoes received can be of different rarity from the one given to Raim.
