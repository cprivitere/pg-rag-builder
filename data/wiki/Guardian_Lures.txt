==Summary==
Trasen in Serbule Hills said, "There are several types of ranalon around here. The large ones with the dangling orb on their heads are my favorite. I would love to see how their glow balls work. Well, if you happen to find any, please show them to me!"
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Trasen]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Tolerated}} favor.

===Preface===
There are several types of ranalon around here. The large ones with the dangling orb on their heads are my favorite. I would love to see how their glow balls work. Well, if you happen to find any, please show them to me!
