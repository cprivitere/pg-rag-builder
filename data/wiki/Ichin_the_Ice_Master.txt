[[Ichin the Ice Master]] is a prestigious Rakshasa merchant, chef, and respected Ice Mage currently working in the city of Rahu. He offers training of recipes and skill cap unlocks to trainees of [[Ice Magic]], and sells his shaved ice creations. '''Ichin''' is also willing to teach adventurers adept at Ice Magic the [[Ice Conjuration]] skill, but not all of his secrets are fit for humanoids.
