== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Goblinese''' is the language spoken by [[Goblin|goblins]]. The language lets you speak to [[Goblin#Friends|friendly goblins]], some of which train abilities and even teach new skills! Fluency in [[Goblinese]] level (35) is all that is required to talk to all goblins except [[Glajur]] level (40) though they may have other requirements to learn from them. For example, [[Urzab]] requires level (50) in order to access training and gifting.


=== In-Game Description ===
Fluency in the language of the [[Goblin|goblins]].


=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Cleo Conyer]] in [[Serbule Hills]] rewards 500 Goblinese XP for the favor [[Cleo's Lost Books]].
* [[Goblin|Goblins]] in [[Goblin Dungeon]] or [[Serbule Hills]] can drop cards with a level requirement of zero. These can teach '''Goblinese'''
* [[Serbule Hills]] has 3 large concentrations of goblins - Starting in the North-East with the Lumbermill, then if you follow the Eastern Zone wall down there is another large concentration at the Farm, and then another in the South-East around Catapults.   
* Occasionally you can find the cards in [[Yetta|Yetta's]] used tab.
* [[Kremmu the Warden]] often drops a level 0 card.
* [[Algapa]] always drop a level 25 card named after him: '''Algapa's Calling Card'''. 
* [[Fugorg]] is another named goblin who drops a level 25 card named after him, similarly to [[Algapa]]. There are a few goblin archers so this may be a worthwhile location to "camp" if you can not handle Algapa's room yet.
* All goblins in [[Boarded up Basement]] have a chance to drop cards, however it can be hard to solo due to high respawn rate and densely populated corridors.
* Drinking '''Decoction of Goblinese Knowledge'''. "Requires Goblinese Level 1. Gain 250 Goblinese XP."

When you are getting started it is recommended to keep in your inventory the level 1,2,3,4 cards so that you can gain several levels as soon as you find the required level 0 card. From there on, if storage is an issue one solution is to drop the cards in a chosen area. They tend to stay on the ground for some time, and once you are in the level 20 range, you will often find cards left behind by other players that you can just pickup and read.
For each level in Goblinese, there seem to be 2 cards of that level. For example: for level 37 there seem to be the Lorga's Calling Card and Larto's Calling Card. For level 38 it seems to be Akogi's Calling Card and Rofok's Calling Card. <br>
For learning each card for the first time will give 50 bonus XP. <br>
Repeatedly reading the same card (if you find it multiple times) only offers very little XP. Cards of level 1 thru 10 only offer 1 XP for repeat-reads. Cards between 11 and 20 offer 2 XP for repeat-reads. And level 21-30 cards seem to offer 3 XP.
