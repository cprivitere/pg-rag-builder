==Patch Notes: April 15, 2025==
This is a bug-fix update. 

:- Fixed pathing issues in the following areas: Serbule boats, Elven Judgment (forest areas), Boss Gauntlet 2, Caves Under Sun Vale, Yeti Cave, Rahu Sewers
:- Added two new repeatable quests to Orrrilund. 
:- Fix for one type of "entity no longer exists" errors
:- Fix bug that caused loading to fail with an error message "Loading failed, continuing anyway"
:- Fix beds: you can successfully sleep in them again!
:- Sir Taggart has had enough alone time, and will talk to players again
:- add basic sound effects to some monsters that were previously eerily silent (work in progress; some types of monsters are still silent)
:- NPCs now sheathe & unsheathe their weapons properly
:- Fix some mislocated weapon sheath positions
:- Fix bug related to sheathing while dead
:- Fix bug causing color tags to sometimes show up in title unlock announcements
:- Fix bug with Work Order for Lumberjack's Arrangement #7 through #9, they were expecting Forager's Arrangements
:- enemy Ballistae reload faster (10 seconds instead of 20 seconds)
:- Fix bug causing some pets to stop attacking after the first few attacks
:- Fix bug where gather-channeling and item-use-channeling would often fail
:- Animal Handling pets match the owner's speed much more reliably
:- Animal Handlings' Sic 'Em and Clever Trick: the pet now favors your target over its own current target (only using its original target if your target is invalid). If neither target is valid it chooses a target that is attacking you.
:- Animal Handling pets' loyalty level enhances the hate instructions implied by Sic 'Em and Clever Trick (increasing the chance that they will follow the owner's requested target even when they're being assaulted by other enemies)
:- Potential fix for bug causing mounts to freeze up after zoning sometimes (we can't reproduce this, so the fix is experimental)
:- Improve pathing around Mythander in Eltibule
:- Improve pathing for spiders in Myconian Caverns
:- Water in Myconian Caverns now actually counts as water
:- Fix elementals' walking animation
