==Summary==
Tyler Green asked you to bring him some Simple Metal Slabs, which are most easily obtained via Surveying. He intimated that Jaime Fatholm in Serbule Hills might know how to survey ores.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Tyler Green]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
