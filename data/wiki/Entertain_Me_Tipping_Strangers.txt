__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Pennoc in Serbule told you to tip a bunch of other players with the /tip command. You should tip players at least 10 coins.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pennoc]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|What we need to cure boredom is culture! And culture demands patrons. So help me foster some culture -- go tip people! Give them at least 10 Councils apiece -- nobody likes a cheapskate.

<i>[ Use the /tip command in the chat window to tip players. For instance, if a player was named Bob, you could type "/tip Bob 10" ]</i>}}

===Requirements===
* Tip Players
* Talk to Pennoc
