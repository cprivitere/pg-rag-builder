==Summary==
[[Floxie]] in the [[Fae Realm]] said, "Oh hey, I had another botany idea. No, this one's great! See, you got me [[Carnivine Tooth|carnivine teeth]], but I have nothing really to compare them to. Why don't you ALSO get me some spriggan weed teeth? Then I can compare them! Go, go! There's science to do!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Floxie]]''' in '''[[Fae Realm]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. You must have previously completed [[Carnivine Teeth]] in order to undertake this quest. (Requires 25 [[Fae (Race_Skill)]] as a Fae)

===Preface===
Oh hey, I had another botany idea. No, this one's great! See, you got me carnivine teeth, but I have nothing really to compare them to. Why don't you ALSO get me some spriggan weed teeth? Then I can compare them! Go, go! There's science to do!
