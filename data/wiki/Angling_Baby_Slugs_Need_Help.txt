==Summary==
While you were angling at Bonnie's Hot Spring in the Ilmari desert, a psychic slug asked you to catch a particularly cruel Dorado. It only comes out at night.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, angle by Bonnie's Hot Spring (next to '''[[Arlan]]''') in '''[[Ilmari Desert]]'''.  {{Hint|Be on the lookout for the Bewildebeest elite mob that patrols the springs area at nighttime.}}
