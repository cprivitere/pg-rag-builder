{{Quote|source=[[Engineer Crall]]|
The temperature is low in this part of the mountains, unnaturally so. Even ''I'' need extra insulation to withstand it.}}
[[Engineer Crall]] is an orc living in [[Vidaria]]. She used to be part of the local mining camp, before setting off on her own to study the local automatons. Level 25 [[Orcish]] to is required to speak with her.
== Location ==
; [[Vidaria]]
: Just north of a [[Meditation Pillar]] in the southeastern mountains.
