==Summary==
[[Kleave]] in [[Eltibule]] said, "We need colorful and shiny decorations. After consulting with [[Kalaba]], I have decided that the best colorful and shiny decorations would be the eyes of fallen foes. However, we should not use the eyes of sentient creatures. Hogan said that the tree should radiate peace. So animal eyes will have to do. Perhaps cat or dinosaur eyes."

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kleave]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''.

===Preface===
We need colorful and shiny decorations. After consulting with Kalaba, I have decided that the best colorful and shiny decorations would be the eyes of fallen foes. However, we should not use the eyes of sentient creatures. Hogan said that the tree should radiate peace. So animal eyes will have to do. Perhaps cat or dinosaur eyes.
