== Services ==
==== Stable Master====
[[Pitre Ferrence]] offers access to manage stable slots.

By coordinating with a colleague in Statehelm, Pitre will offer animal breeding with double the usual mutation rate. Costs 2 Vidaria Renown per breeding, in addition to the usual costs.
