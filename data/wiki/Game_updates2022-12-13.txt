==Update Notes: December 13, 2022==
This is a minor update to address some issues with movement in [[Serbule Hills]]. Various other fixes happened to be ready, so we threw 'em in.

:- [[Serbule Hills]] navigation now uses heightmaps for better accuracy (similar to other areas). We believe this will address issues with players sometimes falling through the ground, and monsters sometimes being unable to move. We'll continue to monitor the issue in case this fix is insufficient.
:- Fix bug that prevented crafting multiples of most recipes
:- Added nonfiction book support for [[Shamanic Infusion]], [[Foraging]], [[Industry]]
:- Dramatically lowered the amount of XP earned by writing nonfiction books (XP was artificially inflated to help jumpstart the skill, but it's good now)
:- [[Cow]] armor should now be visible
:- Improve visuals for Fire Breath, Fireball, & Warmthball
:- Tweak "Explosion" particles which are used by several abilities, namely Controlled Burn, Defensive Burst, & Ring of Fire 
:- Tweaked Lamia appearance
:- Improve animation responsiveness on a bunch of attacks; now all attacks should fire off within a half second of pressing the button
:- Speed up several animations to ensure they are shorter than the global cooldown
:- The buff from the mod "Discharging Strike and Latent Charge boost your Epic Attack damage +X for 15 seconds" now actually lasts 15 seconds
:- Fix bug that would sometimes freeze the Combat Refresh animation over an icon
:- Deployed Amazing candles will now be called Amazing instead of Great
:- All world bosses that were not immune to knockback are now immune to knockback
