==Summary==
Leah Bowman in Povus said, "I don't get much work in animal husbandry any more, but I got an order from an old customer, and I would love to fill it! But what he needs is an aphrodesiac for his pet bees! Of all the weird pets, bees are way up there. I do know how to make what he needs... I just need some high-quality tallow. Could I ask you to render some for me?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Leah Bowman]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''.
