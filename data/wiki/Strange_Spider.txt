The [[Strange Spider]] is a diplomat of the goddess of spiders, [[Akhisa]].  This spider was captured and imprisoned by [[Khyrulek]] for reasons unknown. In gratitude for partially freeing them, the [[Strange Spider]] will sell Spider Juice and Un-Spider juice, for access to the [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]] of [[Spider]].  The [[Strange Spider]] also sells the {{belt|Belt of the Arachnid}}.
