==Summary==
Moxie said, "Hi There! We're fairly new to the caravan! So you like making jewelry too? Let's swap some ideas! Bring me 5 Nice Magic Rings and 5 Nice Enchanted Necklaces. Once I can go over your stuff, I'll share some of what I know too!"

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Moxie]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Jewelry Crafting]] level 46. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Hi There! We're fairly new to the caravan! So you like making jewelry too? Let's swap some ideas! Bring me 5 Nice Magic Rings and 5 Nice Enchanted Necklaces. Once I can go over your stuff, I'll share some of what I know too!

===Requirements===
* Make 5 Nice Magic Rings
* Obtain 5 Nice Magic Rings
* Make 5 Nice Enchanted Necklaces
* Obtain 5 Nice Enchanted Necklaces
* Talk to Moxie
