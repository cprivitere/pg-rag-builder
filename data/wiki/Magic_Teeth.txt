==Summary==
Sanja in the Kur Mountains asked you to find magic teeth from werewolves. They apparently are rather rare.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Sanja]]''' in '''[[Kur Mountains]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Werewolves who never leave wolf form eventually start leaking magic into their bones and organs. It's happened to me, and I quite like the feeling, but I wish to study the phenomenon more... without dissecting myself. See if you can find werewolves whose magic has bled into their teeth. I doubt they'll be too common, but please look around.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain 2x {{Item|Magic Tooth}}
* Talk to Sanja
