[[Sir Brooker]] is an established warrior who works for the Council. He regularly is called upon to train new recruits in the armies. 
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Sir Brooker]] can be found in the Council army training ground during the day. At night, he retreats to a room in the southwest corner of the second floor of the barracks.
