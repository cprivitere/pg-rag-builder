== Services ==
==== Bat Reduction =====
[[Duke of Flapbule]] sells a {{Item|Bat Reduction Talisman}} for 1000 Councils. All sentient [[Giant Bat]]s will want one of these, as it reduces their terrifyingness. This Level 75 Giant Bat racial item allows Giant Bats to speak to most NPCs in [[Statehelm]].

Eeeeh!
