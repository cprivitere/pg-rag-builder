==Summary==
You're killing fire sheep in Serbule Hills.

This Quest can be repeated after 0 hours.



===Preface===
<b>Hunting: Fire Sheep</b>
Fire sheep were not created by a deranged wizard, as is often suggested. Instead, they are an exciting step forward in the natural evolution of the sheep. These creatures are very fragile but are much safer than their non-igniting brethren because local predators have learned to run away from the smell of smouldering wool.
