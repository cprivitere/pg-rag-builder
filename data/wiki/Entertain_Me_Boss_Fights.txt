==Summary==
Pennoc in Serbule told you to kill Elite or Boss creatures. These are often found deep in dungeons.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pennoc]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Entertain Me: Bursts of Death]] in order to undertake this quest.
