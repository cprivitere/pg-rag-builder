==Summary==
Braigon wants a perfect cow hide for an experimental cheese he's making.

(Perfect Cow Hides come from expert skinners who have mastered bovine anatomy. They're quite rare!)

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Braigon]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I have this crazy recipe for cheese that ripens within the hide of a cow. Crazy gimmick, right?! Trouble is, it requires a perfectly-skinned cow hide. I'm a pretty terrible skinner, so that's not going to happen.

But if you happen upon one, save it for me!}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain a {{Item|Trophy Cow Hide}}
