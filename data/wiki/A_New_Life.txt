* [[Midge the Apothecary#Fairy Quests|Complete a Quests]] for [[Midge]] by collecting [[Parasol Mushroom|Parasol Mushrooms]] x 6, making [[Parasol Mushroom Flakes]] x 3, and then making a [[Pick-Me-Up Juice]].
* Help the Local Hive by completing the quests [[Meet the Hive]], [[Pollinate]], and [[Help the Hive]].
* [[Felmer#Fairy Quests|Complete quests]] for [[Felmer]] to gain access to the [[Sun Vale]] shortcut portal in the [[Fae Realm]]. In particular, completion of the quest [[Fashion Kittens]] is required. 
* Learn Fishing from Squidlips in Sun Vale
* Learn Skinning from Silvia in Sun Vale
* Learn Butchering from Silvia in Sun Vale
* Learn Pathology from Preta in Sun Vale
* Learn Foraging from Lisi in Sun Vale
* Learn Nature Appreciation from Rohina in Sun Vale
* Do Crelpin a Favor
