__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Lakrea told you to make your own cheese. She suggested avoiding the fancier cheese recipes that require aging.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lakrea]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|You need a hobby. Why not cheese making? It's delicious and might even make you some money. Of course, the fanciest kinds have to be aged in a cave for a long time, but you should start with one of the easy cheeses that don't take so long.}}

===Requirements===
* Make Cheese
* Talk to Lakrea

Butter and sour cream do not count as a cheese.
