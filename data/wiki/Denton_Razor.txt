{{Quote|source=[[Denton Razor]]|You know, I don't want to divulge too much. You never know who is listening.}}
[[Denton Razor]] is an infamous human bandit working in the Vidaria region. He currently maintains a campsite in the eastern part of the zone, near the primary road. He trains Bowyery and unlocks 81-90 Archery.

; [[Vidaria]]
: Merry Bandit Camp in the east.

== Conversations == 
{{Quote|source=[[Denton Razor]] (when questing for [[Elune]])|
What brings you here?

'''I'm here on behalf of Elune in Statehelm. Apparently you stole from her.'''

I did no such thing! My men did that. Under my guidance and tutelage, yes. But me personally? I haven't stepped foot in Statehelm in weeks!

'''Okay... what would it take for you to give her jewelry back?'''

I don't have it piled in a big hoard here in camp! Not anymore at least. Tell the rich old elf that her heaps of wealth have been distributed far and wide already. And a significant percentage of that wealth was given to the needy, might I add.

'''I see. Is there no way she can get ANY of her jewelry back?'''

Not a lot of people are brave enough to just come into my camp and be a dick to me. I like you. You're an idiot, but a likeable idiot.

I tell you what. Maybe we can work a deal. I have a man sick with Hellfire. Get me some hellfreeze, and I will look around for those baubles.

'''Sigh... I'll see what I can do.''' (Starts Quest)}}
