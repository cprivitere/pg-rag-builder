{{Quote|I am a visiting diplomat from the Grand City of Insects. I should explain that it is not actually a grand city yet. However, we have grand plans, so it made sense to choose a name that we could grow into.}}
[[Yavazek]] is a Mantis diplomat sent to Animal Town to help the animals survive. He says he's an ambassador between insects and talking animals, but something is fishy about his story.

As a resident of [[Animal Town]], [[Yavazek]] is only willing to hold conversations with characters that have transformed into a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]] over 180 real-world minutes prior to the conversation attempt. If you loose track of the time you last transformed, [[Norbert]] is willing to tell you how long you have until the other residents will tolerate talking with you. [[Yavazek]] believes this policy is pointless, but follows it to stay on [[Spot]]'s good side.
==Location==
; [[Sun Vale]]
: Animal Town (located in the southeastern part of [[Sun Vale]], at the mountainous area.)<br/>Can be found in the middle of the town.
== Shopkeeper ==
[[Yavazek]] specializes in selling First Aid kits and Armor Patching kits, as well as parchments and ink.
