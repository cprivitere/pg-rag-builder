==Summary==
Verac said, "Ho there! Making tack for horses is very important work! I'm in need of some extra gear while I'm waiting on Tantai to replenish my leather stock. Can you bring me 10 [[Quality Saddlebag]]s? In exchange I'll share a couple trade secrets with you to help hone your craft.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Verac]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Saddlery]] level 54. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Ho there! Making tack for horses is very important work! I'm in need of some extra gear while I'm waiting on Tantai to replenish my leather stock. Can you bring me 10 [[Quality Saddlebag]]s? In exchange I'll share a couple trade secrets with you to help hone your craft.

===Requirements===
* Craft 10 Quality Saddlebags
* Posses 10 Quality Saddlebags
* Talk to Verac
