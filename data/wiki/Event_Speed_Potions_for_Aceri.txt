__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Aceri said, "I could use your assistance! I hear there is a speed potion that a lot of folks craft around here to get around easier. Could you get me 10 of those [[Agonizing Speed Potion]]s? I need to reverse engineer them so I can make them for the whole group."

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Aceri]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Alchemy]] level 20. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
I could use your assistance! I hear there is a speed potion that a lot of folks craft around here to get around easier. Could you get me 10 of those [[Agonizing Speed Potion]]s? I need to reverse engineer them so I can make them for the whole group.

===Requirements===
* Make 10 Agonizing Speed Potions
* Obtain 10 Agonizing Speed Potions
* Talk to Aceri
