== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Weapon Augmentation''' is an [[Augmentation]] skill that focuses on Weapon Augments, Off-Hand Augments, and Glove Augments.
=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge of the techniques for creating Weapon Augments, Off-Hand Augments, and Glove Augments. (Augments for other types of equipment are covered by other skills).
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* After speaking with [[Nightshade]] in [[Kur Mountains|Kur]] and completing a task for him, '''Weapon Augmentation''' can be learned from [[Malgath]].
* [[Kohan]] and [[Ufkar]] are both able to unlock levels 51-60 and 61-70.
* Levels 71-80 and 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* Levels 81-90 and 91-100 are available to unlock from [[Corinth]].
