==Summary==
Azalak the mantis asked you to create a dish of Venison Psi-Mantis Style. He intends to use it to woo Zeratak.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Azalak]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
