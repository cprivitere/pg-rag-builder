==Summary==
Tavilak in Red Wing Casino said, "The Natural Food Experience ends with a difficult task: growing carrots. This is not a task that the average guest can complete during their stay. As a result, reported guest satisfaction is extraordinarily low. Please grow some carrots and exclaim that you are not perturbed by the slow growing speed of organic matter."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Tavilak]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. You must have previously completed [[The Natural Food Experience, pt 2]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
The Natural Food Experience ends with a difficult task: growing carrots. This is not a task that the average guest can complete during their stay. As a result, reported guest satisfaction is extraordinarily low. Please grow some carrots and exclaim that you are not perturbed by the slow growing speed of organic matter.

===Requirements===
* Grow Carrots x6
* Say aloud 'These carrots grew slowly, and I wouldn't have it any other way. Thanks Red Wing Casino!'
* Talk to Tavilak
