[[Raven Form]] is a [[Curse]] freely usable by [[Druid]]s that have learned the ability. Unlike many other beast forms, Raven Form isn't assumed to have removed your hands and ability to use your equipped weapons.  All normal biped combat skills function as normal.  
== Flight Mechanics ==
Movement is controlled with the same keys as swimming, with R to "rise" and F to "fall."  While in the air and not in combat, power ticks from food are disabled and power is drained slowly.  While in the air and in combat, the power drain is significantly faster.  Though power restoration through food isn't possibly while flying, other forms of power regeneration are allowed.  Normal abilities may be used while airborne without restriction.  This means that abilities that restore power (such as [[Psi Power Wave]]) can extend flight.  With the appropriate equipment mods, out-of-combat flight can be made effectively limitless as long as the appropriate power-restoring abilities are kept active.
