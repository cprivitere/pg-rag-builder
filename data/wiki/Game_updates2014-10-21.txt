==October 21, 2014==

Server changes were made to support the new website. Also, two random bug fixes:

- Winter Court Fey were disappearing too quickly (they are supposed to last ~30 minutes before disappearing, if they haven't been
killed yet)

- NPC [[Nelson Ballard]] claimed to like fancy footwear, of which there was none in the game anymore. Nelson now likes "elegant
footwear", which is any Feet item with the Elegant tag.
