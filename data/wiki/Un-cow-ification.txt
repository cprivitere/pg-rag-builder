[[Harry the Wolf]] in [[Serbule]] says he'll give you some of his precious antidote. But you'll have to pay him handsomely for it!

===Requirements===
* Collect Grass x30
* Obtain Bottles of Milk x5
* Collect Mushrooms x50
* Talk to Harry

* Removes the [[Cow]] curse and turns you back into your previous form. Does not remove other curses or diseases.
