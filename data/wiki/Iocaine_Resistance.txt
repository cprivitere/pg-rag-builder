== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Iocaine Resistance]] appears to be raised with the help of ''Recipe for Iocaine Practice Dose'' which is sold by [[Voo]] in the [[Myconian Cave]] and require friends.
=== In-Game Description ===
Practiced resistance to the deadly mushroom poison known as Iocaine. (Each point of this skill reduces the damage from Iocaine-based poisons by 1%.)
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Learned after eating an {{Item|Iocaine Practice Dose}}.
* Use {{Item|Iocaine Practice Dose}} to gain experience.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Practice Dose Effects ===
Each {{Item|Iocaine Practice Dose}} consumed reduces maximum health by 50 for 5 minutes, and provides 50 XP towards [[Iocaine Resistance]].

Each level in [[Iocaine Resistance]] reduces the damage done by food poisoned with Iocaine (750 direct health damage) by 1%, allowing you to survive if you swallow one.
