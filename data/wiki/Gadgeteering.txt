== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Gadgeteering]] is taught by [[Kohan]] and [[Ufkar]], two Gadgeteers living in the Rakshasan City of [[Rahu]] The two are staunch rivals, and when you raise favor with one, you lose favor with the other.
:Requires Carpentry 25 and Alchemy 25, and use the talk option on either [[Kohan]] or [[Ufkar]].
=== In-Game Description ===
The ability to create elaborate contraptions that automate complex tasks.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Kohan]] Teaches Item Dispenser and Item Vendor Gadgeteering Recipes.
* [[Ufkar]] Teaches Item Raffler Gadgeteering Recipes.
* [[Engineer Crall]] in [[Vidaria]] unlocks levels 51-60.
