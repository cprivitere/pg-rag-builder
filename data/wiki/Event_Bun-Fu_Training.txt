==Summary==
Three Bun-Fu Masters have come into the area to help train their fellow bunnies in the ways of the carrot. Visit Lopsy in the [[Serbule Keep]] garden, Flopsy outside of the [[Kur Mountains]] Inn and Boop outside of the [[New Prestonbule Cave]] in [[Gazluk]] to complete your training! You think you know Bun-Fu? Show me!

[<i> These quests require you to be in [[rabbit|bunny form]] to advance. A free bunny transformation will be available at all quest NPCs. </i>]

This Quest can be repeated after 15 days.

This quest is only available during a BunFu event.
