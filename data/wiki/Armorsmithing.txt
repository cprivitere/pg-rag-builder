== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Armorsmithing''' is the analogue of [[Tailoring]] (cloth) and [[Leatherworking]] (leather), allowing for the production of metal armor.
=== In-Game Description ===
The art of making metal armor.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
Most ordinary [[Armorsmithing]] can be done at any forge. For a list of forge locations, see [[Blacksmithing]]. Advanced [[Armorsmithing]] to make Lycan, Dwarven, or Orcish armor requires special consecrated forges.
=== Consecrated Forges===
High-level [[Armorsmithing]] crafters require forges consecrated to particular gods, if they wish to craft certain styles of armor.
; Consecrated to [[Norala]]
* Found in the [[Wolf Cave]].
; Consecrated to [[Umrad]]
* In the City of [[Povus]], owned by [[Tremmond Blackhammer]].
; Consecrated to [[Tast]]
* Outside the Orc Camp of [[Povus]], northwest from the city.
