'''Treat Disease''' is an ability unlocked by leveling the [[First Aid]] Skill. 

The first three Treat Disease abilities are unlocked by leveling the First Aid skill, and the remaining Treat Disease abilities must be learned from skill trainers.

* [[Treat Disease|Treat Disease 1]] - Automatically Unlocked when level 15 [[First Aid]] skill is reached.
* [[Treat Disease|Treat Disease 2]] - Automatically Unlocked when level 35 [[First Aid]] skill is reached.
* [[Treat Disease|Treat Disease 3]] - Automatically Unlocked when level 50 [[First Aid]] skill is reached.



'''Skill Trainers''' for Treat Disease Include:

* [[Leah Bowman]] - Teaches [[Treat Disease|Treat Disease 4]] when level 65 [[First Aid]] skill is reached. Requires ??? Favor.
