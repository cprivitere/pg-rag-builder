==Summary==
[[Irkima]] in [[Red Wing Casino]] said, "At the Ri-Shin celebration in Statehelm, the trees are lit with fairy lights! Our tree must be lit as well. There are a few fairy shopkeepers in the employ of the casino, and Laphylla says she can place a glamour on our tree... but she demands payment in a substance called 'pixie sugar'. Can you obtain this?"

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Irkima]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.

===Preface===
At the Ri-Shin celebration in Statehelm, the trees are lit with fairy lights! Our tree must be lit as well. There are a few fairy shopkeepers in the employ of the casino, and Laphylla says she can place a glamour on our tree... but she demands payment in a substance called 'pixie sugar'. Can you obtain this?

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Pixie Sugar}} x3
* Talk to [[Irkima]]
