==Summary==
Selaxi in Amulna has asked you to obtain giant hairballs. The most common source is grimalkin in the desert.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Selaxi]]''' in '''[[Ilmari Desert]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
