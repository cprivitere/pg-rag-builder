==Summary==
Bogaku in the Rahu Sewers said, "Ratkin often farm snails for meat and shells, but the ones down here are huge! They must be mutants! Go stab some to death and see if they explode into glowing goo."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Bogaku]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
Ratkin often farm snails for meat and shells, but the ones down here are huge! They must be mutants! Go stab some to death and see if they explode into glowing goo.

===Requirements===
* Kill Farm Snails x5
* Talk to Bogaku
