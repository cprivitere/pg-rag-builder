==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] asked me to loot Fresh Hematic Ichor from creatures and bring it to him. This is a repeatable daily quest offered on Tuesdays and Fridays.

===Prerequisites===
To receive this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[Grasuul the Butler]] and is offered on Tuesdays and Fridays.

===Preface===
{{Quote|My experiments require fresh hematic ichor. It can be hard to find, but just keep drinking people and... you'll find it eventually. Grasuul will know the best places to look today.}}

===Requirements===
* Loot {{Item|Hematic Ichor}} x5 from creatures (must be looted, not purchased)
* Keep the Hematic Ichor in your inventory
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Fresh Hematic Ichor|
{{Quote|Good, it hasn't gotten too... crusty yet.}}

* 50 {{Favor|favor}}
* 10 Blood Oaths
}}

===Notes===
{{Spoiler|Notes|
[[Truxartis]] randomly assigns one daily Blood Oath quest per real-world day. Speak with [[Grasuul]] for guidance on suitable blood targets today.
}}
