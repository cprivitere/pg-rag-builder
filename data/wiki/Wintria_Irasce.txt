{{Quote|source=[[Wintria Irasce]]| I'm visiting from Statehelm to research certain personal projects. This is my first time living away from home, and it's been quite the adventure.}}
[[Wintria Irasce]] is traveling writer and merchant from the city of [[Statehelm]]. In an attempt to temporarily escape from the high expectations of her because of her famous family, she traveled to the Rakshasan [[City of Rahu]].

== Location ==
; [[Rahu]]
: [[Wintria Irasce]] lives alongside [[Hirochi the Booklord]] in a library and bookshop south of the public garden. 
== Shopkeeper ==
[[Wintria Irasce]] sells a selection of introductory non-combat skill books for reading with [[Autodidacticism]]. She also sells some parchment and ink that can be used for creating books with [[Non-Fiction Writing]].

== Conversations == 
{{Quote|source=[[Wintria Irasce]] Talk & Small Talk| 
The weather here is amazing. It's hot, yet the breeze keeps us cool. I will definitely miss this when I return to [[Statehelm]].

...

I'll be staying with Hirochi for a few months while I learn more about Rakshasa history and culture. His books are mostly about war-making... but not all of them.

...

My family is well-known in [[Statehelm]] and I suppose I've taken that for granted my whole life. Coming here has been... eye-opening!

...

New to the city? Here's a tip: when visiting the Tasty Cat, you should avoid the main dish, the House Cat. It's grisly and disappointing. But if you catch [[Nishika]] on a good day she will make her Orange Cat dish, which is far superior.

...

My great-grandmother is a very important woman in [[Statehelm]]. And I'm named after her daughter - my grandmother - who was something of a folk her. So I've always encountered preconceptions about me... until I came to Rahu! Nobody here cares about me a tiny bit! At first that was very upsetting, but now I relish this freedom.

...

The people of Rahu use the term 'evil' very strangely. Some have told me it means 'honoring your parents.' Others say that it means 'not giving in to emotions.' I suspect it's neither of these: it's just a rallying term signifying certain cultural identity.

Unfortunately I've noticed that most people who call themselves evil are not very welcoming to outsiders like me.
}}
