== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
{{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} is used by the practitioners of [[Animal Husbandry]] to change the traits of their stock (at this time, horses, only). It allows reading the entire code used to determine the animal's statistics and appearance.

=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge of the genetics of large herbivores that do NOT chew cud, such as pigs, rhinos, and horses. Also covers hippos, which are not ungulates, but which are close enough.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Read the book {{Item|Secrets of the Ungulates}}. 25 Levels of [[Non-Ruminant Ungulate Anatomy]] are required to read it. This book can be gotten by barter with Gerrux once fulfilling the requirements outlined in the [[Arthropod Genetics#Unlocking Arthropod Genetics|Arthropod Genetics Mechanics]] article.
