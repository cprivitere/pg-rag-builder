==Summary==
[[Silvia]] in [[Sun Vale]] said, "Most of the deer around here are healthy, but a number are rabid. Do me a favor and take care of that problem, would you?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Silvia]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. This quest is only available to [[Fairy|Fae]].

* Kill [[Rabid Deer]] or [[Rabid Island Deer]] x3
* Talk to [[Silvia]]
