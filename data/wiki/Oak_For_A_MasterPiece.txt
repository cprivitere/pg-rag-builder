==Summary==
Roshun the Traitor in Serbule asked for some Perfect Oak Wood with which to create staves.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Roshun The Traitor]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
