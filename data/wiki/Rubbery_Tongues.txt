==Summary==
Tyler Green in Serbule Hills said, "I can fight goblins. I can fight spiders. But the frogs? Those 'ranalons', as they call themselves? They're just too thick in number. It's too hard to fight 'em one at a time!

But, uh... I told our pretty waitress that I'd killed thousands of 'em. Can you help me out? I just need one of their hideous tongues as a 'souvenir' to show to Lana."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Tyler Green]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
