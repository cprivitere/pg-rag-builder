==Summary==
Roshun the Traitor in Serbule mentioned that he wished he had a Trophy Tiger Skin. They are rare items found by expert skinners.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Roshun The Traitor]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I have many bear and panther skins to decorate my store. But the local beasts, the striped cats, should be on the wall, too! I have tried to get a skin but they are never perfect enough. I accept my limitations. I am not an expert skinner. It is enough to be a master warrior and an expert tradesorc.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain a {{Item|Trophy Tiger Skin}}
* Talk to Roshun the Traitor
