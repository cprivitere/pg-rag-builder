== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Demon Phrenology]] is a Sub-Skill of [[Phrenology]] based on the study of the skulls of [[Demon]]s.

=== In-Game Description ===
An understanding of how demons think and feel, based on careful study of the shape of their skulls. (Phrenology skills provide critical-hit chances to all Psychology and Mentalism attacks against the corresponding type of creature. Phrenology also has more esoteric uses.)
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Demon Phrenology]] can be raised through [[Corpse Talking]], [[Lost Notus Record]]s, and potions from [[Pennoc]].

Skulls can be extracted from a corpse using a [[Skull Extractor]] or similar tool. Various types of [[Undead]] can also drop skulls.
