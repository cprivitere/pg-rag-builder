[[Kimeta]] is deceased Rakshasa from the City of Povus. Her anger and regrets at the time of her death caused her spectral form to haunt the town. 
== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Kimeta]] only appears in her former house under specific conditions. This house is located near the Southern Wall of the town, in the Sprawl District, next to a pond.

== Favor ==
[[Kimeta]] does not use the favor system.

== Finding Kimeta and Providing Proof== 
[[Kimeta]]'s ghost will only appear with the right conditions.
* It must be nighttime in Povus. She will not spawn before 8:00pm.
* Ghosts must be spawning in the Sprawl district of the city. This condition is not met every night.
* She will despawn at 1:15am.
The first time [[Kimeta]] is spoken to, she asks for an item. This is not a quest.
{{Quote|source=[[Kimeta]]|
: It's not possible. He would never do that! He was murdered!
; Can you hear me? What are you talking about?
: I'm telling you Tumak would never do that! I lived with him for eight years, through countless tragedies, and he never --never! -- hurt anyone! Even when I wanted him to!
; He never hurt... who?
: If you're so certain of his guilt, then surely you can provide proof, right?
}}
