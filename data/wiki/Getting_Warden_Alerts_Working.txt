==Summary==
Pickles in the Sacred Warden Grotto said, "The whole Warden Alert system is down. The main crystal's been down for months, but now both backup crystals are down, too! I don't know how any of this works, so I'm just going to try taking everything apart, cleaning it, and putting it back together. Unfortunately Cassie spilled my last jar of cleaning acid, which then melted most of my cleaning supplies. Can you get us some new stuff?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Pickles]] in the [[Sacred Grotto]]. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
