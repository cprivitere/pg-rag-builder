==Summary==
Umarak in Povus said, "My stash of cooking herbs wasn't found by the orc invaders! But sadly I am now out of basil, and I need that for many of the meals I prepare. I can tell Tryaka is less happy with my cooking, but what can I do?!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Umarak]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
My stash of cooking herbs wasn't found by the orc invaders! But sadly I am now out of basil, and I need that for many of the meals I prepare. I can tell Tryaka is less happy with my cooking, but what can I do?!

===Midway===
I wish I had fresh basil...

===Requirements===
* Obtain Basil x3
* Talk to Umarak
