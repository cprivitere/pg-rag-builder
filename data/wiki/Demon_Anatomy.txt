== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Demon Anatomy''' is a sub-skill of [[Anatomy]] that explores the physiology of demons. This skill can be learned through [[Corpse Talking]].
=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge of the physiology of demons.
=== Demon Culture ===
Demons are sentient creatures from another realm. They seem fascinated with the current events occurring on [[Alharth]], particularly in the [[Council Lands]]. In the past, the Demons came into conflict with the [[Ravana]] [[Rakshasa]]. Demons reproduce using egg sacks. The Demon army appears to be constructed of individual generals, who lead Pods, and sub-pods of soldiers.

[[Destriel]], the Demon Lord, is a well known face across [[Alharth]]. His armies march on the [[Council Lands]], and contributes his head to the Project: Gorgon logo.

[[Berelius]], Queen of All Things, Benevolent Goddess of Light and Dark, is a demon general who rules dozens of planes. [[Tok of the Eighth Pod]] serves her.

[[Lord Forthragar]] is a Demon Lord. [[Zukelmux The Intransigent]] is a gargoyle that acts a general in his army.
