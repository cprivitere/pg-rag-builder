==Summary==
Use Blanche's awl to chip the giant crystal near a lake somewhere around Serbule. You aren't sure what will happen when you do this. You've been told to be careful.

==Prerequisites==
To start this quest, talk to [[Blanche]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.  OPTIONAL: Start the quest [[The Red Crystal]] before talking to [[Blanche]].

==Notes==
*This quest is part of the central quest "chain", where players learn more about the backstory for the game.  The chain starts with [[Yurra]] and [[The Red Crystal]].
*The [[Creepy Crystal]] required for this quest is located near the lake north-east of [[Serbule Keep]], which later becomes the entrance to the [[Crystal Cavern]].
*Once the quest is complete, the {{Item|Geomancy Awl}} is no longer needed.
