==Summary==
Sammie Grimspine in Serbule Hills said, "You know those flying monsters the ranalon have? Those 'scray'? Yeah. Well, I need a stinger from one. If you find one, bring it to me!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Sammie Grimspine]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

To begin this quest you must have level 1 [[Calligraphy]].
