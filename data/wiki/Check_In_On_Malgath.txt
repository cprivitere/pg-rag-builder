==Summary==
Nightshade asked you to look in on his friend Malgath, who is deep within a cave full of wolves. The cave is somewhere in the Kur Mountains. In return, Nightshade says he'll help you convince Malgath to teach you his unusual craft skill.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Nightshade]]''' in '''[[Kur Mountains]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Malgath was staying here with us at first, but one day he came back from hunting and said he was "infected". Said he had to go take care of his infection, and he moved into a cave not too far from here. I went to check on him a week ago, and the cave is full of wolves! Couldn't get very far in there. I guess he's dead... but if you find him in there, let me know. If he's still alive, I'll help you convince him to train you.}}

===Requirements===
* Talk to Malgath
* Talk to Nightshade
