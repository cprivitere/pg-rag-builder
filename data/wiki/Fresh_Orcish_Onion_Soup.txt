==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "There you are! I've been waiting so long! Let me have the orcish onion soup. And it had better be fresh!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime.
