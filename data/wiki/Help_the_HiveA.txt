==Summary==
A beehive in the Fae Realm said, "Bzz. BzzZZzzz ...
We need your help, fairy. The nibbling sprouts burrow under the ground.

Bzz. ZzzZzzZzzBz ... zzzBzt!
They ambush us while we pollinate. You must kill them!

BzzRtxl!"

This Quest can be repeated after 5 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Beehive]] A''' in '''[[Fae Realm]],''' completing the quests [[Meet the Hive]] and [[Pollinate]]. This quest is only available to Fae.
