== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Candle Making]] is a crafting skill involved with the creation of buff-granting candles. Once a candle is deployed, all who appreciate it before it despawns will be granted the buff.

=== In-Game Description ===
Covers all the skills of the chandler, from rendering various materials into candle wax, to creating the candles themselves, including candles with exotic properties.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Gorvessa]] in [[Hogan's Basement]] trains the skill at '''{{Favor|Comfortable}}''' favor. When purchased, the recipe for {{Item|Shoddy Tallow}} is learned.
* [[Gorvessa]] in [[Hogan's Basement]] unlocks levels 51-60.
* [[Bendith the Banished]] in the [[Fae Realm]] unlocks levels 61-70 and levels 71-80.
* [[Electra Breakdawn]] in [[Statehelm]] unlocks levels 81-90 and 91-100.
==== Candle Making Books ====
[[Candle Making]] books can be found at candle stations across [[Alharth]]. Candle stations teach recipes to users with high enough [[Candle Making]] levels to craft the candles available at that station. Most candles can only be crafted at the station that teaches the recipe for that candle.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Candle Deployment ===
=== Candle Crafting and Stations ===
Most candles may be crafted at any station, but the candles taught by the 5 books can only be crafted at their respective candle-crafting station. For example, Bacon candles may only be crafted at the candle-crafting station in Hogan's Keep.

As of the September 8, 2025 update, the candlemaking station in the Fae realm was moved into Errruka's Cave dungeon. This is consistent with the other stations.
