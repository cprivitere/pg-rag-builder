==Summary==
Hi there! Ready for some combat training? Let's get to it! A skilled rabbit should be able to take down any predator without difficulty, no matter big they are or how many there are! Get out there and show me what you got! Take down 20 Mot-Uraks, 20 Polar Bears and 20 Starving Tundra Wolves. Remember that you must complete this task in rabbit form. No cheating! Report back to me when you're done and I'll load you up with some more bunny juices.

This Quest can be repeated after 15 days.

This quest is only available during a BunFu event. 

===Requirements===
* Kill 20 Mot-Uraks
* Kill 20 Tundra Bears
* Kill 20 Starving Tundra Wolves
* Speak to Boop
