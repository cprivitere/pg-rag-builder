==Summary==
Kalrod said, "Ho there! In a mood to build something? A lot of our chairs were broken on the way here because of the rough ride, even our really good chairs. If you can provide us with 10 [[Amazing Chair]]s, I can show you a few tricks to help with building better ones in the future!"

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kalrod]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Carpentry]] level 70. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Ho there! In a mood to build something? A lot of our chairs were broken on the way here because of the rough ride, even our really good chairs. If you can provide us with 10 [[Amazing Chair]]s, I can show you a few tricks to help with building better ones in the future!

===Requirements===
* Craft 10 Amazing Chairs
* Obtain 10 Amazing Chairs
* Talk to Kalrod
