==Summary==
Charles Thompson, a vendor in a tent outside of Serbule, asked you to obtain some "strange dirt -- you know, the kind that you sometimes find under mushrooms or corpses."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Charles Thompson]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|A favor? Oh, well, I'm out of dirt. Not just regular dirt, you understand -- the strange dirt you sometimes find under mushrooms or corpses. It has a million uses! Most people use it for fertilizer, but I use it in potions. I just use a lot of sugar to mask the taste.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Strange Dirt}} x5
* Talk to Charles Thompson
