==Summary==
Jace Soral in the Kur Mountains asked you to kill nearby deer to reduce the predator food supply.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Jace Soral]]''' in '''[[Kur Mountains]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
