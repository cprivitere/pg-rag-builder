Golems are pets created using [[Battle Chemistry]], which can be programmed by the summoner to perform various actions.

==Unlocking==
The first Golem skill unlocked is Primal Golem at [[Battle Chemistry]] level 11. When first unlocked, he has only one Rule slot.

==Programming==
Once summoned, the Golem can be programmed by clicking the ''Program'' button in the Pets window. A player can access the Pets window by clicking the icon to the left of your 12 ability buttons. If the programming window instantly closes, you may have to be closer to your golem.

Programming consists of setting ''Rules'', each of which has a ''Condition'' and an ''Ability''. When summoned, every few seconds the Golem will look down his list of ''Rule''s until he finds a ''Condition'' that is matched. When found, he will perform the associated ''Ability''.

Both ''Conditions'' and ''Abilities'' need to be learned before they can be applied to a Golem.

==Conditions==
Conditions can currently be purchased from [[Durmok Orebinder]] in [[Rahu]].
