Much like the rest of the inhabitants of [[Kur Mountains]],'''Janet Lews''' clearly doesn't want to be there. Lews was once a master of the [[Sword]], but hurt her back before arriving in the [[Kur Mountains]]. In the past, she was a prisoner in [[Statehelm]], for murder of multiple people...

Janet Lews serves as the teacher for advanced [[Sword]] techniques. Befriend her, and levels above 50 may be reached!{{Hint|Friends or higher}}
