==Summary==
Umarak in Povus said, "My mate is not doing well since the invasion. She used to make jewelry, and I'm hoping I can convince her to take up the trade again. It will make her happier. I just need some cinnabar!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Umarak]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
My mate is not doing well since the invasion. She used to make jewelry, and I'm hoping I can convince her to take up the trade again. It will make her happier. I just need some cinnabar!

===Midway===
Tryaka used to make necklaces of cinnabar. If we still had some, perhaps she'd take up the trade again.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Cinnabar x3
* Talk to Umarak
