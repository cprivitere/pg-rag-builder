[[The Bat in the Hat]] is an Event NPC who first appeared outside of [[Serbule]] during [[Halloween 2019]]. When he appears in Serbule or Serbule Hills, adventurers have one hour to find him and deliver his requested items in exchange for a reward.

In 2020, [[The Bat in the Hat]] returned as a stationary NPC in the [[Red Wing Casino]]. Adventurers who completed a certain number of quests for [[Zhia Lian]] would receive a recipe for the [[Creepy Jack O' Lantern]].

== Events ==
===== Halloween 2019 =====
Gifted  {{Item|Pumpkin-Spice Cookies}} to explorers who found it.
===== Halloween 2020 =====
During [[Halloween 2020]], [[The Bat in the Hat]] appeared in the [[Red Wing Casino]]. It would teach the recipe for {{Item|Creepy Jack O' Lantern}} to anyone who completed at least a minimum number of quests for [[Zhia Lian]].
===== Halloween 2021 =====
In the week leading up to [[Halloween 2021]], [[The Bat in the Hat]] appeared in the [[Serbule]] region, offering free transformations to and from [[Giant Bat]] form.

A few days before the first Halloween Party of the year, [[The Bat in the Hat]] returned to the [[Red Wing Casino]]. Besides handing out the recipe for {{Item|Creepy Jack O' Lantern}}, it also offered two quests. [[Halloween Event: A Hat From a Bat]] first, followed by [[Halloween Event: A Better Hat From a Bat]].
