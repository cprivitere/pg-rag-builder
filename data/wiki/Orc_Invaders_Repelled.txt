==Activity Quest==
[[Orc Invaders Repelled]] is a group of [[Activity Quests]] which do not appear in the quest log. These four almost identical quests share the name of '''Orc Invaders Repelled''' with the only noticeable difference being a change in their InternalName (Vidaria_OrcInvaders1, Vidaria_OrcInvaders2, Vidaria_OrcInvaders3,Vidaria_OrcInvaders4). They have identical rewards.

===Prerequisites===
This [[Activity Quests|Activity Quest]] is automatically completed when an Orc-related Ecological Disaster is prevented.
===Rewards===

* 1 point for Serbule Community Chest.
* Level 90 or 95 Jewelry item.
