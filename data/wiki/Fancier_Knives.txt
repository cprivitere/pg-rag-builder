==Summary==
Yogzi in Sun Vale wants you to make him a bunch of basic skinning knives.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Yogzi]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

To begin this quest you must have level 1 [[Toolcrafting]].

You must have previously completed [[Simple Knives]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Practice makes perfect! Practice practice practice! When you think you're ready, make me some basic skinning knives.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain Basic Skinning Knives (5)
* Talk to Yogzi
