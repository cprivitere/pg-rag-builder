== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Tanning''' lets you turn animal skins into Rolls, which are primarily used in Leatherworking. You also need Tanning for dyeing leather equipment. It requires you to be near a Tanning Rack

=== In-Game Description ===
The craft of turning disgusting animal skins, furs, and hides into leather, fleece, and other material ready to be used in clothing and armor.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* [[Kleave]] teaches Tanning and beginner Tanning recipes
** You must complete the [[Power Potions!]] Quest for [[Kleave]], which grants 200 [[Tanning]] experience and raises Tanning to Level 2, to be able to train Tanning recipes. You must also be at {{favor|Comfortable}} or above.
* [[Sirine]] in [[Rahu]] will unlock levels 51-60 and 61-70, and teaches 51+ Tanning recipes
* [[Felmer]] in [[Fae Realm]] will unlock levels 71-80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]] or [[Grim Jim]] at [[Statehelm]].
* Levels 91-100 are unlocked by [[Grim Jim ]] at [[Statehelm]].

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Tanning Rack Locations ===
Almost all Tanning recipes will require you to be near a Tanning Rack.
; [[Serbule]]
: In the walled area south of town, next to the blacksmith [[Tadion]]. You can buy Tannin powder from [[Therese]] in the nearby garden.
: A second one in the house next to Joeh's house.  Convenient if you are offloading crafted armor to [[Joeh]].
; [[Eltibule]]
: Next to [[Kleave]] in Eltibule Keep (central castle) and next to [[Thimble Pete]] in [[Hogan's Keep]] (south castle).
; [[Sun Vale]]
: Behind [[Legs]] in [[Animal Town]].
; [[Kur Mountains]]
: Next to [[Ukorga]].
: In [[Syndra]]'s house.
; [[Rahu]]
: In the raised central area. There's another one next to Sirine's house.
; [[New Prestonbule Cave]]
: To the right of the entrance
; [[Fae Realm]]
: In the Fel-Dasculan Ruins, near [[Noita the Green]]
