==Summary==
Mope in Povus said, "Six days prior, a band of fortified orcs attempted to cross my fungal fields and chop down my tall mushrooms. They were killed and their bodies are now fertilizer. Yet I fear retaliation, for orcs are not prone to abandoning their schemes. It would be prudent to remove that threat immediately."

This Quest can be repeated after 10 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mope]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor. 

===Preface===
Six days prior, a band of fortified orcs attempted to cross my fungal fields and chop down my tall mushrooms. They were killed and their bodies are now fertilizer. Yet I fear retaliation, for orcs are not prone to abandoning their schemes. It would be prudent to remove that threat immediately.

* Kill Orcs in Their Camps x10
* Talk to Mope
