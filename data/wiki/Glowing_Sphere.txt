[[Glowing Sphere]]s are a race of beings that long ago ascended beyond their mortal forms. They travel the multicosmos seeking to explore and experiment to gain more knowledge. Sometimes, [[Glowing Sphere]]s directly intervene in the development of less intelligent species.
