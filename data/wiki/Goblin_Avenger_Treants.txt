==Summary==
Glortok in the Rahu Sewers said, "I don't understand why those trees are down here. And I hate that! The ratkin stay away from there, as if they know some secret. I hate that, too! Kill the trees!"

This Quest can be repeated after 66 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chief Glortok]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Goblin Avenger: Meat Snails]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
I don't understand why those trees are down here. And I hate that! The ratkin stay away from there, as if they know some secret. I hate that, too! Kill the trees!

===Requirements===
* Kill Treants x15
* Talk to Glortok
