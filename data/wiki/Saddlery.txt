== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Saddlery]] is a sub-skill of [[Leatherworking]] used to create equipment for horses.

=== In-Game Description ===
Creating tack for horses, including saddles, saddlebags, and reins.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===

* [[Pasha]] in [[Amulna]] will teach [[Saddlery]] at {{favor|Comfortable}} favor. Level 50 [[Leatherworking]] is also required. Pasha also unlocks Levels 51-60 and Levels 61-70.

* [[Felmer]] in the [[Fae Realm]] unlocks Levels 71-80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
