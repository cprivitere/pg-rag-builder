[[Sir Hughes]] in [[Statehelm]] asked you to defeat 8 [[Swamp Viper]] in [[Povus]].

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Sir Hughes]]''' in '''[[Statehelm]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|You want to get better at hand-to-hand combat fast? I know just the thing. Wrestle giant snakes. It works! Of course, you need a certain level of skill beforehand. ===Requirements===
* Defeat 8 [[Swamp Viper]] in [[Povus]]
* Talk to [[Sir Hughes]]
