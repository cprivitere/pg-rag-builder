==Summary==
Pennoc in Serbule told you to go out and skin a bunch of animals. You'll need a Skinning Knife or similar tool in your inventory.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pennoc]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Skin carcasses for me! Take a skinning knife and just... yeah, skin them! So gross! But don't do it in town -- I don't want to watch you do it, I just want to be amused by the knowledge that you're out there doing it.}}

===Requirements===
* Skin Corpses
* Talk to Pennoc
