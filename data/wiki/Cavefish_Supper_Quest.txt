==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "I'm in the mood for something simple. Please bring me a cavefish supper. A FRESH cavefish supper, mind you! I'm eating simply, not poorly!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
I'm in the mood for something simple. Please bring me a cavefish supper. A FRESH cavefish supper, mind you! I'm eating simply, not poorly!

===Midway===
When will my cavefish supper be ready? Don't tell me you're catching the cavefish now!

===Requirements===
* Craft Cavefish Supper
* (Keep Cavefish Supper)
* Talk to Ailred
