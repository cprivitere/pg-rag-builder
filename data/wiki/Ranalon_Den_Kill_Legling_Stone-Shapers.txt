==Summary==
Chirrra in  the Serbule Hills Ranalon Den said, "Deep in the legling training area is the mage training section. Right now they're mostly doing stone-shaping, so look for the poorly-worked stones. You definitely want to take these mages out before they fully master their abilities!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chirrra]]''' in '''[[Ranalon Den]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

This is a solo quest from Chirrra. You may have one solo quest from Chirrra at a time. You can take on a new solo quest 15 minutes after completing or canceling this quest.
