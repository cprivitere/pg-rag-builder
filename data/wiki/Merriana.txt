[[Merriana]] is an elven Weather Witch who survived the destruction of [[Dwyndarre]]. 

== Location ==
; [[Serbule Hills]]
: [[Merriana]] can be found inside the once-abandoned Hunting Lodge in western Serbule Hills.

== Favor ==
[[Elven]] travelers who speak to [[Merriana]] will begin with {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. All others begin at {{Favor|Tolerated}}.

== Quests ==
[[Merriana]] is seeking help to find a [[Naturesphere]], a dangerous artifact if used improperly. The quests offered task an adventurer to explore [[Serbule]], [[Serbule Hills]], and various dungeons.
