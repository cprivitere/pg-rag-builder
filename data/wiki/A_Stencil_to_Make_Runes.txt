==Summary==
The Sand Seer in Amulna wanted you to be on the lookout for an "ancient magical stencil". She suggested that she would pay well for it.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[The Sand Seer | Sand Seer]]''' in '''[[Ilmari Desert]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Geometric Rune Charts]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Do you recall those rune charts you found for me? Well, I've studied them a little bit, and now I wish to try to make my own. I just need something to guide my hand. You wouldn't happen to have any ancient magical stencils, by any chance? Well, keep an eye out for one! I've got a fat sack of coins burning a hole in my dress.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain an {{Item|Ancient Stencil}}
* Talk to Sand Seer
