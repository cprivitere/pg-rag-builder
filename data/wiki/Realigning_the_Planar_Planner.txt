==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] sent me to a teleportation sage named [[Elmetaph]] on [[Anagoge Island]] to recalibrate the outdated coordinates in Khyrulek's journal. [[Truxartis]] provides an inaccurate copy of the Planar Planner at quest start; additional objectives are revealed after delivering it to [[Elmetaph]].

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[Deciphering the Planar Planner]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|Khyrulek's journal is almost correct, but her calculations are outdated due to planar shift. They must be recalibrated by a... teleportation sage.

There was such a sage in Statehelm years ago named Elmetaph, but my spies say he lives now in a lighthouse on a desolate island.

Seek out this Elmetaph and force him to fix these calculations.}}

===Requirements===
* Give the Inaccurate Planar Planner Notes to [[Elmetaph]] (in the lighthouse on [[Anagoge Island]])
* Obtain Accurate Planar Planner Notes ([[Elmetaph]] returns the corrected version)
* Obtain {{Item|Hexapus Mouth Ring}} x2 (revealed after speaking with [[Elmetaph]])
* Obtain {{Item|Treant Acorn}} x1 (revealed after speaking with [[Elmetaph]])
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]
