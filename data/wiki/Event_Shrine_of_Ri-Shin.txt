==Summary==
Find the Shrine of Ri-Shin along the southern coast in Kur Mountains. Speak to Snowleaf, the Scion of Ri-Shin.

This Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

This quest is available at [[Druid]] level 1. 

===Preface===
<i>You become aware of a powerful presence reaching out to you. Does Dreva have a task for you? A voice speaks in your mind:</i>

Find the Shrine of Ri-Shin in the Kur Mountains. Ri-Shin is a strong ally; render whatever aid is needed.

===Requirements===
* Visit the Shrine of Ri-Shin
