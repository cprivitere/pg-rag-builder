{{Quote|source=[[Leah Bowman]]|
Thanks for asking. Personally, I think the term 'half-human' is fine. I don't take offense to 'halfie' either, though maybe it's just because I'm used to it. I don't like 'half-breed,' though. It makes me sound like a dog. And 'half-orc' is used as a slur, so... don't. And all the terms with 'green' in the name are slurs: greenskin, greeny, halfgreen, greenback, all of them.}}

[[Leah Bowman]] is an animal tender in the city of Povus. She is both stable master and expert in animal breeding (husbandry).

== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Leah Bowman]] can be found in Old Town, southwest of the Town Status Board, appearing at Town Level 0.

But at least the rakkies didn't care what I look like. I even had some friends here, before they were murdered. Murdered by my father's goons, I might add.
}}
{{Quote|source=[[Leah Bowman]]|
Some halfies can past for human, or at least pass for an orc. But I'm not so lucky. Every race thinks I'm an abomination.}}

When I was old enough, I apprenticed with Yenshi the Animal Guru. He taught me a lot about kuvou an d animal-breeding in general. In fact, this was his house! He was killed in the bed next door to the one I sleep in. Pretty grim, huh?}}
{{Quote|source=[[Leah Bowman]]|
My mom spent her savings on an elven glamour that made me look like a human. Managed to get through school in [[Statehelm]] that way, but eventually people found out. And it was even worse because I'd been hiding it.

So mom brought us back here, the very place she was raped. She was never the same after that. But I learned to love it here. I could be myself and the rakkies didn't care at all.}}
{{Quote|source=[[Leah Bowman]]|
Well for one thing, I inherited incredible anger issues. And my hair is utterly unmanageable. Oh, and I want to kill all humans... but that's just because of how they treat me.}}
