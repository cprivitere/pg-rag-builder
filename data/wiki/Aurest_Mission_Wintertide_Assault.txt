==Summary==
[[Lady Alethina]] in [[Red Wing Casino]] said, "I have an extremely difficult task today. You need to enter the [[Fae Realm|fae realm]] near [[Sun Vale]]. There's a Winter ship in orbit there. Sneak aboard and take out some problems."

This [[Daily Quest]] can be repeated after 1 day.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lady Alethina]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor. This quest is only available to characters with at least two level 65 [[Skills|Combat Skills]].

===Preface===
I have an extremely difficult task today. You need to enter the [[Fae Realm|fae realm]] near [[Sun Vale]]. There's a Winter ship in orbit there. Sneak aboard and take out some problems.

===Requirements===
* Kill [[Ensign|Ensigns]] x8
* Kill [[Captain Evergloam]]
* Kill [[Abominable Soldier|Abominable Soldiers]] x8
* Kill [[Dalvos's Astral Projection]]
* Talk to [[Lady Alethina]]
