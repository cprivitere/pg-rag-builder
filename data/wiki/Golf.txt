== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Golf]] is the common term for a series of games played by Dwarves, Elves, and Humans.
=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in golf-related games. Covers any game involving the propulsion of an object toward a designated goal on the landscape.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Completing a hole of [[Golf]] during [[Pennoc's Pennant]] rewards enough Golf XP to reach Level 1.
