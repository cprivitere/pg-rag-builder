== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Whittling]] is a skill commonly used by orcs to improve their reflexes. [[Whittling]] produces wooden figurines that grant timed bonuses to Combat XP and Crafting XP collection. Certain figurines also have additional esoteric effects. 
=== In-Game Description ===
The art of turning wood into little artistic thingies.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Brugo the Wise]] in [[Povus]] will sell the initial training of [[Whittling]] after reaching {{favor|Comfortable}} favor. At least 50 levels of [[Carpentry]] must be held to learn [[Whittling]].
* [[Brugo the Wise]] in [[Povus]]  unlocks Levels 51-60, 61-70, and 71-80
* [[Engineer Crall]] in [[Vidaria]] unlocks levels 81-90.
* [[Beast Forms]] Are not able to use [[Whittling]] recipes

== Connected Skills ==

===== Related Skills: =====
* [[Carpentry]] - Carpentry and [[Whittling]] use many of the same ingredients.
* [[Knife Fighting]] - [[Whittling]] is used by orcs to improve their skills with knives.
