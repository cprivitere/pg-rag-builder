==Summary==
Raim said, "Want to swap some knowledge to progress your skills? Bring me one full crafted Rough Cloth suit and I will see what I can do to help you flourish at your craft! I only want the enchanted version of the suit... I don't deal with non-enchanted crap!"

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Raim]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Tailoring]] level 14. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Want to swap some knowledge to progress your skills? Bring me one full crafted Rough Cloth suit and I will see what I can do to help you flourish at your craft! I only want the enchanted version of the suit... I don't deal with non-enchanted crap!

===Requirements===
* Craft a Rough Cloth Shirt (Enchanted)
* Obtain a Rough Cloth Shirt (Enchanted)
* Craft some Rough Cloth Pants (Enchanted)
* Obtain some Rough Cloth Pants (Enchanted)
* Craft a Rough Cloth Cowl (Enchanted)
* Obtain a Rough Cloth Cowl (Enchanted)
* Craft some Rough Cloth Gloves (Enchanted)
* Obtain some Rough Cloth Gloves (Enchanted)
* Craft some Rough Cloth Slippers (Enchanted)
* Obtain some Rough Cloth Slippers (Enchanted)
* Talk to Raim
