[[Legs]] is a sentient [[Spider]] that excels in spinning creations from her silk. She also likes to collect hunting trophies from monsters.

As a resident of [[Animal Town]], [[Legs]] is only willing to hold conversations with characters that have transformed into a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]] over 180 real-world minutes prior to the conversation attempt. If you lose track of the time you last transformed, [[Norbert]] is willing to tell you how long you have until the other residents will tolerate talking with you.
