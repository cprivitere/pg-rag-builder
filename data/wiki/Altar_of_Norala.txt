During the full moon, werewolves ([[Lycanthropy]])  may now interact with an altar placed at the entrance of the [[Wolf Cave]] in [[Kur Mountains]]. The Altar performs several tasks for the goddess of the hunt, which will earn them tokens they can then spend on special abilities, unlocks and boons. 

*The tokens must be used during the full moon or they will disappear.
*The Boons and Runspeed buffs that are unlocked with tokens will last until the next Real Life full moon. 
*The permanent buffs will be unlocked permanently.

==Location==
; [[Kur Mountains]]
: In the [[Wolf Cave]], near the zone entrance

==Quests==
* Completing an altar quest will earn 1 token. These tokens disappear if not used during the current full moon.

* [[Wolf: Civilized Races]] (Kill 50 humans, elves or rakshasa)
* [[Wolf: Goblin Killing]] (Kill 50 goblins) 
* [[Wolf: Kill the Scion of Gulagra]] (Kill the [[Scion of Discord]])
* [[Wolf: Wolfsbane]] (Deliver 10 wolfsbane)
* [[Wolf: Hunt Deer]] (Kill 25 deer)
* [[Wolf: Hunt Deer 2]] (Kill 50 deer)
* [[Wolf: Kill the Scion of Norala]] (Kill the [[Scion of Norala]])
* [[Wolf: Orcish Genocide]] (Kill 50 orcs) from orc camps in [[Kur Mountains]]
* [[Wolf: Orcish Genocide 2]] (Kill 100 orcs) from orc camps in [[Kur Mountains]]
* [[Wolf: Ciervos Must Die]] (Kill [[Ciervos The Shadow]])
* [[Wolf: Dragon-Kin]] (Kill 10 Droaches, [[Fire Droach]]es, [[Shocking Droach]]es)
* [[Wolf: Fae Destruction]] (Kill 50 fae or fey creatures) from [[Fairy Slave]]s in [[Serbule Hills]], [[Fey Panther]]s in [[Eltibule]] or [[Sunvale]], [[The Mauler]] and [[The Mangler]] in [[Eltibule]], [[Soldier of the Winter Court|Soldiers of the Winter Court]] or [[Boreal Fist]]s in [[Sunvale]].

==NOTE==
There is Alternate method to raise skills max lv: can be learned from [[Sanja]]

Normally you can't learn abilities till your combat level reaches the required level but with token upgrades you can unlock the abilities earlier. This will replace said ability with the higher tier automatically and if lycanthropy is too low, the ability will be grayed out and you will be unable to use it. Plan accordingly and don't rush into combat after training.
