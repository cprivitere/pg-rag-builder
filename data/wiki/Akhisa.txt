'''Akhisa''' is the Goddess of Spiders and Patience, and considered a Class 2 Nature Spirit by [[Guide to Nature Spirit Classification|Councilwoman Ilvadele Irasce]]. The [[Strange Spider]] serves as a diplomat of Akhisa, and is currently trapped in the [[Serbule Crypt]] by [[Khyrulek]].

There is [[Guild:Player_Guilds|Player Guild]] that worship Akhisha: [[Guild:Akhisa's_Web|Akhisa's Web]] as well as [[Guild:Akhisa's_Attendants| Akhisa's Attendants]] (on Laeth).
