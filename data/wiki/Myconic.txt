== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Myconic]] is a skill linked to the of gathering of mushrooms. Sometimes when picking a mushroom, adventurers will receive experience in [[Myconic]] instead of [[Mycology]]. Those who receive this experience often report seeing vague ideas or hearing strange voices in their head. As skill in [[Myconic]] improves, the messages become clearer. 

Once [[Myconic]] is learned, gathering mushrooms of any variety has a chance to grant experience. Travelers often raise their [[Myconic]] in [[Serbule]] and [[Eltibule]], areas with large quantities of mushrooms compared to the later arid regions of [[Ilmari]] and [[Rahu]].

=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge of the secret language of ancient fungus creatures.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Picking mushrooms across Alharth and the Fae Realm will grant [[Myconic]] experience.
* [[Mushroom Jack]] will teach [[Myconic]] during the hangout '''Hunt for Myconians down in the ravine (4h).'''

=== Unlocking Myconian Mysteries ===
At Level 8 [[Myconic]], adventurers report being able to converse with the Tall Mushrooms around the [[Serbule]] region. Speaking with one of these unusual shrooms will transport the speaker to the [[Myconian Cave]], a tunnel network under the region. In the cave system, sentient Myconians can be spoken to, such as [[Poe]] and [[Way]].

* '''Level 8''' - Access to Myconian Cave
* '''Level 9''' - Understand [[Mu]] fluently.
* '''Level 10''' - Understand [[Jaw]] fluently.
* '''Level 11''' - Understand [[Poe]] and [[Voo]] fluently.
* '''Level 13''' - Understand [[Way]] fluently.
* '''Level 15''' - Understand [[Sem]] fluently.
* '''Level 17''' - Understand [[Yoy]] fluently.
* '''Level 20''' - Understand [[Mope]] fluently.
