This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Leah Bowman]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''.
===Preface===
There's an undecorated tree in Povus. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.
===Requirements===
* Get Old Bones for the Tree.
* Get Mushrooms and Dye for the Tree.
* Get Alchemical Regents for the Tree.
* Get Miss-Fly Hater for the Tree-Topper.
* Talk to [[Leah Bowman]]
