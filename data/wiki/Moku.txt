[[Moku]] is a Rakshasa chef from the City of Povus.
== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Moku]] can be found at the Old Town inn, when the City is at least Level 5.

== Favor ==
[[Moku]] does not use the favor system.

== Training ==
[[Moku]] trains recipes for [[Cooking]], [[Sushi Preparation]] and [[Angling]] (unlocks level 70 for 35 xogrite).

== Services == 
==== Bartering ==== 
Moku offers to barter {{Item|Xogrite Chunk}} for recipes and ingredients. At Povus Town Level 5, he makes one trade per in-game day, restocking in the morning.
