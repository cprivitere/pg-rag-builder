==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] sent me into the [[Animal Nexus]] realm, accessible via [[Eltibule]], to find a Tuvillic Rune and retrieve a secret ingredient.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[A New Strength Potion]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|The secret ingredient in my potion is, of course... a secret. You are not worthy... to know it. Its creation would be beyond you anyway.

I have hid some of this ingredient in various realms... outside of ours. The easiest to reach is via Eltibule.

The winter fae have tunnels that travel through Eltibule. Look for green bears. Oh... you may need to find a key. Speak to humans in the area.

Once inside, look for a Tuvillic Rune. It's not too far... inside.}}

===Requirements===
* Touch Vampire Rune in [[Animal Nexus]]
* Obtain {{Item|Secret Ingredient}} x1
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]
