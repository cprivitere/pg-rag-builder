[[Snowleaf]] is a Treant who serves as a devout Scion of the god of plants and trees, [[Ri-Shin]]. During the [[Winter Holiday]] season, [[Snowleaf]] appears in the [[Kur Mountains]] region in an attempt to recruit travelers to assist in his task of improving and maintaining the Shrine of Ri-Shin.

== Location ==
; [[Serbule]]
: [[Snowleaf]] appears at the Shrine of [[Ri-Shin]] during the [[Winter Holiday]] season.
== Favor ==
[[Snowleaf]] believes that only adventurers who can prove themselves friends of nature and devout to [[Ri-Shin]] can be trusted to accomplish his tasks.

While [[Druid]]s gain his trust naturally, a hidden favor mechanic is used to judge all others. During the [[Winter Holiday]], completing quests to decorate Festive Trees across Alharth will raise this favor value. Completely decorating the Festive Trees in four regions is enough to earn his trust.
== Quests ==
===== Shrine Tasks =====
*This is a snapshot of the Quests for the first year [[Snowleaf]] was introduced.  The pet and total number of items to be donated has changed each year. See [[Winter Holiday]] for details about each years event.
Each task given by [[Snowleaf]] requires a quantity of items sacred to [[Ri-Shin]] to be gathered and turned in. [[Snowleaf]] calculates the value of donations to the shrine using a cumulative point system.
* The first time 99 points is reached, the sidebar ability [[Summon a Radish]] will be awarded.
* Every 99 points earned after learning the ability rewards a {{Item|Ri-Shin Token}}.
* Points are cumulative. If 40 1-point items are turned in just before the end of a task, only 59 items are required during a later task to earn a {{Item|Ri-Shin Token}}.
