==Summary==
[[Silvia]] in [[Sun Vale]] said, "Alien fae creatures are all over Sun Vale! I don't blame you, but you should do your part to help clean this up. Kill fey panthers and bring me their tails as proof!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Silvia]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. You must have previously completed [[Cleanse Sun Vale]] in order to undertake this quest. This quest is only available to [[Fairy|Fae]].
