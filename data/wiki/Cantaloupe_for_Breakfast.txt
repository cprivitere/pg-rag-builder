==Summary==
Umarak in Povus said, "My mate Tryaka used to love cantaloupe for breakfast. But we can't get it here anymore. Sigh..."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Umarak]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
My mate Tryaka used to love cantaloupe for breakfast. But we can't get it here anymore. Sigh...

===Midway===
I wish I had some fresh cantaloupe...

===Requirements===
* Obtain Cantaloupe x3
* Talk to Umarak
