== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Armor Augmentation''' is an [[Augmentation]] skill that focuses on Chest-Armor Augments and Leg-Armor Augments.
=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge of the techniques for creating Chest-Armor Augments and Leg-Armor Augments. (Augments for other types of equipment are covered by other skills).
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* After speaking with [[Nightshade]] in [[Kur Mountains|Kur]] and completing a task for him, '''Armor-Augmentation''' can be learned from [[Syndra]].

'''Armor-Augmentation''' is broken into three tiers of recipes:
* Levels 1-30 & 31-60 are learned from Syndra in Kur Mts.
* Level 61-70 recipes are taught by Kohan in Rahu.
* Levels 71-80 and 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* Levels 81-90 and 91-100 are available to unlock from [[Corinth]].
