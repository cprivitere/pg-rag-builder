[[Brandon Mellus]] is a worshiper of [[Zhia Lian]], determined to host a party. During [[Halloween 2018]] and [[Halloween 2023]], [[Brandon Mellus]] appeared in the town of [[Amulna]], distraught over the long journey to the [[Red Wing Casino]].

==Location==
; [[Ilmari]]
: Found by a large pile of kegs under the tent at the center of town.
