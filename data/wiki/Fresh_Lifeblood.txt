''Previous quest (to unlock): [[Grasuul the Butler]]''

==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] asked me to loot Fresh Lifeblood from creatures and bring it to him. This is a repeatable daily quest offered on Mondays and Thursdays.

===Prerequisites===
To receive this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[Grasuul the Butler]] and is offered on Mondays and Thursdays.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I crave fresh lifeblood! But I am too... Busy to obtain it myself. Go and fetch it for me. Speak with Grasuul, he seems to... Know where to find the easiest lifeblood.}}

===Requirements===
* Loot {{Item|Lifeblood}} x10 from creatures (must be looted, not purchased)
* Keep the Lifeblood in your inventory
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Fresh Lifeblood|
{{Quote|Give!

''[Truxartis drinks the lifeblood.]''

Ah. Now I can continue my work unhindered by thirst.}}

* 50 {{Favor|favor}}
* 10 Blood Oaths
}}

===Notes===
{{Spoiler|Notes|
[[Truxartis]] randomly assigns one daily Blood Oath quest per real-world day from among the four available quests. Speak with [[Grasuul]] for guidance on which creatures are the best blood targets today.
}}
