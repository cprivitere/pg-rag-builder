__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Glajur, a goblin you met under a tower in Kur Mountains, asked for humanoid skulls in order to continue his phrenological research.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Glajur]]''' in '''[[Kur Tower]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I had hoped to master the phrenological areas of humans and elves from the corpses in this crypt, but I didn't find nearly enough skulls. Could you find some humanoid skulls for me? There should be a few lying around in here. Or heck, if you have some from elsewhere, I'd take those too. I just need to continue my studies!}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain 5 [[:Category:Items/HumanoidSkull|Humanoid Skulls]]
* Talk to Glajur
