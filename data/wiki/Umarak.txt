[[Umarak]] is a Rakshasa who has dedicated themselves to trying to improve [[Tryaka]]'s mood.

== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Umarak]] can be found in Old Town, inside a house near the southeast entrance to the Sprawl district. He only appears when the Povus Town Level is at least 8.

== Favor ==
[[Umarak]] does not use the favor system.
