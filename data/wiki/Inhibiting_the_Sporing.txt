==Summary==
Poe the Myconian asked you to retrieve some blusher mushrooms. Poe will use them to inhibit premature sporing.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Poe]]''' in '''[[Myconian Caverns]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I am young for my kind, but I am already sporing! My adventures into the overworld have caused me to bloom prematurely. I am not ready to clone myself, so I must delay the sporing. Do you have some of the inhibitor medicine? Your kind calls it 'Blusher Mushrooms'. I need much of it.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Blusher Mushroom}}s x12
* Talk to Poe
