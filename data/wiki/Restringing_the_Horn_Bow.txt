==Summary==
Sirine in Rahu said, "Well, the bow's almost ready, but I need to restring it. I need cat gut! Bring me a bunch of cat meat and I'll dig through it to find what I need."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Sirine]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Repairing the Horn Bow]] in order to undertake this quest.

* Talk to Sirine
