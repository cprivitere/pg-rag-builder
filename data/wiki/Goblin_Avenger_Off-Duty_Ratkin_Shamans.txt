==Summary==
Glortok in the Rahu Sewers said, "We've lost the element of surprise! Time to reclaim that surprise by killing their necromancers when they're resting. Fools! Hahahaha!"

This Quest can be repeated after 66 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chief Glortok]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Goblin Avenger: Undying Ratkin Blasters]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
We've lost the element of surprise! Time to reclaim that surprise by killing their necromancers when they're resting. Fools! Hahahaha!

===Requirements===
* Kill Relaxing Ratkin Shamans x15
* Talk to Glortok
