== Services ==
{{Quote|source=[[Chancellor Algest]]|No animals! No animals allowed in the bank, I'm afraid. The only exception is for certified animal assistants.}}
Animals will need to register as an assistant with Chancellor Algest in order to access almost all services within Statehelm. This includes all NPC shops and all training. Both the animal player and the humanoid player claiming them as an assistant will need to be present at the same time. The humanoid must pay a registration fee of 5000 councils, and both they and their assistant will be fined if the assistant commits any crimes within the city. Spider and fox players are not able to use their humanoid illusions to be their own owner. 

Spiders, bats, and werewolves are too scary and will need to acquire a magic item elsewhere in [[Statehelm]] in order to speak to Chancellor Algest.

==== War Bonds ====
{{Quote|source=[[Chancellor Algest]]|
You're a business owner, so I won't bullshit you about patriotism. The fact is that War Bonds are a bet -- a bet on the survival of our fair city. We have five of the strongest mages in the world. They've weathered hundreds of storms over their hundreds of years of life. So I would say this is a very safe bet indeed. But I'm not your financial adviser. 

I cannot redeem War Bonds early, no matter the reason. So make sure you don't leave yourself cash-starved. Invest only what you can spare for a year!}}
'''War Bonds''' are a service offered only to individuals with an active business license. War Bonds are purchasable investments which mature and double in value after 365 real-world days. Before they mature, they are essentially worthless.
;Types of War Bonds
* 1,000,000 Councils maturing to 2,000,000 Councils in 1 year.
* 10,000,000 Councils maturing to 20,000,000 (Sold out on April 14, 2026).
