==Summary==
Baroness Marith Felgard in Serbule Hills said, "The ranalon have a sort of outdoor temple near the lake. You'll know it by the two large stone obelisks sticking out of the water nearby. Go there and slay the prayermaster and his minions. The prayer-master is an important leader, and his death will cause chaos among their ranks. I shall pay you 400 Councils."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Marith Felgard]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Tolerated}} favor.
