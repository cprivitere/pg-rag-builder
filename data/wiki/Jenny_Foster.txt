[[Jenny Foster]] is a human currently living in the city of [[Statehelm]]. They work at the Vicious Bat.
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Jenny Foster]] can usually be found at the Vicious Bat. During a Full Moon she is found in Statehelm Sewers near the other animal form trainers.

== Services ==
For 5 NT [[Jenny Foster]] will sell you a [[ Dog Miniaturization Talisman]] that reduces the size of your character model and allows you to speak to NPCs that will talk to animals, but not scary animals. (Only available during Full Moon)
