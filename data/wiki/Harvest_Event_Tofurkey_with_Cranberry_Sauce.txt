==Summary==
Ricatu in Povus said, "Plain old tofurkey is ... kind of dry. Gravy helps, but I bet you can do better. Maybe a sauce ... Oh! How about cranberry sauce! You'll have to find the recipe yourself, though."
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ricatu]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''.  The quest is available during the [[Fall Harvest]]. The quest [[Harvest Event: Tofurkey!]] must be completed.
