==Summary==
There's an undecorated tree in Amulna, a town in the Ilmari desert. Kelim is in charge of decorating it. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kelim]]''' in the '''[[Ilmari Desert]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Get Red Peppers for Decorations
* Get Telka's Tails for Decorations
* Get Manticore Manes for the Tree
* Get Scorpion Stingers for the Tree-Topper
* Talk to [[Kelim]]
