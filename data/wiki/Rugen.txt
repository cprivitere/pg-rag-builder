[[Rugen]] is a Rakshasa living in the hostile section of Rahu City.  He is skilled in the art of [[Sushi Preparation]].
== Location ==
;[[Rahu]]
: The hostile section of [[Rahu]] City.  He will not engage with you meaningfully unless you purchase a visitor's pass from [[Sergeant Ultaka]].
== Shopkeeper ==
[[Rugen]] sells Rahu Rice and Vinegar for [[Sushi_Preparation|the creation of sushi]], along with a few basic prepared sushi items and knives.

== Training ==
[[Rugen]] unlocks progression for Levels 51-60 in [[Fishing]] (at '''Comfortable'''), although the [http://forum.projectgorgon.com/showthread.php?128-Patch-Notes-January-22-2017 January 22 patch notes] indicate this is temporary. He can also unlock Levels 51-60 for [[Skinning]] (at '''Friends''').
