== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Sushi Preparation]] is a sub-skill of [[Cooking]] used in the creation of delicious fish meals.

=== In-Game Description ===
A sub-skill of Cooking used in the creation of delicious fish meals.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Rugen]] teaches [[Sushi Preparation]] after reaching a favor level of {{Favor|Friends}}. Rugen also teaches most of the recipes related to [[Sushi Preparation]].
* [[Crelpin]] teaches [[Crelpin's Sushi]].
* [[Moku]] teaches some level 20-32 recipes.

== Connected Skills ==
[[Sushi Preparation]] is a Sub-Skill of [[Cooking]].
=====Related Skills:=====
* [[Knife Fighting]] - [[Sushi Preparation]] can require 25 Levels in Knife Fighting to learn.
* [[Fishing]] - Other than serving as a method to collect the main ingredient for [[Sushi Preparation]], 25 Levels of Fishing can be required to learn the skill instead of [[Knife Fighting]].
===== Synergy Levels: =====
Bonus [[Synergy Levels]] for [[Sushi Preparation]] can be obtained from the following skills and levels:

:: Level 31
:; [[Fishing]] 
:: Level 55
:; [[Knife Fighting]] 
:: Level 60
