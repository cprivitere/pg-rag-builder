==Summary==
Lakrea gave you a recipe for a Bacon Basket and instructed you to cook one. Eating it is optional.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lakrea]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Eating meat is often bad for your holistic health, but <i>cooking</i> with meat definitely isn't! As you weave the guts of fallen animals into a delicious meal, you'll find your mind soaring to new heights.}}

* Cook a Bacon Basket
* Talk to Lakrea
