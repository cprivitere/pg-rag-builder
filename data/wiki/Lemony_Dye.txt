==Summary==
Jake the Buckler in Sun Vale asked for a pot of Lemon Chiffon dye. You previously learned that a mantis living outside of Serbule can sell you the recipe. There may be other ways to acquire the dye, too.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Jake The Buckler]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Expert's Advice]] in order to undertake this quest.
