[[Shala]] is a Rakshasa collector of items commonly found in the desert regions of [[Ilmari]] and [[Rahu]]. 
== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Shala]] can be found in a shop on the path south of the forge, when the Povus Town Level is at least 6.

== Favor ==
[[Shala]] does not use the favor system.
== Training ==
==== Interpretive Dance Variant ====
[[Shala]] is the teacher for [[Interpretive Dance|Dance 1 (variant D)]]. At least three musicians must be playing instruments near [[Shala]] to convince them to demonstrate their dance.

== Services == 
==== Bartering ==== 
Shala offers to barter items collected in the desert for {{Item|Xogrite Chunk}}. The selection of barters changes at 8am each in-game day.
