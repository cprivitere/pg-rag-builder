==Summary==
Ricatu in Povus said, "I really need a good dipping sauce to go with all this 'meat'. I heard you can do something with pumpkins -- that should be interesting.  But you'll have to find the recipe yourself."
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ricatu]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''.  The quest is available during the [[Fall Harvest]]. The quest [[Harvest Event: Tofurkey!]] must be completed.
