==Summary==
Blanche in Serbule wants an antique spoon.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Blanche]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Someday when I've mastered geology, I'd like to learn more about Anthropology, too: the study of Humans! I guess primitive people just fascinate me. Did you know that Humans discovered cooking before they discovered fire magic? Really! I don't know how they controlled fire without magic, though. I guess that's a mystery lost to the ages.

Speaking of old human cooking, I heard there are antique Human-made spoons in the crypts and caves near here. Could you get me a sample spoon? It'd be fascinating.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain an Antique {{Item|Spoon}}
* Talk to Blanche
