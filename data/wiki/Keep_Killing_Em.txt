==Summary==
Pegast in the Fae Realm said, "You plan to kill more of the Winter Court menace? Wonderful! Go and 'mortal everything up' for the Winter Court!"

This Quest can be repeated after 3 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pegast]]''' in '''[[Fae Realm]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor. You must have previously completed [[Just Kill 'Em]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
You plan to kill more of the Winter Court menace? Wonderful! Go and 'mortal everything up' for the Winter Court!

===Requirements===
* Kill Winter Court Fae x10
* Talk to Pegast
