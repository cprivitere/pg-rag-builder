==Summary==
Lincha in Povus said, "I used to have a beautiful little table where I could leave my paintings to dry. Do you want to know a secret? It was actually a repurposed calligraphy table! I would buy a replacement in an instant, as long as I was confident it wasn't made by goblin slave-labor."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lincha]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime.
