==Summary==
Torgan in New Prestonbule said, "My new hammer must have a handle of impeccable quality. Spruce is, of course, the ideal wood for gripping, but its strength is substandard... except for very occasional pieces of spruce. I need those perfect specimens of spruce wood!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Torgan]]''' in '''[[New Prestonbule Cave]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
