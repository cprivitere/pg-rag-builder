==Summary==
Lakrea told you to catch cavefish. They can be found deep in certain caves, including the caves of Kur Mountains.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lakrea]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|You know what you need? Fish oil. But not just any fish oil -- you need the oil from cave fish. Just holding the fish is enough, but they have to be fresh -- like, super fresh. I recommend catching them yourself.

I hear there's some fish growing in the caves of the Kur Mountains! That's a pretty far trek, but the fresh air will do you good, too.}}

===Requirements===
* Catch some {{Item|Cavefish}} x3
* Talk to Lakrea
