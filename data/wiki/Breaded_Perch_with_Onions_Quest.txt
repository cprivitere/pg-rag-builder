==Summary==
Gershok the Ranalon in Serbule Hills asked for a meal of Breaded Perch with Onions.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Gershok]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|My people are not imaginative with their cooking. We pickle fish. That is what we do. But I yearn for more. I yearn for the delicacy known as Breaded Perch... With Onions. I crave it!}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Breaded Perch with Onions}}
* Talk to Gershok
