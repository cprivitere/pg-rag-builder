==Summary==
Lincha in Povus said, "All my beautiful furniture was destroyed by invaders. Perhaps the most tragic was the loss of my amazing chair. It was hand-crafted by my favorite carpenter! Some day I hope to have a new one that's just as good."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lincha]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
All my beautiful furniture was destroyed by invaders. Perhaps the most tragic was the loss of my amazing chair. It was hand-crafted by my favorite carpenter! Some day I hope to have a new one that's just as good.

===Midway===
I wish I had an amazing new chair...

===Requirements===
* Craft Amazing Chair
* (Keep Amazing Chair)
* Talk to Lincha
