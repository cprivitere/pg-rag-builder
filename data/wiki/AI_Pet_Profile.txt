[[AI Pet Profile]]s (AIP) are provided in the AI.json file, giving the internal name of both a profile name and the internal ability names used by that profile.

Pages placed in the AIP namespace use [[Template:Combat Ability]]  and [[Template:Combat Ability Rage]] to swap the internal names for the icons and ability descriptions seen by players in-game.

Profiles for Wild Monsters can be found in the category [[:Category:AI Profile]] and listed on the [[AI Profile]] page.
==== Pet Profiles ====
Profiles for Tamed Pets are placed in [[:Category:Pet Profile]].
