[[Nightshade]] is a male [[Elven Anatomy|Elf]] who is hiding from the [[Shadow Keepers]] in the Northwestern Outpost of [[Kur Mountains]], which he refers to as a "frozen hell-hole." He mentions that he was once part of a craftsman guild based in [[Sinoca]]. Other members of his guild included [[Syndra]], [[Gribburn]] ,and [[Malgath]]. [[Poe]] was not a guild member, but worked alongside Nightshade for a time. Nightshade is not his real name.
