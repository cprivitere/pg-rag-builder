==Summary==
Syndra in Kur Mountains wishes she had some dragon scales to take home with her.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Syndra]]''' in '''[[Kur Mountains]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Back in Sinoca I had a state of the art research laboratory for inventing new augmentation oils. I had a breakthrough design that requires dragon scales, but, of course... I could never find any dragon scales! Wouldn't it be great if I found some dragon scales to take home with me? It'd almost make this experience worthwhile. Uh... do you know where any already-dead dragons are? ... Me neither.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain Dragon Scales (3)
* Talk to Syndra

===Hints===
Dragon Scales can be obtained from Droaches, located in the [[Dark Chapel]], or from Drakeworms in the [[Ilmari]] Desert.
