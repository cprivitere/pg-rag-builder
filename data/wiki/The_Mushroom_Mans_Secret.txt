==Summary==
Hemmit Magmagrip in New Prestonbule said, "Mox and Bellema don't believe me, but I swear I saw another one of those mushroom men in a cave near here! It was in 'Snowblood Shadow Cave'. I can't understand his way of communicating, but I'm sure he knows the secret of these mushroom rings. If you figure out the secret, tell me!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Hemmit Magmagrip]]''' in '''[[New Prestonbule Cave]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
