==Summary==
You're killing rats in Serbule Hills.

This Quest can be repeated after 0 hours.

===Preface===
<b>Hunting: Rats</b>
Generally considered harmless, some rats do carry diseases. But for the most part even the gigantic varieties are non-hostile. In rural areas they are either treated as pests or as pets, depending on how big of an impact they have on local agriculture.
