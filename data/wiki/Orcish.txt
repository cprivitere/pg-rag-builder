== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Orcish]] measures the comprehension of the [[Orc|Orcish]] language. This language is spoken by [[Orc]]s and other races.

=== In-Game Description ===
Fluency in Orcish, the ancient language of orcs -- as well as many other races and cultures.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Orcs in [[Kur Mountains]] can drop the {{Item|Orcish Language Reference}}. This scroll can teach [[Orcish]].
* [[Melandria]] in [[Gazluk Keep]].

* Interactive objects in [[Gazluk Keep]] will grant you experience in [[Orcish]].

* Many [[Orc]]s in [[Gazluk Keep]] can drop books that can raise your [[Orcish]] knowledge.
