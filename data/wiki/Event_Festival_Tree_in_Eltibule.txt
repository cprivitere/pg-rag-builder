==Summary==
There's an undecorated tree near Kleave in Eltibule. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kleave]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Get Wood for Decorations
* Get Eyeballs for Decoration
* Get Grapefish for the Tree
* Get Fire Dust for the Tree-Topper
* Talk to [[Kleave]]
