==Summary==
Melandria in Gazluk Keep asked you to procure some items needed for the ritual to break Rick's curse.

Melandria said that the flowers she needs only grow at night, near the glowy yellow crystals found in Gazluk.

This Quest can be repeated after 60 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Melandria]]''' in '''[[Gazluk Keep]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Toxic Ice Sludge x3
* Obtain Coldkiss Flowers x5
* Obtain Final Battle Potion
* Obtain Wolf Teeth x3
* Talk to Melandria
