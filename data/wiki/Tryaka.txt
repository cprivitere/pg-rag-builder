{{Quote|source=[[Tryaka]]|
It's just so depressing here, living among the ghosts of my friends.}}
[[Tryaka]] is a Rakshasa merchant living in the City of Povus alongside [[Umarak]]. 

In addition to the attributes below, she is also the source of the [[Scavenger's Ring]], an upgrade of the [[Scrounger's Ring]].
== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Tryaka]] can be found in a home near the southeast arch between Old Town and the Sprawl district, when the Povus Town Level is at least 8.
== Shopkeeper ==
[[Tryaka]] does not sell any items, but is willing to purchase all types of items and equipment.
{{Vendor_purchase_info}}
== Favor ==
[[Tryaka]] does not use the favor system.

== Services ==
==Special Bartering==
After completing [[Tryaka Needs Glass]], [[Tryaka]] offers the option to craft a {{Item|Scavenger's Ring}} of desired level and with chosen skills.
* The first step of the process is to exchange a {{Item|Scrounger's Ring}} for a {{Item|Tryaka's Advanced Crafting Token}}. The Scrounger's Ring will be destroyed.
* The second step of the process is to provide [[Tryaka]] with required information. (Skill 1, Skill 2, Target Level and Rarity)
* The final step of the process is to pay [[Tryaka]]. One {{Item|Tryaka's Advanced Crafting Token}} is required, plus a quantity of Councils and {{Item icon|Live Event Memorabilia|1}} [[Live Event Credits]].
