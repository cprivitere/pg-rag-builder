==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "The service here is truly awful. Do you know how long I've been waiting?! And I had to seat myself! But nevermind. Nevermind all that! I heard that this place makes a great swamp salad. I would like to try this dish."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
The service here is truly awful. Do you know how long I've been waiting?! And I had to seat myself! But nevermind. Nevermind all that! I heard that this place makes a great swamp salad. I would like to try this dish.

===Midway===
Hello again! I ordered the swamp salad. Is there any chance I could receive my food TODAY?!

===Requirements===
* Craft Swamp Salad
* (Keep Swamp Salad)
* Talk to Ailred
