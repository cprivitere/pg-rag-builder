== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Shamanic Infusion''' is one of the 3 item enhancement skills currently in game alongside [[Augmentation]] and [[Transmutation]]. This skill is intended for early-mid game enhancement.

'''Shamanic infusion''' is similar to '''Augmentation''' in that it allows you to '''add one''' magical effect to a piece of gear. However, Shamanic infusion is much simpler: there are fixed recipes and each one provides a predetermined magical effect (example: +armor, +sprint speed, +unarmed damage). Each recipe requires basic animal parts that can be found on monsters located throughout Alharth. (Augmentation, on the other hand, is more complex and involves removing a magical effect/mod from one piece of gear and adding it to a different piece of gear. Therefore, Shamanic Infusion offers a great way for lower and mid-level players to quickly add enhancements to their gear without going through the full Augmentation process.)

=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge and skill in the magical art of enhancing items with the spirit of deceased animals.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
===Enhancement/Craft Points===
Each infusion consumes a certain amount of enhancement points from the item. Looted items start with a pool of 100 points (meaning they can only receive one infusion), while crafted ones have 120 (meaning they can receive an infusion and some other [[Blacksmithing]]/[[Tailoring]] enhancement). 

After an item has been infused with Shamanic infusion, it can also be undone, just like a normal augment, with the "Undo Augment on X Armor" skill.
