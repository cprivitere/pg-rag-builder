== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Dye Making]] is the skill of producing dye to be applied to armor. [[Textile Creation]], [[Tanning]], [[Blacksmithing]], or [[Toolcrafting]] is required to apply the dye to cloth, leather, metal, or organic armor respectively. The skill is learned automatically at Level 1 once a dye recipe is purchased.

You can see see some [[Example Dyed Armor|Examples of Dyed Armor here]]. Note: The armor sets displayed on this page were replaced during the January 2026 launch out of Early Access.

Most dye recipes require either [[Flowers]] or [[Gems]]. Flowers are obtained through [[Foraging]] flower seeds and then growing them with [[Gardening]]. [[Gems]] are obtained through [[Surveying]].

=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in producing dye. These dyes can be used to color armor. This skill covers only the creation of the dye; Textile Creation, Tanning, or Blacksmithing is used to apply the dye, depending on what the armor is made of.

Not every dye is craftable through [[Dye Making]]. NPCs may reward certain dyes after the completion of a quest, while others can be gained as a reward from an event.
