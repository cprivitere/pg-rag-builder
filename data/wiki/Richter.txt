'''Richter''' is an elf who you can interact with in some kind of sewers room that is reached by "Entering the Light" (one of the options that unlocks with the [[Dying]] skill).

The area he is in has no map. It features portals to Serbule Keep and Rahu (both usable with 8 hours cooldown) and a random portal that can be used as often as you have to.
Richter sells various utility items such as Potions, Inks, basic equipment, Dye Pot and Gem Crusher.

== Location ==
; [[Heavenly Sewers]]
: When dead, use the "Enter the Light" option.
