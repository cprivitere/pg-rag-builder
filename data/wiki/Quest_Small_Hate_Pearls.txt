==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] asked me to loot Small Hate Pearls from living creatures and bring them to him. This is a repeatable daily quest offered on Wednesdays and Saturdays.

===Prerequisites===
To receive this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[Grasuul the Butler]] and is offered on Wednesdays and Saturdays.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I have need of small hate pearls. But they must be extraordinarily... fresh, and must come from a living creature. I can't use those worthless manufactured hate pearls!

Speak with Grasuul to learn what blood is fresh today. Hunt those creatures. But be warned -- some creatures are more hateful than others. I am content to wait a day or two if necessary.}}

===Requirements===
* Loot {{Item|Small Hate Pearl}} x5 from living creatures (must be looted — crafted or purchased pearls are not accepted)
* Keep the Small Hate Pearls in your inventory
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Small Hate Pearls|
{{Quote|And these all came from the still-beating hearts of living, or at least un-living, creatures? Good... good. You may go.}}

* 50 {{Favor|favor}}
* 10 Blood Oaths
}}

===Notes===
{{Spoiler|Notes|
[[Truxartis]] randomly assigns one daily Blood Oath quest per real-world day. Speak with [[Grasuul]] for guidance on which blood targets are freshest today.
}}
