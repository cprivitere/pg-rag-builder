==Summary==
Thedar said, "So, you want to be an Armorsmith do you? Let's try something a bit more advanced this time! Forge and bring me one full <b>enchanted</b> Decent Chainmail suit and I will see what I can do to help you advance your craft!

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Thedar]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Armorsmithing]] level 30. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
So, you want to be an Armorsmith do you? Let's try something a bit more advanced this time! Forge and bring me one full <b>enchanted</b> Decent Chainmail suit and I will see what I can do to help you advance your craft!

===Requirements===
* Craft a Decent Chainmail Coat (Enchanted)
* Possess a Decent Chainmail Coat
* Craft some Decent Chainmail Leggings (Enchanted)
* Possess some Decent Chainmail Leggings
* Craft a Decent Chainmail Coif (Enchanted)
* Possess a Decent Chainmail Coif
* Craft some Decent Chainmail Gloves (Enchanted)
* Possess some Decent Chainmail Gloves
* Craft some Decent Chainmail Boots (Enchanted)
* Possess some Decent Chainmail Boots
* Talk to Thedar
