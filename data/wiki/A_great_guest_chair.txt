==Summary==
Lincha in Povus said, "The orcs even stole my guest chair. It was pretty great, too. I wish I could find a replacement! The trouble is that I must know the chair's provenance. I won't support goblin slave labor!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lincha]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
The orcs even stole my guest chair. It was pretty great, too. I wish I could find a replacement! The trouble is that I must know the chair's provenance. I won't support goblin slave labor!

===Midway===
I wish I had a great new chair...

===Requirements===
* Craft Great Chair
* (Keep Great Chair)
* Talk to Lincha
