==Summary==
Gisli the stablemaster in Serbule asked you to place an order for horseshoes with the town's blacksmith, Tadion, who works in the large enclosed keep at the southern end of Serbule town.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Gisli]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Can you go tell Tadion I need more horseshoes? He's the blacksmith in town. You'll find him in the keep -- that enclosed horrible-smelling area at the southern end of town. Tell him I need as many shoes as he can make, but I can only pay in sexual favors. He'll understand.}}

===Requirements===
* Speak with Tadion
* Talk to Gisli
