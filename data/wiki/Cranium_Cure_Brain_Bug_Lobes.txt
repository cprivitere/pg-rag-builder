==Summary==
Echur in Serbule wants you to obtain some Brain Bug Lobes. They can occasionally be found from Brain Bugs.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Echur]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[The Cure For Cranium Blues]] in order to undertake this quest.
