== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Calligraphy]] is the skill of word craft. It is used by the denizens of [[Alharth]] to empower their [[Sword]] skill abilities, and write stories that can be deposited into bookcases. One such bookcase can be found in the town of [[Serbule]].

[[Calligraphy]] is a crafting skill, and thus must be leveled by use of the learned recipes. 

{{Quote|source=[[Sammie Grimspine]]|
It's the art of writing things. With pretty handwriting, basically. But it's so much more than that! Calligraphy requires intense concentration and precision, which will inevitably improve your combat skills, too, especially with the sword.

Plus, at the highest levels of skill, calligraphy becomes something even more. For me, calligraphy is a beautiful meditation on nature and the self. It opens up pathways to your own inner willpower. I promise you'll see real benefits from practicing calligraphy, even if you don't care about writing pretty.
}}

=== In-Game Description ===
The art of writing things. Handy for... writing things. It's also a valuable art for swordsmen and other warriors, who can infuse their art with potent additional effects.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Sammie Grimspine]] in [[Serbule Hills]] is often willing to introduce the art of [[Calligraphy]].
* [[Ufkar]], a Rakshasa Gadgeteer in [[Rahu]], has some skill in [[Calligraphy]]. He can raise an adventurer's skill level cap from 51-60.
* [[Liamu the Watchful]] in [[Vidaria]] raises the level cap from 61-70.

== Connected Skills ==
===== Sub-Skills: =====
* None
===== Secondary Skills: =====
* [[Sword]] - [[Calligraphy]] is a secondary skill for [[Sword]] wielders, as the practice of the Calligraphic arts can grant temporary combat combos.
* [[Knife Fighting]] - Knife fighters do not gain combos from calligraphy, but can benefit from secondary buffs to damage output (slashing/piercing)

===== Related Skills: =====
* [[Sigil Scripting]] - A skill focused more on the production of sigils, both [[Calligraphy]] and [[Sigil Scripting]] rely heavily on specialized inks.
===== Synergy Levels: =====
Bonus [[Synergy Levels]] for [[Calligraphy]] can be obtained from the following skills and levels:

:; [[Geology]] 
:: Level 40
:; [[Nature Appreciation]]
:: Level 20
:; [[Non-Fiction Writing]]

:; [[Sigil Scripting]]
:: Level 10, Level 30, Level 50.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Carpentry Benches ===
The use of Calligraphy Benches made by the [[Carpentry]] skill can increase the active time of a Calligraphy combo or insight. The training to make a calligraphy bench can be obtained from [[Paul Vaughn]].
=== Player Libraries ===
A [[Player Library]] is an intractable object often found in a city. Through the use of [[Calligraphy]], a {{Item|Blank Book}} can be transformed into a unique book at a Writing Table. These books can be submitted to player libraries across [[Alharth]].
