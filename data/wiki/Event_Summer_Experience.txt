==Summary==
It's time to enjoy summer activities! Complete the check-list before this event ends to receive a few summer reward items. (Night time activities only count after 7pm and before 5am)
==Prerequisites==
This quest begins when Event:Summertime Experience is active.
==Preface==
{{Quote|
It's time to enjoy summer! Your quest journal has a check-list of traditional summer activities. Complete them all before the event ends to receive some items that'll help you enjoy the weather.
}}

==Requirements==
<!-- Add requirements here. Use {{Item}} template for items, and link mobs. -->
* Fish up some [[Clownfish]]. x5
* Fillet the [[Clownfish]]. x5
* Cook a meal with fish in it.
* Get stung by an insect (or spider, ect.).
* Light a campfire at night.
* Dance at night. x12
* Kill rampaging tigers. x3
* Butcher a tiger for its meat.
* Make some Fireworks. (Any Type of Firework. {{Item|Small Firework}} x1, {{Item|Purple Firework}} x1, {{Item|Green Firework}} x1)
* Launch some fireworks at night x10. (Any Type of Firework. {{Item|Small Firework}}, {{Item|Purple Firework}}, {{Item|Green Firework}})
* Lastly, say 'Ahh, this is the life'
