==Summary==
Jake the Buckler in Sun Vale asked for some iridium to make his buckle shine better. He suggested that it can sometimes be mined in Kur Mountains.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Jake The Buckler]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Close Friends}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Expert's Advice]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I just can't get the buckle to shine like it needs to! You've gotta help me again. The Wombat told me about a magic rock called 'Iridium' which can make things glow. Find me some of this stuff! Womby said he mined some in the Kur Mountains one time.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Iridium}} x3
* Talk to Jake the Buckler
