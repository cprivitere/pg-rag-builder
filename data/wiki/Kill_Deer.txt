__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Kill 10 deer and return to Marna.

==Prerequisites==
To start this quest, talk to [[Marna]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You start the quest through the '''Talk''' tab instead of the '''Do Favor''' most often seen for later NPC quests.  When she offers the quest (see text below), select '''You got it!''' to accept the quest.  If you are not ready to accept the quest, select '''I'll pass. I just want to shop.''' and she will offer you the quest when you next return.

==Preface==
{{Quote|Oh, while you're here, could you help me kill some deer? I need their hides for health and armor kits. Kill 10 of 'em and I'll give you some kits for free.}}

==Requirements==
*Kill 10 Deer ([[Doe Deer]] and [[Buck Deer]]). {{Hint|Deer are found in the hills to the south and west of Serbule.}}
*Talk to Marna.

==Rewards==
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Kill Deer|

{{Quote|Thanks! By the way, I recommend using Archery or Fire Magic to kill deer.  You don't want to have to chase them down with a sword! See Velkort to learn Fire Magic.}}

*100 XP in [[First Aid]]
*100 XP in [[Armor Patching]]
}}
