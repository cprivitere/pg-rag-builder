==Summary==
Azalak the mantis asked you to find some psi mantises that have snuck into Serbule, and discreetly slay them before they bring the town's wrath down upon the mantis race.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Azalak]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|We mantises were slaves of Dalvos, alongside those idiot brain bugs, for decades. But now we are free! And we seek culture desperately. It makes many of my brethren foolish.

Several fool mantises snuck into the town of Serbule in order to steal your culture. It has been many days, and I fear they will unleash a terrible reprisal upon us all. Go and find them, and do whatever is necessary to discreetly resolve the issue. Murder would be the wisest choice. Yes. Murder them.}}

===Requirements===
* Kill Psychic Mantis Spies in Serbule {{Hint|The two psychic mantises inside the house in Serbule Keep across from the garden (entrance to PVP dungeon).}}
* Talk to Azalak
