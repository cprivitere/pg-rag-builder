==Summary==
Umarak in Povus said, "The orc invaders stole everything. Even our blankets! I've made a thin blanket from old rags, but as it gets colder, we'll need something warmer. If only sheep grew around here, I could get some wool!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Umarak]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
The orc invaders stole everything. Even our blankets! I've made a thin blanket from old rags, but as it gets colder, we'll need something warmer. If only sheep grew around here, I could get some wool!

===Midway===
If I had a lot of wool, I could prepare for winter. Without blankets, we'll freeze!

===Requirements===
* Obtain Wool x12
* Talk to Umarak
