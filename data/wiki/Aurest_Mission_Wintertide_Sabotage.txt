==Summary==
[[Lady Alethina]] in [[Red Wing Casino]] said, "I hope you're as good as you think you are, because this one is tricky. Enter the [[Fae Realm|fae realm]] near [[Sun Vale]]. There's a [[The Wintertide|Winter planeship]] there; sneak aboard. Then find some bird-creature named... Hmm, my notes say "[[Coldfeather]]" but there's also something called "[[Hotfeather]]" aboard. Kill them both, to be safe.

I also need you to open some of their storage chests and take note of the contents. You should be able to find keys aboard the ship. Good luck. Bring friends."

This [[Daily Quest]] can be repeated after 1 day.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lady Alethina]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor. This quest is only available to characters with at least two level 65 [[Skills|Combat Skills]].

===Preface===
I hope you're as good as you think you are, because this one is tricky. Enter the [[Fae Realm|fae realm]] near [[Sun Vale]]. There's a [[The Wintertide|Winter planeship]] there; sneak aboard. Then find some bird-creature named... Hmm, my notes say "[[Coldfeather]]" but there's also something called "[[Hotfeather]]" aboard. Kill them both, to be safe.

I also need you to open some of their storage chests and take note of the contents. You should be able to find keys aboard the ship. Good luck. Bring friends.

===Requirements===
* Kill [[Hotfeather]]
* Kill [[Coldfeather]]
* Unlock Loot Containers (Total from Any Deck) x5
* Unlock Loot Containers (At least 1 from Engineering Deck)
* Talk to [[Lady Alethina]]
