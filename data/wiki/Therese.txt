[[Therese]] is a gardener, and refugee from [[Dwyndarre]]. Recently, [[Therese]] decided to start growing herbs and vegetables to help feed her fellow townsfolk, and to reduce the burden on [[Ivyn]] and [[Jara]]. Unlike most Elves, [[Therese]] has no problems getting dirty, and she can often be found at the [[Serbule Keep]] garden. She maintains a stock of basic vegetable seeds for sale, as well as various types of tannin powder.

[[Therese]] became friends with [[Durstin Tallow]] and [[Brianna Willer]], residents of Serbule Hills, during her travels to find other gardeners. She thinks that [[Gretchen Salas]] is a horrible person.
