==Summary==
Loche said, "Hi there! Can you help me? I need an extra supply of spore bombs to help take care of enemies we may encounter. My melodious voice lures them in... then I blow them to pieces! Can you produce me 30 [[Nightmarish Spore Bomb]]s? It would be a great help!"

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Loche]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Mycology]] level 75. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Hi there! Can you help me? I need an extra supply of spore bombs to help take care of enemies we may encounter. My melodious voice lures them in... then I blow them to pieces! Can you produce me 30 [[Nightmarish Spore Bomb]]s? It would be a great help!

===Requirements===
* Make 10 Nightmarish Spore Bombs
* Obtain 30 Nightmarish Spore Bombs
* Talk to Loche
