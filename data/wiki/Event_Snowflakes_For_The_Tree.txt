==Summary==
[[Rick Snapley]] in [[New Prestonbule]] said, "I remember as a snotrat having to cut out hundreds of paper snowflakes for the tree each year. Tedious work, but it taught me how to use a knife! We'll need some stencils, though... otherwise they won't all be even, and then what's the point?"

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Rick Snapley]]''' in '''[[New Prestonbule Cave]]'''.

===Preface===
I remember as a snotrat having to cut out hundreds of paper snowflakes for the tree each year. Tedious work, but it taught me how to use a knife! We'll need some stencils, though... otherwise they won't all be even, and then what's the point?

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Ancient Stencil}} x3
* Talk to [[Rick Snapley]]
