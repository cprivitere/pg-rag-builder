[[Kelim]] is a male [[Rakshasa]] living in the walled town of [[Amulna]] in the [[Ilmari]] Desert. He stands in a small garden plot in the corner of the city and sells gardening supplies, as well as providing training for gardening 51-60 and 61-70.
Kelim purchased a caravan of dirt to support his pepper growing operation. He expects to be able to pay off his debt in ten to fifteen years.
