==Summary==
There's an undecorated tree in the Red Wing Casino. When fully decorated, casino employees will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This [[Winter Holiday|Ri-Shin Winter Celebration]] Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Irkima]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Get Bones for the Tree
* Get Game Chips for the Tree
* Get Pixie Sugar
* Get Red Roses to dye the Tree-Topper
* Talk to [[Irkima]]
