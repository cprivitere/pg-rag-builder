While [[Three-Eyes]] appears to be a simple beggar, his knowledge of the citizens of [[Rahu]] is unheard of. He seems to make his living serving as an information broker.
==Location==
; [[Rahu]]
: Found on the front porch of a house in the hostile area of the City of [[Rahu]].
==Rumors==
For a fee, accepted in the form of single-Council alms,  [[Three-Eyes]] is willing to provide information and insights about the citizens of Rahu.
; First Rumor
: You know the Tasty Cat, that tacky little restaurant near the docks? The [[Nishika|owner]] catered the last [[Formal Execution]], and now [[General Pask|Pask]] won't stop talking about their disgusting cat-meat dishes. He loves the place! So tacky, right?
; Second Rumor
: A funny one for you: that crazy little rak, [[Lakrea]], the one that teaches 'wellness'... she's in love with the [[Ashk|creepy soothsayer]] near the docks! I guess there's a mate out there for everybody... well, not for me, but for everybody else. ''Sniff.''
; Third Rumor
: You know that [[Lady Windsong|talking bird]] that's always snooping around? I heard it's not a bird at all. It's a d-r-u-i-d. Unconfirmed, so don't freak out. But if it's true, we're in big trouble.
; Fourth Rumor
: The [[Onkara]] went after the desert bandits again last week. I hear it didn't go too well. The bandits were better-equipped than ever before!
; Fifth Rumor
: Rumor is that the Council war with those demons isn't going so well. I hear the Rubywalls are cracking!
; Sixth Rumor
: I heard that [[General Pask]] is running out of orc prisoners to execute. Rumor is that Pask is hoping for another orcish invasion attempt so that he can keep the [[Formal Execution]] parties going. They're his favorite thing!
