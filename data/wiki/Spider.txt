== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Spider''' is a unique [[Beast Forms| Beast Form]] that can only be attained by drinking the [[Spider Juice]] potion sold by the [[Strange Spider]], who is trapped deep in [[Serbule Crypt]]. Getting out of the form is done the same way, but with the far more expensive [[Un-Spider Juice]].

Spiders serve the Goddess of Spiders and Patience, [[Akhisa]].

=== In-Game Description ===
The art of living and fighting as a spider.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* [[Strange Spider]] will sell you a potion to turn into a Spider. BE WARNED, THIS IS PERMANENT UNTIL DISPELLED. 
* [[Strange Spider]] also happens to sell a potion to undo being a Spider. If turning Spider, it might be a good idea to pick up one of these, too, so you can un-Spider when you want.
* Some NPCs, even if they will talk to most animals, will not talk to scary ones. Spiders are quite scary.
* Strange Spider also sells scrolls which teach a variety of spider abilities: Various levels of [[Spit Acid]], [[Insidious Illusion]], and [[Incubate]].
* [[Legs]] in [[Animal Town]] will unlock Spider levels 51-60, 61-70 and can teach you level 51+ abilities.
* [[Noita the Green]] unlocks Spider levels 71-80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Big Footed Jim]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* Levels 91-100 are unlocked by [[Skitter]] at [[Statehelm]].
* Prodigy levels are unlocked by [[Skitter]] at [[Statehelm]].

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Illusion ===
Using the Insidious Illusion ability, a Spider can temporarily disguise themselves as a humanoid to fool shopkeepers. This ability consumes one [[Moonstone]]. It lasts a short time and has a fairly long cooldown.
=== Riding ===
As a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]], Spiders are not able to use the traditional Horse mounts offered through [[Riding]]. Instead, [[Legs]] in [[Animal Town]] teaches mount-form to Spider players who are at least level 25, as well as the speed boost ability ''Move Those Legs''.
===Equipment===
Crafters producing equipment for use by [[Spider]]s should obtain the [[Sigil Scripting]] recipe '''Spider Inscription,''' which allows for the creation of {{Item|Spider Malachite}}. 

This attuned gemstone can be used in any recipe which calls for the use of [[:Category:Items/Crystal|Crystals]] to create equipment with [[Spider]] Treasure Effects.

There are items that only [[Spider]]s can wear. Some of them will turn you blue! (Spider Casings other than Shoddy/Rough/Crude)
