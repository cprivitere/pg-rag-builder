==Summary==
Bogaku in the Rahu Sewers said, "Even if these ratkin aren't growing crops here, they're surely making their own cheese! I need a sample of their aged cheese... perhaps it will tell us secrets. Now, when I say that, I don't mean cheese can talk. So don't be afraid. Just get the cheese."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Bogaku]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.

===Preface===
Even if these ratkin aren't growing crops here, they're surely making their own cheese! I need a sample of their aged cheese... perhaps it will tell us secrets. Now, when I say that, I don't mean cheese can talk. So don't be afraid. Just get the cheese.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Ratkin Blue Cheese
* Talk to Bogaku
