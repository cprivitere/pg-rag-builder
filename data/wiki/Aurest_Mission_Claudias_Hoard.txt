==Summary==
[[Lady Alethina]] in the [[Red Wing Casino]] said, "I have a new lead on the secret military force buried in the [[Ilmari desert]]! You're going to help me crack this once and for all. Head to the desert and find the bunker the locals call "the [[Labyrinth|labyrinth]]." It's south of [[Amulna]], and rakshasa bandits are standing right near the entrance. Once you're underground, go down a few floors to the minotaur halls, then down again, and yet again. At the lowest floor of this place is a hag named Claudia. Kill her! Bring friends -- hags are no joke.

Claudia is said to have a vast treasure trove, which you can keep! All I need are the important documents that should be inside.

I understand there's some sort of mechanism that protects her treasure. Just figure it out and get it done."

This [[Daily Quest]] can be repeated after 1 day.

===Prerequisites===
<br>To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lady Alethina]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor. This quest is only available to characters with at least two level 50 [[Skills|Combat Skills]].

===Preface===
I have a new lead on the secret military force buried in the [[Ilmari desert]]! You're going to help me crack this once and for all. Head to the desert and find the bunker the locals call "[[Labyrinth|the labyrinth]]." It's south of Amulna, and rakshasa bandits are standing right near the entrance. Once you're underground, go down a few floors to the [[Labyrinth|minotaur halls]], then down again, and yet again. At the lowest floor of this place is a hag named [[Claudia]]. Kill her! Bring friends -- hags are no joke.

[[Claudia]] is said to have a vast treasure trove, which you can keep! All I need are the important documents that should be inside.

I understand there's some sort of mechanism that protects her treasure. Just figure it out and get it done.

===Requirements===
* Travel to the [[Labyrinth]]. Lady Alethina will teleport you to the beginning of the dungeon in exchange for an [[Aquamarine]].
* Kill [[Claudia]]
* Open Claudia's Supply Chests x3
* Obtain [[Claudia's Notes]]
* Talk to [[Lady Alethina]]
