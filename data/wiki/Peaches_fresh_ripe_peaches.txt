==Summary==
Umarak in Povus said, "Tryaka's favorite snack was peaches. They always cheered her up! Pity they only grow far from here..."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Umarak]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
Tryaka's favorite snack was peaches. They always cheered her up! Pity they only grow far from here...

===Midway===
I wish I had some peaches to give to Tryaka.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Peaches x20
* Talk to Umarak
