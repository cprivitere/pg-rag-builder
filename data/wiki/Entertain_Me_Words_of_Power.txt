==Summary==
Pennoc in Serbule told you to say words of power. (You can research words of power when you have several levels of the Lore skill, which you can obtain by examining lore items in the world.)

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pennoc]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Once I heard a wizard say a weird word -- just some gibberish -- and then they disappeared! Do you know any magic words? Say some for me!

* Say Words of Power
