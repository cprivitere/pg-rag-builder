==Summary==
Rappanel in Serbule Hills said, "So, do you have those filleted grapefish for me yet?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Rappanel]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[The Grapefish Roundup]] in order to undertake this quest.
