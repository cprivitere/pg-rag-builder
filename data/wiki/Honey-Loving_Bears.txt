==Summary==
Crelpin in the Fae Realm said, "We've managed to get the bears out of the western bee fields, but south and east of here, they're a real problem! They're especially dense during the day when they hunt for honey. Yes, they eat my damned crop! Why can't they be more like the panthers?! Anyway, you just gotta stop 'em. They're a menace!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Crelpin]]''' in '''[[Fae Realm]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
