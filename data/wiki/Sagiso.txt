[[Sagiso]] is a priest of [[Errana]] working in the city of [[Statehelm]]. He is proficient in [[Mentalism]] and enjoys a variety of hobbies.
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Sagiso]] can be found in his room at the top floor of the Elven Apartment after 7:30pm.
