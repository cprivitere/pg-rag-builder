==Summary==
Glortok in the Rahu Sewers said, "Nevermind subtlety. We want them to stop mining, so just destroy their undead miners!"

This Quest can be repeated after 66 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chief Glortok]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Goblin Avenger: Ratkin Necro-Shamans]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
Nevermind subtlety. We want them to stop mining, so just destroy their undead miners!

===Requirements===
* Kill Undying Ratkin Miners x15
* Talk to Glortok
