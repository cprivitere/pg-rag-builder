{{Quote|Welcome to Animal Town. I am Raul, and I am pleased to meet you.}}
[[Raul]] is a bull, one of the first inhabitants of the Town. It was his idea to install [[Spot]] as a figurehead mayor, although that plan backfired somewhat. He's also wisely cautious around spiders!

As a resident of [[Animal Town]], [[Raul]] is only willing to hold conversations with characters that have transformed into a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]] over 180 real-world minutes prior to the conversation attempt. If you loose track of the time you last transformed, [[Norbert]] is willing to tell you how long you have until the other residents will tolerate talking with you.
