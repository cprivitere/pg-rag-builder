[[Agrashab]] is the god of Trolls, and a fae spirit that maintains an unusually broad active region of influence, extending to over 600 fae worlds. Agrashab maintains a seat on the [[Fairy Court]], but is in conflict with the [[Winter Queen]]. [[Agrashab]] presents himself through avatars in his various realms, including [[Agrashab/Sun Vale|Sun Vale]] and the [[Agrashab/Fae Realm|Fae Realm]].

== Lore ==
[[Agrashab]] is an active spirit, spreading his influence wherever Trolls can be found.
