==April 13 Update==
<b>If you were logged in when this update occurred, shut down your client and restart the launcher so that you patch the client!</b>
: This is a bug-fix update:
: - Summoned pets once again show up in the pet window
: - The water is once again visible in Serbule
: - Beakers can once again be used to activate [[Battle Chemistry]]
: - Pets in "Assist" mode no longer provoke fights with nearby monsters (that aren't already fighting)
: - lowered the volume on most of the gemshorn instrument tracks
: - equipment that boosted the fire damage from Fire Arrow accidentally lit you, the archer, on fire
: - The various oils were much rarer than intended. They now drop more often and from more types of creatures. Mid-range monsters are more likely to drop Crossing Oil and Uncrossing Oil. Higher-level monsters are more likely to drop the Fear Oil and Hate Oil. (Hope Oil remains quite rare and can only drop from extremely dangerous creatures, like [[Lamia]]s and [[Basilisk Controller]]s.)
: - a lore item near [[Tremor]] had gotten buried in a desk and could only be tab-selected
: - Tweaked the wording on [[Nightshade]]'s quests to make it easier to understand that you need to COMPLETE those quests (by going back to Nighshade) in order to be eligible to learn those NPC's special craft skills. (They are unusual in that they won't teach you their craft skill just by leveling up their Favor : -- you need Nightshade's blessing also.)
: - Player spiders can now use the /dance [1-6] emotes, and have a jump animation again
: - added a coin-value to various practice-recipe junk items so they can be sold
