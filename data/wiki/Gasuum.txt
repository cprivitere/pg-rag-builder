== Training ==
==== Interpretive Dance Variant ====
[[Gasu'um]] is the teacher for [[Interpretive Dance|Dance 6 (variant B)]]. At least three players need to be performing (dancing or playing music) nearby, this can be accomplished with 1 instrument and 2 dancers near [[Gasu'um]] to convince them to demonstrate their dance. If [[Gasu'um]] is in an non-idle animation, keeping her dialogue window open will allow her to dance.
