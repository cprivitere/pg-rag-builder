==Summary==
Irkima in Red Wing Casino said, "Please speak to Tavilak about her previous mates -- the ones she killed. Why did she do it? Is this why she is unwilling to mate with me?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Irkima]]''' in '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor. The quest [[Letting Qatik Down Gently]] must have been completed.
