==Summary==
While angling in Povus near orcish land, a strange voice asked you to help kill Old Dreadtooth. Apparently it's a pretty big fish, and it only comes out at midnight.
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, angle by the orc camp in '''[[Povus]]'''.
