[[Shovel Dispenser]] is a pale goat summoned by [[Developers|otherworldly forces]] in times of need. As the name suggests, this goat can give the gift of a {{Item|Shovel}}.
== Location ==
; [[Fae Realm]] 
: The [[Shovel Dispenser]] was summoned to the [[Fae Realm]] in March of 2020 to aid newly created fairies.
== Favor ==
[[Shovel Dispenser]] does not use the favor system.
== Services == 
==== Shovel Dispensing ==== 
If spoken to without having a {{Item|Shovel}} in your inventory, the [[Shovel Dispenser]] goat will gift one to you.
