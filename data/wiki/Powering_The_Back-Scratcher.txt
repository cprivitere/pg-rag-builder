==Summary==
Furlak in Rahu asked you to bring him some Dwarven N Cores. He claims they can be found in ruins deep in the Ilmari desert.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Furlak]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
