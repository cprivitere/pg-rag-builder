== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Textile Creation''' focuses on processing Cotton. Many of the items it creates are required for [[Tailoring]], and later recipes will increase the amount of cloth you make per cotton. A high level in Textile Creation is strongly recommended for those who plan to do a lot of tailoring.

=== In-Game Description ===
The craft of turning raw ingredients into cloth. Textile creation includes cotton, wool, silk, and many other fiber types. Cloth made from animal hides (e.g. leather) comes from the Tanning skill instead.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===

=== Carded Cotton Creation ===
Most recipes require Carded Cotton, or require a different textile that requires Carded Cotton to make. Some recipes require Perfect Cotton, which you might also generate while carding cotton. At first, you can only created Carded Cotton by hand, with no chance of Perfect Cotton. As you gain levels and buy better Carded Cotton recipes, the potential amount of Perfect Cotton increases. At Master (level 45), you can take advantage of a recipe that changes the Cotton requirement from 5 to 4, giving you a 25% boost to production and more perfect cotton than with Expert (level 25).

There are also possible buffs from dance exposure and from certain floral arrangements which can further increase the chances for Perfect Cotton.
