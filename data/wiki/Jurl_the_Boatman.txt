'''Jurl the Boatman''' offers you a ferry to the strange island of [[Sun Vale]]. His dialogue options will tell you a little bit about the place.

If the player asks ''"Where else can you take me?"'', the '''Boatman''' can return the player to [[Anagoge Island]] by going through the dialogue prompts for 300 Councils. First time travelling to either location as an animal will incur an additional one time fee.
