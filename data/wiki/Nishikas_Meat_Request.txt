==Summary==
Nishika in Rahu asked you to obtain a large amount of meat from grimalkins in the desert.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Nishika]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

{{Quote|My specialty is cat-meat recipes. A lot of visitors expect the meat to be disgusting, but with the right seasoning, it's quite good! And plentiful, too ... or at least it usually is.
