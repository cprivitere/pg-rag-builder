==Activity Quest==
[[Gargoyle Invasion Thwarted]] is a group of [[Activity Quests]] which do not appear in the quest log. These three almost identical quests share the name of '''Gargoyle Invasion Thwarted''' with the only noticeable difference being a change in their InternalName (Vidaria_GargoyleInvasion1, Vidaria_GargoyleInvasion2, Vidaria_GargoyleInvasion3). They have identical rewards.

===Prerequisites===
This [[Activity Quests|Activity Quest]] is automatically completed when a Gargoyle-related Ecological Disaster is prevented.
