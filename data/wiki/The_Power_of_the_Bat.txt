==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] asked me to gather ingredients for an inscription ceremony in [[Sun Vale]]. Completing this quest rewards the [[Bat Form]] ability.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[The Strength Potion's Strength]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|The potion worked! I feel stronger. Healthier. More virile. I can feel my powers returning! I just need to unlock... them again.

Help me with the unlocking ritual, and I shall unlock the power of the bat for you, too!

I just need some supplies for the inscription ceremony.

Now where did I leave the ghostly guano? Ah, of course. On an island known as Sun Vale, look for a ruin. That should be enough to go on, right? Oh, yes: some of those grim little Winter Fae like to... frolic nearby.

For the other ingredients, I'm certain your... cosmopolitan upbringing will aid you in their procurance.}}

===Requirements===
* Touch Vampire Rune in [[Sun Vale]] (in the ruins)
* Obtain {{Item|Ghostly Guano}} x2
* Obtain {{Item|Guano}} x2
* Obtain {{Item|Acur Blood}} x1
* Obtain {{Item|Toxic Frog Skin}} x1
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]
