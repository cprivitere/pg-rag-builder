==Summary==
There's an undecorated tree in Animal Town near Spot's tent. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Spot]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. This quest is only available to long time animals.

===Requirements===
* Get Onions for the Tree
* Get Sausages for the Tree
* Get Plates for the Tree
* Get Cheshire for the Tree-Topper
* Talk to [[Spot]]
