==Summary==
<i>Zhia Lian:</i> It's deathbat breeding season! Oh, you mortals call them flapskulls. That nickname makes Kajich so angry! They were supposed to be scary! But your nickname ruined it and he abandoned them. So I fixed up his creation: I gave them the ability to breed. It was a good idea given the situation at the time, I promise! But less good now. Anyway, they're showing up at night near a small lake in the Ilmari desert. They don't come out during the day, so you'll have to pull an all-nighter for this one.

This Quest can be repeated after 60 days.
