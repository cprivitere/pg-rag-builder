'''Alharth''' is a continent home to a wide variety of races, and holds many climates. The Human empire of the Council Lands makes up most of the continent, and is large enough that many of its residents believe that '''Alharth''' is the name for the entire world. The Elven country of [[Verta]], the Rakshasa empire, and the [[Crone Hegemony]] border the [[Council Lands]].
