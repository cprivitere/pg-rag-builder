==Summary==
George Madler has asked you to kill a goblin named Algapa. George thinks this goblin can be found in the dungeons beneath Eltibule Keep.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[George Madler]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|The second goblin was the ship captain that held my wife as a slave. But that ship sank. So instead, I'll take revenge on his son, Algapa. Seems fair, right?

And best of all, his son is right here! He's trying to make a name for himself under Eltibule Keep! I bet it'll be no trouble to kill him. Well... probably a <i>little</i> trouble. But it would help me sleep at night.}}

===Requirements===
* Kill the Goblin named Algapa
* Talk to George Madler
