__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Pennoc in Serbule told you to trade with another player. (Use the Trade option when examining another player. You don't have to actually trade items -- just so long as one or the other of you gets at least one item, that'll count.)

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pennoc]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Serbule desperately needs more traveling circuses to show up. But we're too small. So we need to facilitate trade! Go: go trade! Trade with other adventurers like yourself, so that the word starts to spread.}}

===Requirements===
* Trade With Another Player
* Talk to Pennoc
