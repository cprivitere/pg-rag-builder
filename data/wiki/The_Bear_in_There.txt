==Summary==
Jara's barn has been coopted by a huge grizzly bear. She'd be grateful if I could kill it, but she doesn't really think I'm capable of doing so.

The bear can be found in the shed just west of Jara. It does have a respawn timer, so it may not be there immediately upon arrival.

==Prerequisites==
To start this quest, talk to [[Jara]] west of [[Serbule Keep]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

==Preface==
{{Quote|Holy mother of pearl, do NOT go in there. BEAR! A huge one! In my barn! What am I going to do? I made that barn myself! It's my only one! And it ate my chickens!}}

==Requirements==
* Kill Bear in Barn. {{Hint|The bear is quite tough.  Make sure you're strong enough, or perhaps bring some friends!}}
* Talk to Jara.
