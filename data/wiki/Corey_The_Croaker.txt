[[Corey The Croaker]]is a [[Spring Court]] fairy currently hiding in the [[Winter Nexus]].

Corey dislikes the Summer and [[Autumn Court]] stereotype that [[Spring Court]] fae are insane. He is helping free the captured [[Summer Court]] fae, because the [[Winter Queen]] has captured the [[Spring King]].

Talking to Corey with a ''Fairy Wing'' will result in a dialogue option to give him the wing. In return, you gain 5 favor and Corey places a ''Flower Appreciation'' buff that boosts your Max Power by 63 for one hour.
