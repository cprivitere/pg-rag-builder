[[Amutasa]] is a renowned [[Toolcrafting|Tool maker]] who has set up shop in the City of [[Rahu]]. She mentions that she is often up every night working tirelessly on new tools to fufil orders. One day, she hopes to make custom armor using dyed snail shells.

== Services ==
==== Consignment ====
; Amutasa offers [[Consignment]] at {{favor|Friends}}.
: Mark-up fee of 10%, no stocking fee.
: Max 1 active consignments (Tools, alchemy ingredients).
: Max 4 consignments per week.

==== Augmentation ====
[[Amutasa]] can install Level 0-60 Augments for 650 Councils. You must be {{favor|Friends}} or better.
