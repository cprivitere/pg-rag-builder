==Summary==
Lakrea told you to work the word 'truculent' into your next conversation.

Truculent: adjective: eager to argue or quick to start a fight. Similar to 'belligerent' but with an implication of cruelty or savagery.

(Note: if you use the word out of context, the game won't know, but <i>you'll</i> know that you lost 10,000 Roleplay Points.)

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lakrea]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Holistic Wellness: 'Confidant']] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|You need to expand your useful vocabulary. Not your total vocabulary, your <i>useful</i> vocabulary. You know thousands of words that you never think to use in daily life. Am I right? Of course I'm right. Here's a word to use: 'truculent'.

<i>[ Truculent: adjective: eager to argue or quick to start a fight. Similar to 'belligerent' but with an implication of cruelty or savagery. ]</i>

Just find a place to work that into your next conversation. But don't use it out of context! Use it right!}}

===Requirements===
* Use 'Truculent' In a Conversation
* Talk to Lakrea
