==Summary==
Julius Patton in Serbule Hills said, "I used to grow veggies myself, but I don't move as smoothly as I used to. Gotta save my energy for the grapes! Which means I'm in a bit of an embarrassing situation: I need to restock my larder with onions. Can you grow some for me?"

===Prerequisites===
