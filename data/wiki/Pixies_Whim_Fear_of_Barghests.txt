==Summary==
Uriana in the Fae Realm said, "Yesterday I experienced fear. Why should I suffer fear?! I am immortal! Yet I did fear. I was hunted by another immortal: a true barghest, and I was afraid! I slew it, but I knew it would return, and so my fear only grew! I do not like fear. I do not like it! Go and find the barghest's new body and vanquish my fear with your actions!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Uriana]] in the [[Fae Realm]]. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.
