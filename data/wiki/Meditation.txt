== Meditation Overview==
[[Meditation]] is a skill used by adventurers of all varieties, although [[Unarmed]] users will find the greatest benefits. Through brief [[Meditation]] sessions at [[Meditation Pillar]]s and Arches across Alharth, adventurers can learn temporary attack combinations or receive boosts. Travelers to the frozen [[Kur Mountains]] region will find great use from the Cold Resistance provided by Meditation Orcish #15 and #53.  Meditation does benefit from bonuses to crafting experience gain.
=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in centering yourself and creating inner focus. This is especially helpful when using Unarmed combat skill. To meditate, you must be near a Meditation Pillar. Each meditation also requires a simple focus item, such as a fruit or flower.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Meditation Pillar]]s will grant access to the skill when first touched.
* [[Echur]] offers some tips about Meditation, and Hang Out options that grant experience.
* [[Ashk the Answerer]] unlocks levels 51 - 60 and teaches Meditations of similar level.

=== Unarmed Meditation Combos ===
Adventurers skilled in [[Unarmed]] combat may seek out [[Meditation]] to receive bonuses that activate when abilities are used in a particular order. There are three sets of [[Meditation Combos]] granted through [[Meditation]]. After the third [[Meditation]] per day, only the Recipe Combos will be received.
* [[Meditation Combos#Pillar Combos|Pillar Combos]] - Determined by the Meditation Pillar or Arch you use.
* [[Meditation Combos#Daily Combos|Daily Combos]] - Determined by day, with ten rotating bonuses.
* [[Meditation Combos#Recipe Combos|Recipe Combos]] - Granted through the use of a recipe.

=== Meditation Pillars of Alharth and Beyond ===
Across [[Alharth]], [[Meditation Pillar]]s have been placed in places well suited for [[Meditation]], however not always with the safety of the meditator in mind. Functionally identical '''Meditation Arches''' appear in fairy-associated regions such as [[Sun Vale]] and the [[Fae Realm]].

* [[Anagoge]] - 0 Meditation Pillars
* [[Serbule]] - 5 Meditation Pillars
* [[Serbule Hills]] - 5 Meditation Pillars
* [[Eltibule]] - 4 Meditation Pillars
* [[Red Wing Casino]] - 1 Meditation Pillar
* [[Kur Mountains]] - 5 Meditation Pillars
* [[Sun Vale]] - 5 Meditation Arches
* [[Ilmari]] - 2 Meditation Pillars
* [[Rahu]] - 4 Meditation Pillars
* [[Gazluk]] - 4 Meditation Pillars
* [[Fae Realm]] - 6 Meditation Arches
* [[Povus]] - 4 Meditation Pillars
