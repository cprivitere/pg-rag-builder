Elaina in Sun Vale's Animal Town asked you to get her some myconian jelly.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Elaina]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
