[[Moxie]] is the Jeweler of the [[Crafting Caravan]]. The repeatable quests [[Moxie]] offers give {{Item|Crafter's Guild Token}} as rewards. These tokens are bartered with [[Orran]] during the event.
== Location ==
; [[Red Wing Casino]]
: [[Moxie]] can be encountered during the [[Crafting Caravan]] event.

==Special Bartering==
After completing [[Event: Bonus - Moxie Needs Metal Slabs]], the player can give Moxie an exceptional (red) or better {{Item|Amulet of the Rugged Traveler}} and receive {{Item|Moxie's Advanced Crafting Token}}. The player may then barter this advanced crafting token along with a number of {{Item|Crafter's Guild Token}} to receive a higher level {{Item|Enhanced Amulet of the Rugged Traveler}}.  The number of Crafter's Guild Tokens that must be given is equal to half the level of the new Amulet of the Rugged Traveler (30 tokens for level 60, 35 for level 70, or 40 for level 80).  The new amulet received can be of different rarity from the one given to Moxie.
