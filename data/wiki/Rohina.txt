I am the master of mind over matter. Or at least I thought I was, before becoming a druid.

[[Rohina]] is a mentalist trainer in Sun Vale that is struggling with her life as a druid. She can train Mentalism 51-60 and abilities in that range. As an example of her skills, she grew a reasonably large tree in one day.

{{Quote|Grow, damn you!

Not you! This tree! I'm losing to [[the Wombat]]!

'''Beg pardon?'''

The other [[Druid|druids]] were showing off. [[Silvia]] filled her home with flowers, [[Bernard]] grew a wall of palm trees to surround his bed. I showed off how quickly I could grow a tree. Grew this one in a day!

'''Impressive!'''

Except then [[the Wombat]] laughed and grew that other tree there, even taller than mine, in half the time. And... I can't make mine grow any more! Damn you, tree!

'''Huh. Well, can I help?'''

You're not even a [[druid]]! How could you help? No, no. Leave me to this! I can do it! Stop doubting me!

'''Okay...'''
}}
