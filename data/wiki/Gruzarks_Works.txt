==Summary==
Helena Veilmoor in Hogan's Keep asked you to get something written by the poet [[Gruzark]]. She didn't seem to know where Gruzark lives, though.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Helena]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
