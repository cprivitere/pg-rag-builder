''Previous quest: [[The Stolen Lens]] first quest of Chain A''

==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] sent me to [[Khyrulek's Crypt]] beneath [[Serbule]] to find a Tuvillic Rune and retrieve Khyrulek's Planar Planner. Completing this quest also unlocks Chain B.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[The Stolen Lens]] and receiving the Vampirism skill from [[Truxartis]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|Listen carefully.

Seek a lich named Khyrulek underneath a town called Serbule. The lich... isn't there. But there may be... guardians.

This lich had a planar guide which I need. It will be hidden in a trove of Tuvillus artifacts.

If I recall... you can find it in her sleeping quarters.

Look for a rune of Tuvillus -- a red sigil. It will be... on the wall.

Touch it to reveal the hoard. Bring me back Khyrulek's Planar Planner.}}

===Requirements===
* Touch Vampire Rune in [[Khyrulek's Crypt]] (beneath [[Serbule]])
* Obtain {{Item|Khyrulek's Planar Planner}}
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Khyrulek's Planar Planner|
{{Quote|Yes! This is her precious book of...wait...it's in code. Can you read this?

Nor can I. We have more work to do. Let me... think.}}

* 50 {{Favor|favor}}
* 10 Blood Oaths
* Unlocks [[Deciphering the Planar Planner]] (Chain A continues)
* Unlocks [[Reinvigorating the Mind]] (Chain B — can run in parallel with Chain A)
}}

===Notes===
