==Summary==
[[Qatik]] in [[Red Wing Casino]] said, "I've had [[Irkima]] study the available lore on the fae realm and he found an interesting detail. Some of the leaders of the Winter Queen's forces are granted the title of 'Puck', along with associated powers. And to prove their puckhood they are given a badge made of ice. Or crystal. Or icy crystal. Anyway, we need some of those badges. They may hold the secret to infinite power!

It has taken much influence on my part, but I've convinced the teleportation attendant to send you to the [[Winter Nexus]] itself. But go quickly before he tires of maintaining the link to such a distant place!

<i>[A group of 4+ level 40+ players recommended.]</i>"

This [[Daily Quest]] can be repeated after 1 day.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Qatik]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
I've had [[Irkima]] study the available lore on the [[fae realm]] and he found an interesting detail. It has taken much influence on my part, but I've convinced the teleportation attendant to send you to the Winter Nexus itself. But go quickly before he tires of maintaining the link to such a distant place!

<i>[A group of 4+ level 40+ players recommended.]</i>

===Requirements===
* Travel to the [[Winter Nexus]]. The [[Teleportation Attendant]] in the [[Red Wing Casino]] will teleport you directly there while the daily quest is being offered.
* Kill [[Dementia Puck]] and Retrieve the {{Item|Puckish Badge}}
* Kill [[Despair Puck]] and Retrieve the {{Item|Puckish Badge}}
* Kill [[Bonfire Puck]] and Retrieve the {{Item|Puckish Badge}}
* Talk to [[Qatik]]
