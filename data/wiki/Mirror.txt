[[Mirror]] is a seemingly sentient mirror that can communicate by displaying scrolling text on its glass. The [[Mirror]] was once a cultist of [[Zhia Lian]] named Rick.

== Location ==
; [[Gazluk Keep]]
: [[Mirror]] can be found during the [[Seasonal Event]] of [[Halloween]], near [[Melandria]].
==Quests==
[[Mirror]] offers no quest directly.
* The quest to find [[Mirror]], [[Zhia Lian Event: Help Rick the Mirror]], is offered by [[Brandon Mellus]].
* The quest to save [[Mirror]], [[Zhia Lian Event: The Mirror Breaker]], is offered by [[Melandria]].

== Events ==
===== Halloween =====
[[Mirror]] has appeared during [[Halloween 2019]] and [[Halloween 2020]].
