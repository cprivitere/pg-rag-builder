[[Ogamboe]] is giant who pays councils in exchange for the completion of [[Industry|Work Orders]] in the [[Fae Realm]].  The associated Work Order Board is far to the north, in the [[Fae Realm#Fel-Dasculan Ruins|Fel-Dasculan Ruins]]. He is only present at night.

{{Quote|Hello! I am Ogamboe the Deal Hunter. You may have heard of my famous boutique, but I'm not here to sell things. I'm here to buy things! In particular, I am buying very special orders for my customers.

I've set up a work order board near the fairies north of here. If you have any of the things I need, bring them to me! Don't worry, I pay in "Councils"!

Oh, but I can't stay here during the day. I don't want to get on the Winter Queen's bad side! I'll show up whenever the queen's nighttime reappears. Wait for me here.
}}
