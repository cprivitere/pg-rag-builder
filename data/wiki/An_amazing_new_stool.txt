==Summary==
Lincha in Povus said, "The orcs destroyed all my beautiful furniture when they attacked Povus. I miss my meditation stool so badly! It was amazing! I would love to have a new one, but the furniture sold in Rahu is made by goblin slave-labor. Many in Rahu turn a blind eye to slavery, but I won't! My furniture has to be made by a crafter I know!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lincha]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime.
