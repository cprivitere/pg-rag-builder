The Floriculturist still has a lot of missing memories, and requires reminders of his past life in order to train you in most things. These reminders take the form of 'Memory Notes'.

Memory Notes are acquired by first finding and inspecting [[:Category:Items/UninspectedMemento|Mementos]], an item just like any other. Once you inspect a Memento, you will receive a hint about which NPC in the world can identify it for you. Gift it to that NPC in exchange for a [[Memory Note]]. But be careful: gifting it to the wrong NPC will lose it permanently!

== Location ==
Meditating on a small flying rock in [[Vidaria]]. To talk to [[The Floriculturist]] you need level 50 in [[Corpse Talking]].
Each conversation can be initiatiated with one [[Fetish Bag]].

== Conversations == 

To talk to [[The Floriculturist]] you need level 50 in [[Corpse Talking]].
Each conversation can be initiatiated with one [[Fetish Bag]].

{{Quote|source=[[The Floriculturist]]|
You... can speak? To me?

'''I've spoken with plenty of undead before. Now, what do you have to teach me?'''

Me? ... Teach? ... You?

I don't think so. I am not here to teach, I am here to ... learn.

'''That sounds pretty deep for a skeleton.'''

Deep... yes... that's what I am. That's what I <i>was</i>... But no longer, I have been given a second chance at life... and what a beautiful life it is!

'''What do you mean, you were deep?'''

Deep... under the ground. But recently I was unearthed... Risen... And now, I am here.

'''Now you're here, and you think life is beautiful?'''

Of course I do! How could you not? Take a look around... Have you ever seen such beauty, from such a wonderful vantage point? It is... <i>breathtaking</i>.

'''Are you sure it's not death that took your breath?'''

I suppose you are <i>technically</i> correct. But that only means I am more... experienced in breathtaking experiences. So believe me when I tell you that this view is extraordinary.

'''Sure. So, what happened to your first chance at life?'''

That... I do not know. I was buried deep. For how long? It's unclear; It could be weeks, it could be centuries. My memories... have faded.

'''You really don't remember anything?'''

Not a single thing. Not when I was born, not what I did with my life... not even my own name.

'''Perhaps I can help you.'''

Help me? To what... to reclaim my memories?

'''Either that, or help you get back down to the ground.'''

Absolutely not! I quite enjoy the view from up here. But my memories, on the other hand...

Yes, if you can help me in some way, I accept. And who knows, I may even be able to... teach you a thing or two from my past life.

'''Great. Any idea how I can help?'''

Well, I died here, or my body was carried here.. so here might be a good place to start. Perhaps you can look around for something meaning full.

'''And then bring it back to you?'''

Well, no... I doubt that seeing a mere item will jog my memory.. but perhaps if you can find something meaningful, you can seek out someone who <i>does</i> know its importance, and they can inform you.

'''That sounds sensible. I'll return when I have more info.'''}}

You receive the quest [[Undead Memories]].

{{Quote|source=[[The Floriculturist]]|

Welcome back... Do you know who I am yet?

'''What am I supposed to be doing again?'''

Hunt around in the immediate vicinity for anything... meaningful, then find someone who can tell you about it. Should be... simple, right?

'''Got it. I'll get back when I have all the info.'''}}

Upon completing [[Undead Memories]].

{{Quote|source=[[The Floriculturist]]|

Welcome back... Do you know who I am yet?

'''Yes, I've finished investigating.'''

Wonderful. Who am I?

'''You are an elven florist that's been dead for 10,000 years.'''

Ten... thousand? I suppose that explains the memory loss.

[The skeleton pauses for a moment and looks off to the side.]

Yes, now that you mention it I do remember the flowers... So many bouquets I must have put together thousands of them...

'''You are also a landscape painter.'''

A painter... Yes!

[The skeleton's skull turns to look over to the horizon.]

I captured so many beautiful vistas in my life... but never any quite so stunning as this one.

'''That's all the info I have.'''

I see. While I'm sure there <i>are</i> more details, these that you've brought me have already... reawakened me.

Thank you... I may have a thing or two I can teach you, now.

'''Splendid.'''
}}
