==Summary==
Sammie Grimspine in Serbule Hills said, "I heard that goblins took the logging camps to the northeast... and that they're using fairies as slaves! So terrible! Now, I could never condone killing anybody, but IF a slave were to HAPPEN to perish, and if they had cold iron shackles in good condition, and IF those shackles made it back to me, I would be very grateful. Not that I want you to kill anybody, though! No no no. Just... be on the lookout for cold iron shackles. That's all."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Sammie Grimspine]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
