==Summary==
A goblin in the Rahu Sewers said that you would be allowed entrance to the goblins' sanctuary if you retrieve a lost notebook. He said it was in the "ratkin nesting warrens", and not too far from their hideout.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Goblin Door Guard]]''' in '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Bogaku's Notebook
* Talk to Goblin Guard
