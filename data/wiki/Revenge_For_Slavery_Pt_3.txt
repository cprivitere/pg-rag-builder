==Summary==
George Madler asked you to kill a bear named Graz. He doesn't know where Graz lives, though, except that it's likely in a cave filled with goblins.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[George Madler]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|The third person I need revenge on isn't really a goblin at all. It's a bear. A bear named Graz, famous for eating disobedient slaves. I lost track of where they'd moved Graz. There were some cave-ins around here recently, and the tribe that owned him is missing. Hell, Graz may be dead already. I just need to know Graz will never hurt another slave. Can you make sure of that?}}

===Requirements===
* Kill the bear named Graz
* Talk to George Madler
