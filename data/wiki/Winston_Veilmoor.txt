[[Winston Veilmoor]] is the proprietor of the famous Veilmoor Blacksmith shop, which operates out of eastern [[Statehelm]]. He can teach level 81-90 & 91-100 [[Blacksmithing]], [[Bladesmithing]], and [[Armorsmithing]].
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: Found in the Veilmoor shop of the eastern market.
