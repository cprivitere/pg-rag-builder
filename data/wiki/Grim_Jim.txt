[[Grim Jim]] is a merchant in the city of [[Statehelm]].
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Grim Jim]] can be found in the marketplace of Statehelm. His stand is among the north-most row of stalls.

Name's Jim. Not that anyone believes me.

'''What was that? Nobody believes you?'''

Another Jim is pretending to be me. He stole my food stall. And worse.

'''Worse?'''

He stole my contacts! My sellers. I can't buy fresh produce anymore because they sell it all to him. I had to start all over. Now I buy and sell meat and skins. It's good quality, but I hate it. I used to be a vegetarian. Anyway, it's old news. Take a look at my wares.}}
{{Quote|source=[[Grim Jim]]|
You never really know who your friends truly are until you're replaced by a doppleganger who takes over your life. Almost everyone I knew sided with him over me. 

And the worst part is that when I got angry and depressed over it, they started calling me "Grim Jim."

I'm trying to get over it, but it's fucking hard, okay.}}
