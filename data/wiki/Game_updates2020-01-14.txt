==January 14 Hotfix==
The server was rebooted to fix a bug:
: - Fixed the Warden intro sub-quest "The Warden Test", which couldn't be completed in the new Sun Vale

It also included these less-important fixes which happened to be ready:
: - All crafted belts now have a chance to be Uncommon (instead of non-magical). That chance is 33%, 66%, or 100%, depending on the difficulty of the recipe. (Previously, only a select few belt recipes could craft magical belts.)
: - Crafted bat witch hats have better chances of being higher-rarity
