== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Artistry]] is used to create Jack-o'-Lanterns and Fireworks. 
=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in the visual arts, including painting, sculpting, and mosaic creation.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* {{Item|How To Be An Artist}} - This book teaches [[Artistry]], and requires Level 25 or higher [[Art History]] to read.

* {{Item|Recipe: Small Fireworks}} - This book, often dropped by Goblins during a summer celebration, will teach [[Artistry]] its readers.

*{{Item|Art: Jack O' Lantern}} - This recipe scroll, dropped during Halloween events, will teach [[Artistry]] to its readers.

== Connected Skills ==
===== Sub-Skills: =====
* None
===== Secondary Skills: =====
* [[Art History]] - Level 25 Art History may be required to learn Artistry if a {{Item|How To Be An Artist}} is used.
===== Related Skills: =====
* [[Blacksmithing]] - While [[Artistry]] is the study of sculpting, actual sculptures are currently crafted through [[Blacksmithing]].
