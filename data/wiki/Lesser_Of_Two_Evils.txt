==Summary==
Ferris Blueheart in Vidaria said, "You've been incredibly helpful in my research thus far, but we've only been dealing with the minions. I still have many questions about the higher ranks of the demons. We should start by examining the large ones. I've noticed that one of them seems more in-charge than the other, despite no obvious physical differences. The brains have been so helpful in the past, and you've proven that you're clearly capable of extracting them, so bring me some from each, if you don't mind."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ferris Blueheart]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. The quest [[The Swarmer Hypothesis]] must be completed first
