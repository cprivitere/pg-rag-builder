==Activity Quest==
[[Scorpion Nest Destroyed]] is a group of [[Activity Quests]] which do not appear in the quest log. These four almost identical quests share the name of '''Scorpion Nest Destroyed''' with the only noticeable difference being a change in their InternalName (Vidaria_ScorpionNest1, Vidaria_ScorpionNest2, Vidaria_ScorpionNest3, Vidaria_ScorpionNest4). They have identical rewards.

===Prerequisites===
This [[Activity Quests|Activity Quest]] is automatically completed when a scorpion-related Ecological Disaster is prevented.
===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Scorpion Nest Destroyed|
{{Quote|
You destroyed the erupting nest of Tunnel Scorpions!}}
* 10 [[Vidaria Renown]].
* 1 point for Serbule Community Chest.
}}
