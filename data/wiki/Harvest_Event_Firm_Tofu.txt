==Summary==
Braigon in Eltibule said, "Tofu can be made in three main ways. Firm tofu is used when the recipe calls for a protein that won't fall apart! I can teach you how to make it in exchange for a fresh Brunost cheese."
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Braigon]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''.  The quest is available during the [[Fall Harvest]]. The quest [[Harvest Event: Not Quite Meat]] must be completed first.
