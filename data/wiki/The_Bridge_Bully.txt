==Summary==
Jumper in Sun Vale's Animal Town asked you to defeat the Bridge Bully, a ranalan that lives in the river near a natural crossing point.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Jumper]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Over on the little island northeast of here there's this tiny lake with a bridge. Very cozy. It's a famous make out spot for talking birds. Well, regular birds too, I guess. But now there's a ranalon hiding there, using it as his personal hunting ground. It's not right! That island isn't even in ranalon territory!

We can't let him camp there and kill our friends. But he runs whenever he sees me bouncing toward him. He doesn't know you, so you should be able to get him. So go get him!}}

===Requirements===
* Kill the Ranalan Bully
* Talk to Jumper
