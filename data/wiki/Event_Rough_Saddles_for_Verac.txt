==Summary==
Verac said, "Ho there! Making tack for horses is very important work! I'm in need of some extra gear while I'm waiting on Tantai to replenish my leather stock. Can you bring me 10 [[Rough Saddle]]s? In exchange I'll share a couple trade secrets with you to help hone your craft.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Verac]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Saddlery]] level 13. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Ho there! Making tack for horses is very important work! I'm in need of some extra gear while I'm waiting on Tantai to replenish my leather stock. Can you bring me 10 [[Rough Saddle]]s? In exchange I'll share a couple trade secrets with you to help hone your craft.

===Requirements===
* Craft 10 Rough Saddles
* Posses 10 Rough Saddles
* Talk to Verac
