==Summary==
Crelpin in the Fae Realm said, "This land is just <i>infested</i> with spriggan weeds! I think the pixies plant them or something. They spend the days gathering sun, but at night they hunt for meat! There's a bunch growing south of town, and if you think about it, it'd be really irresponsible for you to NOT kill those pests."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Crelpin]]''' in '''[[Fae Realm]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
This land is just <i>infested</i> with spriggan weeds! I think the pixies plant them or something. They spend the days gathering sun, but at night they hunt for meat! There's a bunch growing south of town, and if you think about it, it'd be really irresponsible for you to NOT kill those pests.

* Kill Spriggan Weeds x7
* Talk to Crelpin
