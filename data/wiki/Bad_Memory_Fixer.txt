==Summary==
Midge in the Fae Realm said, "Look, I don't need random help from random mortals! Somebody brought me this potion of memory inhibition, and I've been studying it. I think I can invert the effect. Well, I can invert <i>something</i>: memories, emotions, internal organs, something. And something is better than nothing.

I wrote down the things that I need, but hmm, they'll have to come from a mortal world. Wait, you're a mortal! Here. Get this stuff! Hurry! Go!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Midge the Apothecary]]''' in '''[[Fae Realm]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
Look, I don't need random help from random mortals! Somebody brought me this potion of memory inhibition, and I've been studying it. I think I can invert the effect. Well, I can invert <i>something</i>: memories, emotions, internal organs, something. And something is better than nothing.

* Obtain Blue Cheese
* Obtain Cinnabar x3
* Obtain Hope Oil
* Talk to Midge
