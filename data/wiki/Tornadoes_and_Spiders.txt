==Summary==
Lumpfuzz in the Ilmari Desert said, "So yeah, thanks for coming. I heard about this from that elf seer across town: part of the desert is coated in giant spiders that are, like, mated with tornados? I don't understand it, but wherever the spiders walk, the tornados go too. And the elf says that they've come topside to lay their eggs in the wind to scatter far and wide. That's not good.\n\nI've been out killin' em all morning, but there's plenty left for you! Claws and fangs don't work too well, but they go down with some lightning bolts. Head southeast, you'll hear 'em before you see 'em."

===Prerequisites===
Only [[Warden]]s may obtain this quest. To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lumpfuzz]]''' in '''[[Ilmari]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.
