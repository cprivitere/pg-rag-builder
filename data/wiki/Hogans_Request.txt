==Summary==
Hogan the staff fighter has requested some items as payment for teaching you staff combat.

==Prerequisites==
To start this quest, talk to [[Hogan]] in [[Hogan's Keep]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
*Hogan will not offer the dialogue under the Talk tab to begin this quest to players in an animal form.

==Preface==
{{Quote|Make yourself comfortable in my courtyard.

'''Can you teach me Staff fighting?'''

Yes. But I only want serious students. If you're willing to show your interest by performing a few small tasks for me, I'd be delighted to teach you.}}

==Requirements==

*Obtain {{Item|Ancient Bronze Coin}} x2
*Obtain {{Item|Giant Snail Shell}} x2 {{Hint|Perfect Giant Snail Shells work also}}
*Obtain [[:Category:Items/Poetry|Poetry]] {{Hint| Gruzark's Chapbook, Unidentified Poetry Book, Elven Poetry, Goblin Poetry, and possibly other items work too.}}
*Talk to [[Hogan]]
