==Summary==
Selaxi in Amulna asked for some carrots. She mentioned that there's a small garden lot in Amulna where you could grow them.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Selaxi]]''' in '''[[Ilmari Desert]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Can you run over to Kelim's little garden -- it's over in the corner of town -- and see if he's going to have more carrots any time in the next year? He just keeps growing peppers!

You know what, don't bother talking to the lout. Just get me carrots!}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Carrot}}s x6
* Talk to Selaxi
