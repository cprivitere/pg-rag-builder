==Summary==
A goblin named Jumjab in Eltibule asked for oversized mandibles, sometimes found on Tor-Uraks.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Jumjab]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I set up camp here so that I could kill the Tor-Uraks and take their mandibles. Sometimes you get a good, strong mandible from them, and those are precious to me! But it turns out Tor-Uraks are much, much too dangerous for me to kill. Do you have any mandibles, by chance?}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Oversized Mandible}}s x3
* Talk to Jumjab
