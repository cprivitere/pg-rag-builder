__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Lakrea told you to put 40 pieces of equipment in your inventory and then talk to her.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lakrea]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|You love equipment, right? All adventurers do! But you never want to carry around excess equipment: too heavy, not enough space, blah blah blah. You need to push through that perceived boundary. Go get... I don't know, say 40 pieces of equipment. Carry them all over here at once and let me see you.}}

===Requirements===
* Hold Equipment In Inventory
* Talk to Lakrea

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Holistic Wellness: Hold Gear|
{{Quote|That's... that's a lot of equipment. Okay, you look like you're about to herniate something. Go put that stuff down!}}

* 50 XP in [[Holistic Wellness]]
}}

===Bugs===
As of February 2020, there is a bug in this quest which prevents any item in a loadout from counting towards the required 40 pieces of equipment.
