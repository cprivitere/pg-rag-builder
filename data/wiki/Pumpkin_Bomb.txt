[[Pumpkin Bomb]] These are Special Abilities (side-bar skills). Only available during the Halloween event. 
* Complete the "Kill Skeletons" mini-quest.
* Complete the "Kill Skeleton Mages" mini-quest.
* You'll get a "Kill Pumpkin Mimics" mini-quest. After killing 7 evil pumpkins, you automatically learn Pumpkin Bomb (1). This quest is different from the stand-alone quest from Brianna to kill 15 pumpkin mimics. 

The remaining Pumpkin Bomb 2 (and Pumpkin Turrets 1/2) skills are available via books dropped during the Halloween event.
