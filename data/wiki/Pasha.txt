[[Pasha]] is a Rakshasa shopkeeper and saddler who trained in [[Statehelm]] before returning to her homeland.

== Location ==
; [[Ilmari]]
: [[Pasha]] can be found in the town of [[Amulna]]. When the weather is good, she can be seen outside around the town square.
== Shopkeeper ==
[[Pasha]] sells basic equipment for horses.
