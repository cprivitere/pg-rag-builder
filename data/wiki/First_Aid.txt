== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[First Aid]] provides healing abilities to any adventurer regardless of their primary or secondary skills, as abilities for [[First Aid]] are placed on the sidebar. First Aid Kits purchased from NPCs or crafted through [[Toolcrafting]] are required to use most abilities in this skill.

=== In-Game Description ===
The ability to use first aid kits to heal yourself or others.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Marna]] in [[Serbule]] will teach [[First Aid]] through conversation, and will bestow 200 XP in [[First Aid]] upon completion of the 2-hour Hang Out '''Fill out first aid kits.'''
* [[Norbert]] teaches First Aid abilities usable by [[Beast Forms]].
* [[Yagreet]] in [[Sun Vale]] teaches First Aid 6 and Resuscitate 1.
* [[Mox Warcut]] in [[Gazluk]]'s [[New Prestonbule Cave]] will unlock First Aid 51-60 at {{Favor|Friends}} and unlock First Aid 61-70 at {{Favor|Best Friends}}. He also teaches level-appropriate abilities.
* [[Backfat]] in the [[Fae Realm]] is able to unlock 71-80 and teach abilities of those levels.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Serendipity Cheddar]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Sir Hughes]] in [[Statehelm]] will unlock 81-90 and 91-100.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Equipment ===
Crafters producing equipment seeking [[First Aid]] improving Treasure Effects should obtain the [[Sigil Scripting]] recipe '''First Aid Inscription''', which allows for the creation of [[First Aid Garnet]]. 

This attuned gemstone can be used in any recipe which calls for the use of [[:Category:Items/Crystal|Crystals]] to create equipment with [[First Aid/Treasure Effects|First Aid Treasure Effects]].
