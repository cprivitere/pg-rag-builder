==Summary==
Ser Lorentz in Vidaria said, "Thanks for getting those brains. They apparently confirmed Mr. Blueheart's theory. Have you met him? He's in camp here. Squirrely man, but his heart's in the right place. Anyway, I have another request from him. You know those skulls with wings near the walls? Yeah, kill those and get a sample of their blood. I know, skulls don't have blood! But he says there's blood in the wings."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ser Lorentz]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. The quest [[Swarmer Brains]] must be completed first.
