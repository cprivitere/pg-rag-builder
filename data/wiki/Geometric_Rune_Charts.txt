==Summary==
The Sand Seer in Amulna asked for some Geometric Rune Scrolls. She said that many Rakshasa in the nearby city of Rahu are gifted in making them.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[The Sand Seer | Sand Seer]]''' in '''[[Ilmari Desert]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I found a most interesting item yesterday, and entirely by chance. The tempestuous young Rakshasa that sells dyes -- I forget her name -- had a scroll for sale. This scroll is covered in geometric lines that closely resemble the area's ley lines. It was quite warm to the touch, too. It's quite fascinating. I assumed it was the work of some sage, but she said they're very common in her home city and that many people there can make them. If you go to Rahu, see if you can find some for me.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Geometric Rune Chart}}s x3
* Talk to Sand Seer
