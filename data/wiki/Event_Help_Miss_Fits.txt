==Summary==
You've found Miss Fits! But she doesn't seem interested in going home until she wins another game. Help by bringing her some red wing tokens so she can buy a game ticket!

This Quest can be repeated after 15 days.

You must have previously completed [[Event: Lost Scapegoats]] in order to undertake this quest. This quest is only available during a Lost Scapegoats event. 

===Requirements===
* Obtain 5 Red Wing Tokens
* Speak with Miss Fits
