{{Quote|I get to make the laws because I'm the mayor. Daisy gets to be my evil vizier. She said all mayors have one.}}
[[Spot]] is an unfortunate dog-turned-Human. Her former master, a wizard, transformed her into a [[Human]] to save her life. Although she's smarter than your average dog, one could safely say she's dumber than your average talking animal. Spot serves as the mayor of Animal Town solely because of her looks. The nearby [[Ranalon]] are frightened of Spot, and avoid the village.

As a resident of [[Animal Town]], [[Spot]] is only willing to hold conversations with characters that have transformed into a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]] over 180 real-world minutes prior to the conversation attempt. If you lose track of the time you last transformed, [[Norbert]] is willing to tell you how long you have until the other residents will tolerate talking with you.
== Location ==
; [[Sun Vale]]
: [[Animal Town]] (located in the southeastern part of [[Sun Vale]], at the mountainous area.)<br/>She constantly walks around, but is easy to find because she is the only "human" in town.

[[Spot]] sells First Aid kits and Armor Patching kits, but also a variety of crafting supplies.

==== Interpretive Dance Variant ====
[[Spot]] is the teacher for [[Interpretive Dance|Dance 5 (variant B)]]. At least three musicians must be playing instruments near [[Spot]] to convince them to demonstrate their dance. {{Hint|Beware the scorpions if you're neither Druid nor Animal!}}
