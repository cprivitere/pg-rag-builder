==Summary==
Glortok in the Rahu Sewers said, "The skeletal ratkin mages with electricity powers... those are very annoying! Kill them!"

This Quest can be repeated after 66 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chief Glortok]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Goblin Avenger: Treants]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
