==Summary==
Rick Snapley in New Prestonbule said, "A Ri-Shin tree needs a topper, something that glows. But I don't wanna use one of those glowing yellow crystals... I know Sona says she can make 'em safe, but it's too dangerous.

Let's just... I dunno, let's just stick some candles on top, like my family used to do. But we'll need to light 'em every night... got any spare candles?"

This Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Rick Snapley]]''' in '''[[New Prestonbule Cave]]'''.
