== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
{{Quote|
The Art of Appreciating Nature.  
}}
'''Nature Appreciation''' is a  skill that allows you to use flowers to grant various 1-hour buffs.  This is not considered a crafting skill, and does not benefit from crafting experience bonuses.

=== In-Game Description ===
The ability to study nature and find inner peace and contentment. Even the most hardened warriors need the ability to appreciate nature.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Smelling a flower 
* Unlock levels 51-60, 61-70, and 71-80 from [[Crelpin]]
* Unlock levels 81-90 from [[The Floriculturist]]
* Buy [[Traditional Birthing Arrangement]] from [[Zhao]] at 562 councils each that provides you with 672 xp per item.  Nature Appreciation 25 needed to use.

== Connected Skills ==
===== Related Skills: =====
Primary:
* [[Foraging]]
* [[Gardening]]<br>Flower seeds are obtained via foraging and as drops on certain mobs.  The seeds must be grown into flowers using the Gardening skill.
* [[Flower Arrangement]]
