[[Ailred]] is an impatient resident of the city of Povus. Each in-game day, [[Ailred]] will make a request for an item in the form of a quest. The quests do not have time limits, however a new quest can not be obtained until a previously assigned one is completed or abandoned.

== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Ailred]] can be found in Old Town, inside the remains of the Inn. He only appears when the Povus Town Level is at least 5.

== Favor ==
[[Ailred]] does not use the favor system.
