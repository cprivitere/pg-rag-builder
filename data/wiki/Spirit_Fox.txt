== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Spirit Fox]] is a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]].

=== In-Game Description ===
Mastery of the techniques of the spirit fox: an awakened fox with supernatural powers.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Completely Normal Fox]]  in the [[Wolf Cave]] turns the player into a [[Spirit Fox]] and teaches [[Spirit Fox]] combat skill after gifting them a [[Spirit Stone]].
* Another way to become a [[Spirit Fox]] and learn the ability is to trade a [[Spirit Stone]] to another player who is currently a [[Spirit Fox]]. Once you do that, they revert to their previous form and you become a [[Spirit Fox]].
* [[Completely Normal Fox]] in the [[Wolf Cave]] will teach the Trick Fox ability line after reaching [[Spirit Fox]] Level 12. (You have to have been a [[Spirit Fox]] for at least 24 hours.)
* [[Mittens]] in [[Kur Mountains]] unlocks the Level 51 - 60 and Level 61-70 skill caps.
* [[Noita the Green]] in the [[Fae Realm]] unlocks the Level 71-80 skill cap.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Big Footed Jim]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Smoke Tail]] in [[Statehelm Sewers]] unlocks the 91-100 skill cap and [[Prodigy Potential]].

=== To Stop Being a {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===

There are only two ways to cease being a [[Spirit Fox]]. One is to have another player trade you a [[Spirit Stone]], transforming them into a [[Spirit Fox]] and reverting you to your previous form. The other way is by drinking a [[Minor Panacea]].

===== Related Skills: =====
* [[Unarmed]] - Spirit Fox and Unarmed work best as a melee-based skill set.
* [[Warden]] - Spirit Fox and Warden work very well together as combined skills, with a significant amount of possible electricity and/or fire synergy.
* [[Necromancy]] - Necromancy is possible if a Necromancy symbol is equipped (armor, clothing or jewelry only, Necro-enabled weapons do not work for this).
* [[Priest]] - Priest is possible if an Arisetsu symbol is equipped (armor, clothing, or jewelry only, Priest-enabled weapons do not work for this).
* [[Animal Handling]] - Spirit Fox and Animal Handling work well together.
* [[Psychology]] - Spirit Fox and Psychology work well together also, and is commonly used for Fox-based speed-builds.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==

=== Spirit Items ===
See Lorebook [[So You're a Spirit Fox]].
; {{Item|Anchor Rune}}
: If a [[Spirit Fox]] is gifted an Anchor Rune through a trade, they become [[Anchored]], preventing the use of any [[Spirit Fox]] abilities for 15 minutes.
: If a [[Spirit Fox]] is mailed an Anchor Rune through [[Pigeon|Pigeon Mail]], they will not suffer the debuff.
; {{Item|Spirit Stone}}
: If a [[Spirit Fox]] is gifted a Spirit Stone through a trade, the gifter transforms into a Fox, and the receiver reverts to their previous form. The receiver must be in fox form while trading.
: If a [[Spirit Fox]] purchases a Spirt Stone from a vendor, no transformation occurs.
: If a [[Spirit Fox]] is mailed a Spirit Stone through [[Pigeon|Pigeon Mail]], no transformation occurs.

=== Riding ===
As a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]], [[Spirit Fox]] are not able to use the traditional Horse mounts offered through [[Riding]]. Instead, [[Mittens]] in [[Kur Mountains]] teaches mount-form to Spirit Fox players who are at least level 25 and have '''{{Favor|Friends}}''' favor.
===Equipment===
Crafters producing equipment for use by [[Spirit Fox]] should obtain the [[Sigil Scripting]] recipe '''Spirit Fox Inscription,''' which allows for the creation of {{Item|Spirit Fox Lapis}}. 

This attuned gemstone can be used in any recipe which calls for the use of [[:Category:Items/Crystal|Crystals]] to create equipment with [[Spirit Fox]] Treasure Effects.
