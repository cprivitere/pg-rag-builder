==Summary==
Thedar said, "So, you want to be an Armorsmith do you? Let's get to the good stuff now! Forge and bring me one full Amazing Orcish Plate suit and I will see what I can do to help you advance your craft!

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Thedar]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Armorsmithing]] level 70. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
So, you want to be an Armorsmith do you? Let's get to the good stuff now! Forge and bring me one full Amazing Orcish Plate suit and I will see what I can do to help you advance your craft!

* Possess a Amazing Orcish Plate Coat
* Craft some Amazing Orcish Plate Greaves (Enchanted)
* Possess some Amazing Orcish Plate Greaves
* Craft a Amazing Orcish Plate Helm (Enchanted)
* Possess a Amazing Orcish Plate Helm
* Craft some Amazing Orcish Plate Gauntlets (Enchanted)
* Possess some Amazing Orcish Plate Gauntlets
* Craft some Amazing Orcish Plate Boots (Enchanted)
* Possess some Amazing Orcish Plate Boots
* Talk to Thedar
