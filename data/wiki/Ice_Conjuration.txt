== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Ice Conjuration]] is a skill learned by expert [[Ice Magic|Ice Mages]] that allows for the creation of frozen treats and weapons.

=== In-Game Description ===
A sub-skill of Ice Magic dedicated to the creation of delicate ice items. These items tend to melt in hot environments.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Ichin the Ice Master]] will teach this skill to Adventurers with 50 Levels in [[Ice Magic]].
* [[Bendith the Banished]] teaches recipes for Pear and Peach Shaved Ice.

== Connected Skills ==
