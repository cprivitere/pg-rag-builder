==Activity Quest==
[[Crazed Animals Subdued]] is a group of [[Activity Quests]] which do not appear in the quest log. These four almost identical quests share the name of '''Crazed Animals Subdued''' with the only noticeable difference being a change in their InternalName (Vidaria_CrazedAnimals1, Vidaria_CrazedAnimals2, Vidaria_CrazedAnimals3, Vidaria_CrazedAnimals4). They have identical rewards.

===Prerequisites===
This [[Activity Quests|Activity Quest]] is automatically completed when an infected-beast-related Ecological Disaster is prevented.
