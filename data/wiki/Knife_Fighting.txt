== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Knife Fighting]] is a combat skill focused on heavy Damage Over Time(DoT) attacks. Combining its attacks with its evasion and light self-healing abilities make it a strong main skill. 

There are also throwing knives that can be used as a ranged attack. These work much like arrows in that they sometimes (85%) are recoverable when looting enemies, otherwise they are lost on use. 
=== In-Game Description ===
Using daggers and thrown knives in combat. Knife combat is extremely damaging, relying on traumatic injuries, poison, and subterfuge.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Bahdba]] teaches this skill in the countryside to the southeast of Serbule Keep. It is a starting skill for the Fairy race. Some Blacksmithing Throwing Knife recipes are taught by [[Bahdba]].
* [[Lisi]] teaches many special [[Knife Fighting]] abilities.
* [[Rick Snapley]], a former bandit fence living in the remote [[New Prestonbule Cave]], can unlock Levels 51-70 [[Knife Fighting]] and teaches the associated abilities.
* [[Pegast]] unlocks 71-80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Emersil the Ever-Gloating]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Tessa Starlight]] in [[Statehelm]] unlocks 81-90, 91-100, and [[Prodigy Potential]].
