==Summary==
Norbert in Sun Vale has tasked you with a list of deeds to prove your worthiness to join the Wardens.

You must have previously completed [[The External Resource Coordinator]] in order to undertake this quest. 

===Requirements===
* Obtain Bananas x10
* Touch Meditation Spots in Sun Vale x5
* Kill Ranalon in Sun Vale x10
* Speak with Norbert
