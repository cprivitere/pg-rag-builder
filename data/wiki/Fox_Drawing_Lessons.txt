==Summary==
Barnaby in the Sacred Warden Grotto said, "I've been teaching the foxes how to read by sounding out the Human language symbols. I don't think they're ready for writing yet -- truth be told I have a hard time with writing myself! But it's time they became familiar with paper and ink. We'll need a LOT of both."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Barnaby]]''' in the '''[[Sacred Grotto]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
I've been teaching the foxes how to read by sounding out the Human language symbols. I don't think they're ready for writing yet -- truth be told I have a hard time with writing myself! But it's time they became familiar with paper and ink. We'll need a LOT of both.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Cheap Paper x99 (simple parchments)
* Obtain Cheap Ink x99 (basic inks)
* Talk to Barnaby
