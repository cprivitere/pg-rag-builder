[[Aktaari Cave]] is part of a vast cave system underneath the [[Povus]] region. This cave system is often considered split into four major sections. The [[Errruka's Cave]], [[Forthragarian Caves]], [[Nightmare Caves]], and [[Aktaari Cave]]. Trainers for the majority of the level 90 unlocks can be found in these caves.
==Points of Interest==
====Egg Run====
See the dedicated page on [[Egg Run]] for full details.
====Queen's Door====
The [[Aktaari Queen]] guards a door which only opens at the time of her death.

== Tips ==
The Aktaari Cave is the location of the regularly attended "Egg Run." All players can attend, however lower level players will receive no benefit from crushing the eggs. It is a good source of experience, [[Xogrite Chunk]], and level 90-95 gear.
