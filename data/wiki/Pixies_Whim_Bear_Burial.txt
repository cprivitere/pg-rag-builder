==Summary==
Uriana in the Fae Realm said, "It is difficult to remember what beast is a true immortal and what is not. But I have invented something in the human tongue to help! Bury bears. Because they are mortal, you see? "Bury bears"? Go and practice until this trick is burned into your mind."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Uriana]] in the [[Fae Realm]]. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

{{Quote|
It is difficult to remember what beast is a true immortal and what is not. But I have invented something in the human tongue to help! Bury bears. Because they are mortal, you see? "Bury bears"? Go and practice until this trick is burned into your mind.
}}

===Requirements===
* Bury [[Fae Bear]]s x5
* Talk to Uriana.
