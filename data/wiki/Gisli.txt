[[Gisli]] is a Dwarf acting as [[Serbule Keep]]'s stablemaster. He offers access to stables to leave [[Animal Handling]] pets you may have trained up. Gisli likes to guess peoples' backstories. Before the destruction of [[Dwyndarre]], Gisli ran a luxury pet care center in the city, taking care of designer pets.
