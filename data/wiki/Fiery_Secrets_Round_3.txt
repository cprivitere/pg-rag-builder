==Summary==
Marna wants me to burn myself on a total of 25 different candles inside the nearby crypt! That's going to take a lot of delving.
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Marna]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor. The quest [[Fiery Secrets, Round 2]] must be completed first.
===Preface===
Okay, last time. Just go touch candles until your hand burns off. Hyperbole! Relax! How about 25, just to be damned sure we've got it done?
===Requirements===
* Touch 25 of the 35 candles within the [[Serbule Crypt]].
* Talk to [[Marna]].
