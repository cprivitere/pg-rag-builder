==Summary==
A beehive in the Fae Realm said, "Bzz. BzzZZzzz ...

We cannot pollinate the red flowers nearby. This endangers future honey potential!

Bzz. ZzzZzzZzzBz ... zzzBzt!

Please pollinate them for us!"

This Quest can be repeated after 5 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Beehive]] D''' in '''[[Fae Realm]],''' completing the quest [[Meet the Hive]]. This quest is only available to Fae.

===Preface===
Bzz. BzzZZzzz ...

We cannot pollinate the red flowers nearby. This endangers future honey potential!

Bzz. ZzzZzzZzzBz ... zzzBzt!

Please pollinate them for us!

===Requirements===
* Pollinate Flower to the North
* Pollinate Flower to the Northeast
* Pollinate Flower to the Southeast
* Pollinate Flower to the Southwest
* Pollinate Flower to the Northwest
* Pollinate Flower to the West
* Talk to the Bee Hive
