==Summary==
Mittens in Kur Mountains asked for some lac to help make paint. She says that it can be found on giant insects, including Tor-Uraks.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mittens]]''' in '''[[Kur Mountains]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Disguising Fox Outpost]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Okay, these agates are great. But to make a useful paint I also need some lac. It comes from insects... but harvesting little insects would take too long. I bet the tor-urak that live around here would have it in larger quantities.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Lac}} x6
* Talk to Mittens
