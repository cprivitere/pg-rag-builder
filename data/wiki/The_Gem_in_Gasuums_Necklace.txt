==Summary==
Gasu'um in New Prestonbule said, "The central decoration of the necklace you broke is a rare and beautiful crystal known as vervadium. It will be difficult to find. But should you find a piece, bring it to me. I will pay you ten times its value!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Gasu'um]]''' in '''[[New Prestonbule Cave]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Sentimental Jewelry]] in order to undertake this quest.
