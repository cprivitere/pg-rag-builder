==September 23, 2014==

This is a small bug-fix update. The client did not change and no new download should be required.

===Build Notes===

* Fixed bug that caused monsters to become immobilized forever and/or delete themselves if they saw a player flying overhead
* Sped up some monster respawns in sun vale. Slowed down some resource respawns

* The Elite monsters in Sun Vale can no longer be buried or eaten. (So that everybody has a chance to get their loot)

* Joeh now allows weapon and armor consignments (instead of just calligraphy—reIated ones)

* Items that were supposed to boost Druid damage % were boosting Hammer damage instead. (Will be corrected when items are reequipped)

* Added new belts associated with the Hammer and Druid skills. These are available for purchase from Agrashab (troll vendor)
* Fixed issues with monsters moving through water in Sun Vale area

* Removed Hardcore death penalty requirement from Summer Court armor

* Fixed minor fairy scripting issues

* Added more debugging to track down the cause of monsters becoming immobile
