==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "What took you so long?! I've been waiting for more than an hour! Nevermind. I don't care about your excuses. Please prepare a stuffed bell pepper for me."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
What took you so long?! I've been waiting for more than an hour! Nevermind. I don't care about your excuses. Please prepare a stuffed bell pepper for me.

===Midway===
Yes, remember my order? The stuffed bell pepper? I'm still waiting...

===Requirements===
* Craft Stuffed Bell Pepper
* (Keep Stuffed Bell Pepper)
* Talk to Ailred
