[[Lincha]] is a resident of the city of Povus. Each in-game day, [[Lincha]] will make a request for an item in the form of a quest. The quests do not have time limits, however a new quest can not be obtained until a previously assigned one is completed or abandoned.

== Location ==
; [[Povus]]
: [[Lincha]] can be found in Old Town in a building near the northeast entrance to the Sprawl district. She only appears when the Povus Town Level is at least ???.

== Favor ==
