==Summary==
Mandibles in [[Red Wing Casino]] said, "I miss my time in [[Sedgewick Forest]], when I worked with my brethren to keep goblin populations in check. Would you kill goblins in my name? As they perish, make sure to state, 'This is for Mandibles.'"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mandibles]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
