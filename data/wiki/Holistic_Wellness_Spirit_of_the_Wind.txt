==Summary==
Lakrea directed you to stand in a sandstorm. There are sandstorms in the Ilmari Desert. (You must be within 65 meters of the center of the storm to advance the quest.)

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lakrea]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Your mind will never fly free while your thoughts stay so firmly planted on the ground. You need to start thinking like the wind! Hmm, I know -- why don't you go think <i>in</i> the wind? In a sandstorm! Now don't get crazy -- just stay in the sand storm for a few seconds at a time, or you might get hurt!}}

===Requirements===
* Stay in a Sandstorm
* Talk to Lakrea
