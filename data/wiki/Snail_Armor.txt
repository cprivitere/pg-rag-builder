==Summary==
Yogzi in Sun Vale wants evidence of snail armor. He believes an innkeeper in Eltibule can teach the technique. A simple pair of snail-shell boots should suffice.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Yogzi]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

To begin this quest you must have level 1 [[Toolcrafting]].

You must have previously completed [[Fancier Knives]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Some random wanderer told me that in a far off land, they make armor out of snail shells using the same techniques I use to make tools. Ridiculous, isn't it?

Go find out if it's true. Search far and wide! Leave no stone unturned!

Well, if you want a hint, sure... I heard that some inn keeper in Eltibule is an expert at using snail bits.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Decent Snail Boots}}
* Talk to Yogzi
