==Summary==
On Sun Vale's main island, on the northeast beach, some sort of entity asked you to remove a huge clownfish. It can only be caught at dusk or dawn (5am or 7pm).
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, angle by the northeast side of the main island of '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. {{Hint|If can angling Sharks then it's place, when angling Eel move east.}}

{{Quote|
