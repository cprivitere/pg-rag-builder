== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Blacksmithing''' is a crafting skill often confused with [[Armorsmithing]]. Blacksmiths do not create armor, but can improve the protective capabilities of armor. Many Blacksmiths create items that can be used in [[Carpentry]], [[Toolcrafting]], [[Cheesemaking]], and other skills.
=== In-Game Description ===
Making and enhancing metal equipment. Blacksmiths don't create armor directly, but they can buttress metal armor's effectiveness. They also create metal parts for many other skills.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Tadion]], an Elf living in [[Serbule Keep|Serbule]], will teach Blacksmithing to anyone who has reached Level 25 [[Armor Patching]].
* [[Kalaba]], a Rakshasa living at the [[Eltibule Keep]], is knowledgeable in crafting Deer Shoes using Blacksmithing.
* [[Bahdba]], a Fae residing near the stone statue in the southeast of Serbule, is knowledgeable in crafting throwing knives.
* [[Borghild]] is rumored to contain many secrets. Blacksmithing recipes for metal-based statues may be discovered in this dangerous dungeon.
* [[Falkrin Overstrike]], a resident of [[Amulna]], the last remaining town in the [[Ilmari]] Desert, can raise the Skill Cap of '''Blacksmithing''' from level 50 to 60, and level 60 to 70.
* [[Kodan]], a Lycanthrope living in [[Sun Vale]]'s [[Animal Town]], can also raise the Skill Cap to 60 and 70.
* [[Solgribue]] will aid developing blacksmith in reaching levels 71 to 80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Winston Veilmoor]], a Human who works in [[Statehelm]]'s market district can raise the Skill Cap from levels 80 to 90, and 90 to 100.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Forge Locations===
; [[Serbule]]
: A forge can be found in the enclosed section inside [[Serbule Keep]]. It is near the A-aisle player merchant stalls, next to a tanning rack, and belongs to [[Tadion]].
; [[Eltibule]]
: There is a forge in [[Eltibule Keep]], to the right as you come through the front gates. It is often used by [[Kalaba]].
; [[Red Wing Casino]]
: The boiler near [[Dorimir Fangblade]] (near the Employee Entrance) can be used as a forge.
; [[Rahu]]
: A forge can be found in the center of the friendly section of the [[Rahu|City of Rahu]], up the stairs, next to the garden, tanning rack, and cotton gin.
; [[Kur Mountains]]
: A forge can be found at [[Ukorga]]'s Northwestern Outpost. It is in the building with the [[Council Storage]] machine, right next to [[Janet Lews]].
; [[Ilmari]]
: A forge can be found inside the northern gate of [[Amulna]], next to the Work Order board.
; [[New Prestonbule Cave]]
: Toward the back of the cave, past the second portcullis, the orc [[Torgan]] works next to a forge. You can use the forge even if you don't know enough [[Orcish]] (Level 25 or higher) to communicate with him.
; [[Fae Realm]]
: There is a forge near [[Solgribue]], the friendly troll in the main Fae settlement.
; [[Povus]]
: There are two forges in Povus. One is behind the building with [[Tremmond Blackhammer]] and is consecrated to Umrad, meaning besides ordinary [[Blacksmithing]], [[Toolcrafting]], [[Jewelry Crafting]], and [[Armorsmithing]], it can also be used to make Dwarven metal armor, a specialized type of metal armor. Another special forge is consecrated to Tast in the Orc encampment and is needed when crafting Orcish metal armor.
; [[Wolf Cave]]
: There is a forge consecrated to [[Norala the Huntress]] down one of the early side tunnels not far from the entrance. It is needed to forge metal armor for lycans. Like the other consecrated forges, it can also be used to make other items that require a regular forge.

Notable zones without forges: [[Anagoge Island]], [[Serbule Hills]], [[Sun Vale]], and [[Gazluk]], as well as the dungeons and caves not named here.

== Trivia ==
The forge in [[Eltibule]] town is described as a ''Forge-Like Fire Pit''. The forge in the [[Red Wing Casino]] is described as an ''Overheating Boiler,'' but don't worry, it's in no danger of exploding, for now.

[[Helena Veilmoor]]'s family is famous for its Blacksmithing. Their shop is in [[Statehelm]].
