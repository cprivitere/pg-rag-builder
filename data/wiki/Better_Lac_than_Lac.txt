==Summary==
Kib the Unkillable in Red Wing Casino said, "My friend Irkima has told me a fanciful tale! There are insects on this continent that secrete lac that is so strong that it has different properties. Irkima called it 'ultra-lac', but of course I'm certain that sages in this land have a more appropriate name for it. I must see this substance! Can you procure some?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Kib the Unkillable]]''' in '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.
