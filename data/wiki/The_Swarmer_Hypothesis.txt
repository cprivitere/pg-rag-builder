==Summary==
Ferris Blueheart in Vidaria said, "Ah, you were the one that got me the swarmer brains and the flapskull blood? Very good. I have need of you again: you must help me prove my hypothesis! I just need brains from demon soldiers so I can compare them with the swarmer brains.""

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ferris Blueheart]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. The quest [[Flapskull Blood]] from [[Ser Lorentz]] must be completed first
