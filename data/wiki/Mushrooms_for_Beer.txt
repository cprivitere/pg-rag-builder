==Summary==
Brianna Willer in Serbule Hills said, "Sometimes delicious beer uses gross ingredients. Mushrooms, for instance! But I've found that my boletus-and-apple pale ale is incredibly delicious! I've just run out of boletus mushrooms. Can you gather some for me?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Brianna Willer]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
