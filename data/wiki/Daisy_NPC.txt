{{Quote|Hi. I-it-it's hard t-t-to talk t-t-to strangers. B-but welcome!}}
[[Daisy (NPC)|Daisy]] is a criminally shy doe that plods around..shyly. She has a knack for making <strike>drugs</strike> Bounceweed, which her husband [[Jumper]] enjoys a bit too much. She has been working to reduce the addictiveness of her concoctions. Before reaching [[Animal Town]], [[Daisy (NPC)|Daisy]] used the [[Awakening]] on [[Jumper]] with the help of her former master.

As a resident of [[Animal Town]], [[Daisy (NPC)|Daisy]] is only willing to hold conversations with characters that have transformed into a [[Beast Forms|Beast Form]] over 180 real-world minutes prior to the conversation attempt. If you lose track of the time you last transformed, [[Norbert]] is willing to tell you how long you have until the other residents will tolerate talking with you.
