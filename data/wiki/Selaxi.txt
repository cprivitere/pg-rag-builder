[[Selaxi]] is a female [[Rakshasa]] living in the walled town of [[Amulna]] in the [[Ilmari]] Desert. She has a small food shop just off the center of the town and sells cooking supplies, as well as providing training for cooking 51-60 and high level cooking recipes at friends.  She is convinced that her signature dish, the Scorpion Stinger will make her famous once the town increases in population.
