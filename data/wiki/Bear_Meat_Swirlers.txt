==Summary==
Mandibles in [[Red Wing Casino]] said, "My Sausage Swirlers are a hit with many varieties of mammalian guests. However, I believe I could improve on their flavor if they were made of bear meat -- or a similar beast -- instead of goblin meat. Would you procure some for me?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mandibles]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
