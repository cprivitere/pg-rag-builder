==Summary==
Mushroom Jack in Serbule asked you to deliver a very personal message to Therese down at the windmill.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mushroom Jack]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Take a message to Therese down by the windmill, will you? Ask her to come up here tonight for a good ploughing. And have her bring some root vegetables. I know how she loves those.}}

===Requirements===
* Deliver a message to Therese
* Talk to Mushroom Jack
