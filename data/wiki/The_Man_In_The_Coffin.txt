__NOTOC__
==Summary==
Truxartis asked me to bring him two jars of Acur Blood.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, find and interact with the coffin in the [[Vidaria]] trapdoor tower in the northeast corner of the zone. No prior quests are required.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I have a... magical illness.

I need a rare substance known as Acur Blood. I know ...not... where one such as... yourself... would find this thing. Perhaps seek out sages or ...mystics. In any case, I grant you no hope. But if you succeed, I shall reward you handsomely.
Wait! Actually, make it two jars of Acur Blood.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Acur Blood}} x2
* Return to the Coffin

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for The Man In The Coffin|
{{Quote|A hand came out of the coffin and snatched the Acur Blood. A moment later, a pale human stepped out.}}

* 50 {{Favor|favor}}
* 10,000 Councils
}}

===Notes===
{{Spoiler|Notes|
Acur Blood drops from completing world events: the ''[[Close The Portal]]'' event in [[Eltibule]] (1 AM in-game) and [[Sun Vale]] (3 AM in-game), and the ''[[Cultist Uprising]]'' event in [[Statehelm]] (5 AM in-game).

If Truxartis is not present in the coffin, try logging out and back in to the zone.
}}
