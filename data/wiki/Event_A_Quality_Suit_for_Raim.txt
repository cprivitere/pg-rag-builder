==Summary==
Raim said, "Want to swap some knowledge to progress your skills? Bring me one full crafted Quality Cloth suit and I will see what I can do to help you flourish at your craft!"

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Raim]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Tailoring]] level 54. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Want to swap some knowledge to progress your skills? Bring me one full crafted Quality Cloth suit and I will see what I can do to help you flourish at your craft!

===Requirements===
* Craft a Quality Cloth Shirt
* Obtain a Quality Cloth Shirt
* Craft some Quality Cloth Pants
* Obtain some Quality Cloth Pants
* Craft a Quality Cloth Cowl
* Obtain a Quality Cloth Cowl
* Craft some Quality Cloth Gloves
* Obtain some Quality Cloth Gloves
* Craft some Quality Cloth Slippers
* Obtain some Quality Cloth Slippers
* Talk to Raim
