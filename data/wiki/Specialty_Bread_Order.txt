==Summary==
Mandibles in [[Red Wing Casino]] said, "One of our VIP guests has requested a special variety of bread, and I do not know how to make it! Another guest told me that a human somewhere in Kur can teach the recipe, but of course that helps me very little. I am in dire need of this 'vegan nut barley bread', so if you manage to bake some, please bring it to me!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Mandibles]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
