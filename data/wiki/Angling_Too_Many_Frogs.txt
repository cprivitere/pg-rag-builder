==Summary==
While angling in a lake in northeast Povus, you encountered a voice that asked you to remove some of the aggressive frogs from the lake. These frogs can be caught with angling at dusk, and a little while after. (7pm-8pm)

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, angle by the lake in northeast '''[[Povus]]'''. Angling level 60 is required; the lake contains [[Rock Carp]], [[Yellow Perch]] and [[Mukklo]].
