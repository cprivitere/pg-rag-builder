Furlak in Rahu asked you to find a perfume called Telka's Tail. He suggested rakshasa bandits might have it.

To start this quest, talk to '''[[Furlak]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.
