== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Bladesmithing''' is used to craft bladed weapons like swords, knives, dirks, claws, and tuneup kits.

=== In-Game Description ===
A specialization of Blacksmithing. Although bladesmiths can craft excellent weapons, they are often more preoccupied with maintaining blades at peak performance.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Inella]] in [[Elven Judgement]] will teach Bladesmithing after you defeat [[Kunax the Dread]].
* Levels 71-80 and 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Winston Veilmoor]] in [[Statehelm]]'s market district unlocks Levels 81-90 and 91-100.
