==Summary==
Agrashab in the Fae Realm said, "I've tried indirect methods of dealing with the phoenix issue, but the threat grows ever hotter. My subjects have even given a name to the birds' stolen home: 'firebird mountain.' It is not a comforting name to fire-averse trolls. Please resolve this issue by removing the phoenices."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Agrashab/Fae Realm|Agrashab]] in the [[Fae Realm]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.
