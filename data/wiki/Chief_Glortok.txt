[[Chief Glortok]] is the chief of the goblins in Rahu Sewer and keeper of [[Xedrite]]. The only thing he hates more than non-goblins is the dangers in the sewers and is willing to hire some help.

== Location ==
; [[Rahu Sewer]]
: [[Chief Glortok]] is always in the goblin hideout in the [[Rahu Sewer]].
