==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] sent me to [[Lomas' Tower]] in the [[Kur Mountains]] to recover the body parts of the First Thrall hidden in three separate locations.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[Accessing the Safehouse]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|I need to reawaken my blood powers. If only I hadn't killed the First Thrall years ago and stashed his body parts beneath a ruined tower in the Kur Mountains! But alas, I did.

Go and find them for me. Try not to alert Lomas to your presence -- you shouldn't need to see him at all. If you do encounter Lomas, tell him that I'll get him the money soon.}}

===Requirements===
* Touch Tuvillic Rune 1 in [[Lomas' Tower]] · Obtain {{Item|First Thrall's Skull}}
* Touch Tuvillic Rune 2 in [[Lomas' Tower]] · Obtain {{Item|First Thrall's Lucky Femur}}
* Touch Tuvillic Rune 3 in [[Lomas' Tower]] · Obtain {{Item|First Thrall's Arm}}
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]
