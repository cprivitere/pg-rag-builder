[[Sanja]] is a werewolf that teaches advanced abilities for [[Lycanthropy]].  As such, she's a vital necessity for players that want to play in werewolf form consistently.
== Location ==
; [[Kur Mountains]]
: Near the North-Westernmost point on the map that isn't in the ocean, next to a campfire and [[Jace Soral]].
