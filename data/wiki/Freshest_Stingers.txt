==Summary==
Raina in the Fae Realm said, "Don't tell Crelpin, but I've got a special order for some bee stingers. The only catch is that they have to be fresh! A certain troll will pay well for them, and I'll give you half. Deal?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Raina]]''' in '''[[Fae Realm]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. You must have previously completed [[Freshest Pears]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
Don't tell Crelpin, but I've got a special order for some bee stingers. The only catch is that they have to be fresh! A certain troll will pay well for them, and I'll give you half. Deal?

===Requirements===
* Loot Fae Bee Stingers x3
* (Keep the Stingers) x3
* Talk to Raina
