==Summary==
[[Landri the Cold]] asked you to enter the dungeon underneath the tower in Kur and find her old mentor, a necromancer named [[Lomas]]. She wants his skull as proof that he's fully dead.

[[Landri]] gave you a special amulet that can let you enter the ice barrier deep within the dungeon.
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Landri the Cold]]''' in '''[[Kur Mountains]]'''.  The quest is available after reading a signpost just inside the entrance of [[Kur Tower]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|

===Requirements===
* Get Past The Ice Barrier. (Use {{Item|Ice Bypass Spell}}).
* Kill Lomas, Take His Skull. (Loot {{Item|Lomas's Skull}}).
* Talk to Landri

===Rewards===
{{Spoiler|Rewards for Into the Kur Tower|
* A conversation with [[Landri the Cold]] that allows [[Ice Magic]] to be learned if [[Fire Magic]] is at least Level 25.
* This unlocks her training menu, giving access to the other skills she teaches.
}}

==Notes==
If you lose your {{Item|Ice Bypass Spell}}, you can either drop the quest and re-accept it to get a new copy, or you can trade with another player for it.
