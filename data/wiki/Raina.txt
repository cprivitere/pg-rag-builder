== Conversations ==
{{Spoiler|New Fairy Convo|
{{Quote|
[The fairy woman smiles brightly at you ... although her face falls a bit when she recognizes you. She recovers quickly, but you can't help thinking she's a little disappointed that you aren't someone else.]

Oh, (player name)! Thank the King you made it!

'''I'm sorry... who are you?'''

You don't recognize me?! Is it because I changed my hair?

'''I don't remember... anything.'''

Oh! Ohhh... that's not good.

[Raina frowns while she thinks.]

Maybe my friends made it out already, but they also lost their memories. They might not remember that we're supposed to meet here. If they don't show up in a few years I might have to go looking for them.

But you're here now! I am so glad to see you!

'''Why is that?'''

Because I'm glad you're not being tortured, of course! We bound you to this lifestone - well, a mortal helped us do it, to be honest. Otherwise you might be in the [[Winter Queen|Winter Queen's]] clutches right now.

'''Winter Queen?'''

You *have* lost your memory! The Season has changed and [[Winter Court|Winter]] is in charge. But the [[Winter Queen]] has a wasp in her tassels - she thinks that if she tortures fairies long enough they'll become Winter fairies. As if we could change our Season out of season!

[She clears her throat and looks away for a moment.]

'''That's horrible!'''

It's going to be a long 20,000 years. I just hope my friends remember to meet up here.

'''So I'm soulbound here? What does that mean?'''

When you die, you'll come back here.

'''Wait, *die*? Aren't we immortal?'''

We can still die, we just come back. But the [[Winter Queen]] has her people watching all the main lifestones. If you come back to one of those, they scoop you right up.

'''Then I'm glad I'm bound here! What now?'''

Why don't you get settled in? Flit around and meet everyone - maybe someone will spark your memory! Check with [[Pegast]] and [[Felmer]] in particular. [[Midge]] might be able to help with your memory. And you'd better talk to [[Crelpin]] about your pets. Who knows the last time they had a good flight!

'''Okay. I can do that.''' (Starts 5 quests: [[A New Life]], [[Visit Crelpin]], [[Visit Felmer]], [[Visit Midge]], and [[Visit Pegast]].)

Also, take a look in those chests and see if there is anything you can use.

'''Thanks.'''
}}
}}
