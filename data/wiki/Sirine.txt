[[Sirine]] is a 438 year-old elf, currently working as a tanner in the Rakshasa City of [[Rahu]]. She likes to tell people she once was famous for Cheesemaking. She has a "crazy" ex-wife who used to make alchemical accelerants used in [[Tanning]].
