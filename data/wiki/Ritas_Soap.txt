==Summary==
Rita in Serbule wants you to deliver some soap she made to Helena who lives in Eltibule, and can be found "somewhere in one of those little mini-castle things".

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Rita]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

{{Quote|Oh, I made some floral soap for Helena. She lives way off in Eltibule though, in one of those little mini-castle things, so I haven't had a chance to give it to her. She's always smelled a little... bad... so I thought... well, hopefully she gets the hint in time for my next orgy!}}
