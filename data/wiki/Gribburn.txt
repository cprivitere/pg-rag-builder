'''Gribburn''' is a [[Goblin]] who was once part of [[Malvol]]'s clan, and also a business partner with [[Nightshade]]. Fluency in [[Goblinese]] (20) is required to talk to him. When the [[Shadow Keepers]] threatened Nightshade, Gribburn decided to track down Malvol. He quite likes [[Hogan]]'s basement. At some point in the past, he lived in a lagoon near [[Sinoca]].

Gribburn is skilled in distilling magical oils that can be applied to helms and shoes.

: In the room to the left of the entrance corridor.
