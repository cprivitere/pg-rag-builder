== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Corpse Talking]] allows you to commune with the dead when you encounter an uncovered grave. Normally, one can choose to dig up a corpse, rebury it, or if in possession of some {{Item|Uncrossing Oil}}, purify it. Using one or more {{Item|Fetish Bag}}, a [[Corpse Talking|Corpse Talker]] may be able to listen to the thoughts, memories, and knowledge of the buried soul. Depending on your experience, you have a chance of learning about the life of the deceased (gaining experience in a random skill), obtaining a boon (handed an item), or learning forgotten knowledge (learning skills like [[Gender Studies]] and [[Demon Anatomy]]).
=== In-Game Description ===
Ability to commune telepathically with nearby dead bodies. Higher skill grants higher chances of persuading recalcitrant spirits, as well as higher comprehension rates when learning from the dead.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Jumjab]] teaches Corpse Talking to {{favor|Friends}} with 20 levels of [[Necromancy]] or [[Shamanic Infusion]]. You will need at least 35 [[Goblinese]] to understand him, and he may demand a fee of 1000 Councils.
* [[Daniel Murderdark]] teaches Corpse Talking 51-60 to those with at least 50 levels of [[Necromancy]].
* [[Aralorn]] and [[Grakelsput]] unlock Corpse Talking 61-70.

=== Uncovered Graves===
