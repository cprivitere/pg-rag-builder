'''Lugnir''' is a an [[Elven]] refugee living in [[Serbule Keep]]. He offers a series of Golem-based Challenges. 

==Location==
; [[Serbule]]
: He waits by the training dummies in the Keep.

== Trivia ==
: Despite claiming to have a beard in his text, Lugnir is clean shaven.
