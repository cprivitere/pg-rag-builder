== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Jewelry Augmentation''' is an [[Augmentation]] skill that focuses on Necklace Augments and Ring Augments.
=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge of the techniques for creating Necklace Augments and Ring Augments. (Augments for other types of equipment are covered by other skills).
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* After speaking with [[Nightshade]] in [[Kur Mountains|Kur]] and completing a task for him, '''Jewelry Augmentation''' can be learned from [[Poe]].
* Levels 71-80 and 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* Levels 81-90 and 91-100 are available to unlock from [[Corinth]].
