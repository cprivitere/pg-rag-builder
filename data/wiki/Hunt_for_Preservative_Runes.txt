==Summary==
Leonard Allenson claims that there are strange runes buried among some of the corpses inside Borghild. He wants you to find some and bring them back to him.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Leonard Allenson]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|So Borghild has hundreds of buried dead, right? But no! Actually there are <i>thousands</i> of people buried here. When the older bones collapsed into dust, people would just reuse the old caskets. Some of those caskets are 500 years old and date back to the second dwarven control of Borghild. You know what that means: yes! Some of those tombs have dwarven preservative runes! Isn't it exciting?! If you got me some, I would be so happy.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Preservative Rune}}s x5
* Talk to Leonard Allenson
