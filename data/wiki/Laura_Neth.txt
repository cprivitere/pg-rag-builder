[[Laura Neth]] is a human shopkeeper found in the Kur Mountains outpost. Her father is a chef In [[Statehelm]]. He's not a very nice man, but he's an amazing baker, and his seafood is the best Laura's ever had. It turns out Laura is actually adopted, and her father is a werewolf. For some reason, Laura dislikes Orcs. She will not talk to animals, but animals can still turn in work orders to her. 

During the winter holiday season, Laura has been known to give out the recipe for her [[Laura Neth's Famous Tart| famous tarts]].
