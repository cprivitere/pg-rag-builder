== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Genetics]] is an umbrella skill used by experts in [[Animal Husbandry]].
=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge of the genetics of creatures in general. This is an umbrella skill that encompasses a vast suite of sub-skills for particular types of creatures. This skill represents generalized genetics knowledge.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Genetics]] is automatically learned when Level 1 of the sub-skills is earned. (At this time, only [[Arthropod Genetics]] and [[Non-Ruminant Ungulate Genetics]] are in the game).
