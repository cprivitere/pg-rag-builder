==Summary==
Willem Fangblade in Red Wing Casino said, "So that last cheese, the 'cheshire', turned out to be slightly too bitter, so it doesn't go well with wine. Look, I don't fuckin' know, it's all just cheese ta me. But I need a different one. A 'gloucester'. It's supposed to be better, accordin' to my cousin that runs a stall down the hall. Ya got any of that?"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Willem Fangblade]]''' in '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''.  The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. You must also complete [[Cheese For Reasons]].
