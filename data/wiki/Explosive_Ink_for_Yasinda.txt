==Summary==
Yasinda in Hogan's Keep asked for some explosive ink.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Yasinda]]''' in '''[[Eltibule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Kar-Get on Villas Wake]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I have decided not to take Kar-Get on Wake's daughter. It is right and just, but I would feel too guilty. Instead I will use my trade to honor her. I will make an explosive map, and give Sinda a symbolic burial by fire. Hm, I am out of explosive ink. Do you have any?}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Basic Volatile Ink}} x5
* Talk to Yasinda
