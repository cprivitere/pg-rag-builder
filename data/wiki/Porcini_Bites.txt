==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "Well, you've made me wait so long for my food that I'm not even that hungry. I'll take the appetizers to go, please. Oh, don't tell me you lost my appetizer order?! It was spicy porcini bites! I need two servings. And not too greasy!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
Well, you've made me wait so long for my food that I'm not even that hungry. I'll take the appetizers to go, please. Oh, don't tell me you lost my appetizer order?! It was spicy porcini bites! I need two servings. And not too greasy!

===Midway===
Where are my porcini bites? Remember, I ordered two!

===Requirements===
* Craft Spicy Porcini Bites
* (Keep Spicy Porcini Bites) x2
* Talk to Ailred
