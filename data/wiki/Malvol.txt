[[Malvol]] is the leader of a band of goblins who escaped slavery (ie. no longer serves war contracts, in Goblin customs). Fluency in [[Goblinese]] (25) is required to talk to him, although you can still get his quests and small talk without any.

==Location==
; [[Hogan's Basement]] in [[Eltibule]]
: Found in a room to the left of the entrance corridor.
