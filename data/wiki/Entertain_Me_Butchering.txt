==Summary==
Pennoc in Serbule told you to go out and butcher a bunch of animals. You'll need a Butcher Knife or similar tool in your inventory.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Pennoc]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.

* Butcher Corpses
* Talk to Pennoc
