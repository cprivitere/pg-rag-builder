==Summary==
Ensign Cricket aboard the Wintertide said, "Any invasion force would be dumb not to take out the phoenixes on the engine deck. We don't use them in day-to-day operations, but if we have to cold-start an engine, we use their superheated breath."

This Quest can be repeated after 30 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ensign Cricket]]''' in '''[[The Wintertide]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor. 

===Preface===
Any invasion force would be dumb not to take out the phoenixes on the engine deck. We don't use them in day-to-day operations, but if we have to cold-start an engine, we use their superheated breath.

===Requirements===
* Kill Phoenix Firestarters x8
* Talk to Ensign Cricket
