==Summary==
Chirrra in  the Serbule Hills Ranalon Den said, "There's food plants everywhere in here -- look for some watercress, and bring it to me. I need a lot of it; I have an idea."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chirrra]]''' in '''[[Ranalon Den]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

This is a solo quest from Chirrra. You may have one solo quest from Chirrra at a time. You can take on a new solo quest 15 minutes after completing or canceling this quest.

===Preface===
{{Quote|There's food plants everywhere in here -- look for some watercress, and bring it to me. I need a lot of it; I have an idea.}}

This is a [[:Category:Quests/Chirrra Tier 1|Tier 1]] quest from Chirrra.

===Requirements===
* Harvest {{Item|Watercress}} x4 {{Hint|Watercress must be harvested from Ranalon Meals on the ground to count for this quest.}}
* Deliver Fresh {{Item|Watercress}}
* Talk to Chirrra
