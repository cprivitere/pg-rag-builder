==Summary==
Thedar said, "So, you want to be an Armorsmith do you? Let's start out simple! Forge and bring me one full <b>unenchanted</b> Crude Chainmail suit and I will see what I can do to help you advance your craft!
This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Thedar]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Armorsmithing]] level 17. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
So, you want to be an Armorsmith do you? Let's start out simple! Forge and bring me one full <b>unenchanted</b> Crude Chainmail suit and I will see what I can do to help you advance your craft!

===Requirements===
* Craft a Crude Chainmail Coat (NOT Enchanted)
* Possess a Crude Chainmail Coat
* Craft some Crude Chainmail Leggings (NOT Enchanted)
* Possess some Crude Chainmail Leggings
* Craft a Crude Chainmail Coif (NOT Enchanted)
* Possess a Crude Chainmail Coif
* Craft some Crude Chainmail Gloves (NOT Enchanted)
* Possess some Crude Chainmail Gloves
* Craft some Crude Chainmail Boots (NOT Enchanted)
* Possess some Crude Chainmail Boots
* Talk to Thedar
