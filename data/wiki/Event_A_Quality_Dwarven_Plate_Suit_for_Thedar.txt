==Summary==
Thedar said, "So, you want to be an Armorsmith do you? Let's move on to something more complex! Forge and bring me one full Quality Dwarven Plate suit and I will see what I can do to help you advance your craft!

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Thedar]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Armorsmithing]] level 52. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
So, you want to be an Armorsmith do you? Let's move on to something more complex! Forge and bring me one full Quality Dwarven Plate suit and I will see what I can do to help you advance your craft!

===Requirements===
* Craft a Quality Dwarven Plate Coat (Enchanted)
* Possess a Quality Dwarven Plate Coat
* Craft some Quality Dwarven Plate Greaves (Enchanted)
* Possess some Quality Dwarven Plate Greaves
* Craft a Quality Dwarven Plate Helm (Enchanted)
* Possess a Quality Dwarven Plate Helm
* Craft some Quality Dwarven Plate Gauntlets (Enchanted)
* Possess some Quality Dwarven Plate Gauntlets
* Craft some Quality Dwarven Plate Boots (Enchanted)
* Possess some Quality Dwarven Plate Boots
* Talk to Thedar
