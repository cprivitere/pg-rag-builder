==Summary==
Velkort wants me to go grab a piece of blue crystal from a nearby crypt. The entrance is in the graveyard. I'm to avoid the central room with the giant spider, and work my way around the perimeter until I reach an energy barrier. The secret code for the barrier is 397.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Velkort]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. The quest [[Get Crystal Samples]] offered by [[Blanche]] must be completed first.
