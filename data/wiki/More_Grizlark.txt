==Summary==
Ferand Deftwhisper in Povus said, "I'm using grizlark faster than I expected! Can you get me some more? I'll pay like before."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 day.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ferand Deftwhisper]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. You must have previously completed [[Only the Freshest Grizlark]] in order to undertake this quest. 

===Preface===
I'm using grizlark faster than I expected! Can you get me some more? I'll pay like before.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Grizlark x5
* Talk to Ferand Deftwhisper
