==Summary==
Norbert in Sun Vale's Animal Town asked you to obtain some raw Iocaine mushrooms, which are quite rare and only grow in the deepest and dankest of caves.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Norbert]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

{{Quote|I love mushrooms, of course, but there's one I haven't tried yet. Iocaine. It grows in deep, dark, scary caves, and it's completely lethal... but I think I know a way to make it consumable. I know it's insane, but I've just gotta have some! ... Have you got any?}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Iocaine Mushroom}}s x5
