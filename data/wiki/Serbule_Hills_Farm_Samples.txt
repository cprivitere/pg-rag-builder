==Summary==
Baroness Marith Felgard in Serbule Hills said, "I know that most of the farms in Serbule Hills have been abandoned, but I still need to soil samples from each one. It's part of my scientific duties, you understand. Here. Put a bit of soil from each farm in here and bring it back. Simple as cake! And I shall award you 700 Councils."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Marith Felgard]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|I know that most of the farms in Serbule Hills have been abandoned, but I still need to soil samples from each one. It's part of my scientific duties, you understand. Here. Put a bit of soil from each farm in here and bring it back. Simple as cake! And I shall award you 700 Councils.}}

===Requirements===
* Get Soil from Brianna Willer's Farm
* Get Soil from Julius Patton's Farm
* Get Soil from Abandoned North Farm
* Get Soil from Farm in Ranalon Territory
* Get Soil from Farm in Goblin Territory
* Talk to Marith Felgard

To get the soil samples from the farms, use the '''[[Soil Sample Container]]''' while standing in the farming areas. '''[[Marith Felgard]]''' gives you that item when starting this quest.
