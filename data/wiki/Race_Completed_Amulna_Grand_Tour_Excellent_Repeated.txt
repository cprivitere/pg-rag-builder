==Activity Quest==
[[Race Completed (Amulna Grand Tour) Excellent Repeated]] is an [[Activity Quests|Activity Quest]] which does not appear in the quest log. It shares the displayed name '''Race Completed''' with all other racing quests.

===Prerequisites===
This [[Activity Quests|Activity Quest]] is automatically completed when the [[Racing]] Amulna Grand Tour is finished with an excellent time.
