== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Beast Metabolism''' is a secondary skill under [[Lycanthropy]] that helps you get more benefit out of eating corpses while in wolf form.
=== In-Game Description ===
Represents your metabolic efficiency when consuming raw meats.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* A [[Lycanthropy|Lycanthrope]] will obtain [[Beast Metabolism]] when they first consume a carcass in wolf form.
== Connected Skills ==
=====Sub-Skills:=====
* None.
=====Secondary Skills:=====
* [[Lycanthropy]] - [[Beast Metabolism]] is a skill given to those with Lycanthropy.
===== Related Skills:=====
* [[Beast Speech]] - Beast Speech is another skill that all Lycanthropes learn.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
=== Leveling Beast Metabolism ===
Every time you eat a corpse in wolf form, you will get experience towards this skill.
