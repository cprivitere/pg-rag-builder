==Summary==
A beehive in the Fae Realm said, "Bzz. BzzZZzzz ... We need your help, fairy. The spriggan weeds ambush us while we pollinate.

Bzz. ZzzZzzZzzBz ... zzzBzt!

You must kill them!"

This Quest can be repeated after 5 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Beehive]] B''' in '''[[Fae Realm]],''' completing the quest [[Meet the Hive]]. This quest is only available to Fae.

===Preface===
Bzz. BzzZZzzz ... We need your help, fairy. The spriggan weeds ambush us while we pollinate.

Bzz. ZzzZzzZzzBz ... zzzBzt!

You must kill them!

===Requirements===
* Kill [[Spriggan Weed]]s x10
* Talk to the Bee Hive
