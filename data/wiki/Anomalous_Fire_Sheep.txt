==Summary==
Landri in the Kur Mountains asked you to cull the Fire Sheep that have been recently appearing up in Kur.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Landri]]''' in '''[[Kur Mountains]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|Have you seen those damned sheep that are always on fire? It's genetic, apparently. Some insane wizard did something to them and let fire sheep loose on the land. A stray fire sheep must have made its way here and started mating with the local population. Unfortunately it's dominant, so we need to cull these before it gets too widespread.}}

===Requirements===
* Kill [[Anomalous Sheep]] in Kur Mountains
* Talk to Landri the Cold
