==Summary==
Percussa in Rahu said, "I like to make my own drums so I can get exactly the sound i need. My newest drum needs to have a perfect resonance, and for that, I need really great animal skin! Don't tan it -- I know it'll stink, but bring me the untanned hide. I need to process it myself in a special way. I'll pay you for the work!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Percussa]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
