{{Quote|
Fire Magic is my heart, but I believe there's something beyond it. Something even more deadly. If only I had time to do more research.
}}
[[Velkort]] lives in a house in [[Serbule]], and he is found walking around near the well. He is easily noticed as there is usually a Sentient Flame creature nearby (possibly one of his creations, neato!). Speak with him, and he may share the secrets of [[Fire Magic]].

[[Velkort]] seems to harbor feelings for [[Blanche]].

See [[Fire Dust]].
