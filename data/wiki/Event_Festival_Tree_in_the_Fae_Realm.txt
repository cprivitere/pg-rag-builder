==Summary==
There's an undecorated tree in the Fae Realm. When fully decorated, townsfolk will leave you presents under the tree each day during the holiday season.

This Quest can be repeated after 91 days.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Backfat]]''' in '''[[Fae Realm]]'''.

===Requirements===
* Get Butterflies for the Tree
* Get Fae Honeycombs for the Tree
* Get Pixie Dust and Pipe for the Tree
* Get Safety Equipment for the Tree
* Talk to [[Backfat]]
