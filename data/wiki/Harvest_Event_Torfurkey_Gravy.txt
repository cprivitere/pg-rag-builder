==Summary==
Ricatu in Povus said, "Gravy would be perfect with this tofurkey you brought me -- give it some extra moistness. Can you make me some? You'll have to find the recipe yourself, though."
===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ricatu]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''.  The quest is available during the [[Fall Harvest]]. The quest [[Harvest Event: Tofurkey!]] must be completed.
