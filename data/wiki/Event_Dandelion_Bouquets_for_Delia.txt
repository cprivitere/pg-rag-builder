==Summary==
Delia said, "Hi! I am flat exhausted from traveling so much. Everyone else is too! We need some fresh flowers to perk everyone up. Can you make me 10 [[Dandelion Bouquet]]s? I will gladly give you some new ideas to expand your arrangement choices for it."

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Delia]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Flower Arrangement]] level 70. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Hi! I am flat exhausted from traveling so much. Everyone else is too! We need some fresh flowers to perk everyone up. Can you make me 10 [[Dandelion Bouquet]]s? I will gladly give you some new ideas to expand your arrangement choices for it.

===Requirements===
* Make 10 Dandelion Bouquet
* Obtain 10 Dandelion Bouquet
* Talk to Delia
