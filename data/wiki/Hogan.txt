{{Quote|Make yourself comfortable in my courtyard.

'''So this is your keep?'''

This place was constructed a decade ago as a guardhouse, where Council soldiers would patrol the trade route from here to Gazluk. Of course, that trade route is unlikely to exist now. I was given permission to live here and perhaps found a monastery. In exchange, I keep the monster population in check.
}}
[[Hogan]] is the owner of the castle, referred in-game as ''Hogan's Keep''. Hogan is a [[Staff|staff combat]] expert and is usually seen practicing his moves, when not talking to the visitors.
