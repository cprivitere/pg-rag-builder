==Summary==
Jaime Fatholm would appreciate if you killed a sheep that's humiliated her. She said, "He lives on a hill south of here. Not the hill immediately south, but the one past that, in between the Baroness and the old hunting lodge."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Jaime Fatholm]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.

To begin this quest you must have level 1 [[Mining]].

{{Quote|Look. We're friends so I'm gonna level with you. I'm... I'm a little scared to go back out there. There was a sheep that nearly killed me. Just a fuckin' <i>sheep</i>. Not the hill immediately south, but the one past that, in between the Baroness and the old hunting lodge.}}
