== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Milling]] is a skill that determines success and yield when milling items, such as barley into flour.
=== In-Game Description ===
Skill in properly operating a grainmill.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* Learned automatically after milling an item at a Grainmill's Grindstone.
== Connected Skills ==
===== Sub-Skills: =====
* None
===== Secondary Skills: =====
* [[Foraging]] - Foraging is needed to gather the seeds required for [[Milling]].
* [[Gardening]] - Gardening is needed to grow the plants milled into various flours using [[Milling]].
===== Related Skills: =====
* [[Cooking]] - Flour produced with [[Milling]] can be used in various [[Cooking]] recipes.

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
==== Grainmill Yields ====
Each item compatible with [[Milling]] has a fixed Difficulty Level and XP gain for a successful mill. The average yield (how many milled outputs are received for an input) of an item is variable and improves as [[Milling]] levels are raised. 
'''{{Item|Flax Seeds}}''' - Difficulty Level 1, 25 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Ground Flaxseed}}.
* x3.00 yield at level 40.
'''{{Item|Barley}}''' - Difficulty Level 5, 50 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Barley Flour}}.
* x1.00 yield at Milling level 0.
'''{{Item|Sugarcane}}''' - Difficulty Level 5, 50 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Sugar}}.
* x7.10 yield at Milling level 36.
* x7.50 yield at Milling level 40.
'''[[:Category:Items/Seed|Flower Seeds]]''' - Difficulty Level 10, 75 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Ground Flower Seeds}}.
* x0.50 yield at Milling level 0.
* x2.20 yield at Milling level 17.
* x3.00 yield at Milling level 25.
* x3.00 yield at Milling level 36.
'''{{Item|Tundra Rye}}''' - Difficulty Level 15, 100 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Rye Flour}}.
* x3.00 yield at Milling level 40.
* x3.00 yield at Milling level 45.
'''{{Item|Almonds}}''' - Difficulty Level 18, 110 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Almond Flour}}.
* x3.00 yield at Milling level 40.
* x3.00 yield at Milling level 45.
'''{{Item|Oat Groats}}''' - Difficulty Level 25, 150 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Oat Flour}}.
* x0.25 yield at Milling level 0.
* x0.70 yield at Milling level 17.
* x1.50 yield at Milling level 25.
* x3.00 yield at Milling level 36.
* x3.00 yield at Milling level 45.
'''{{Item|Corn}}''' - Difficulty Level 30, 250 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Cornmeal}}.
* x0.00 yield until ~Milling level 22.
* x2.10 yield at Milling level 36.
* x2.50 yield at Milling level 40.
'''{{Item|Orcish Wheat}}''' - Difficulty Level 35, 500 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Orcish Flour}}.
* x0.00 yield until ~Milling level 22.
* x2.00 yield at Milling level 40.
* x2.50 yield at Milling level 45.
'''{{Item|Cocoa Beans}}''' - Difficulty Level 45, 750 Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Primitive Cocoa Powder}}.
* x0.25 yield at Milling level 36.
* x0.50 yield at Milling level 40.


'''{{Item|Chia Seeds}}''' - Difficulty Level ?, ? Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Ground Chia Seeds}}.
'''{{Item|Moonspelt}}''' - Difficulty Level ?, ? Milling XP per success.
* Milled into {{Item|Moonspelt Flour}}.

==== Grindstones ====
Grindstones are intractable objects inside Grainmills which are used in the milling process.
* [[Serbule]] - Inside the base of a windmill at Serbule Keep.
* [[Vidaria]] - Inside the base of a windmill on the southwestern farms.
==== Notes on Output ====
* A single stack of [[Barley]] can take a character from Level 0 to Level 17 and may result in about 220 [[Barley Flour]].
