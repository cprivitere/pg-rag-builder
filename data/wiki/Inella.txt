[[Inella]] Spawns in the doorway near [[Kunax the Dread]] after you slay him and provides training for various unlocks and recipes.

== Location ==
[[Elven Judgement]]
:Spawns in the doorway near [[Kunax the Dread]] after you slay him.

== Courses ==
Inella will teach one free course every time you slay [[Kunax the Dread]].


'''Learn [[Bladesmithing]]'''

Requires 50 blacksmithing 


'''Unlock [[Alchemy]] Skill Levels 51-60'''

"Even the most professional alchemists won't be able to handle this course! Hands-on lessons cover the creation of astounding inks."


'''Unlock [[Sigil Scripting]] Skill Levels 61-70'''

"Taught by our own famed alumni, the Ogre Mage Rugl, this course requires deft hands and vast experience. Comes with homework."


'''Mastering Linen'''

"Learn the ancient elven techniques of growing and retting linen. While time-consuming to create, linen clothing is valuable to mages and the esoterically inclined. Includes flax seeds." Requires Textile Creation 70


'''Toolcrafting Across Cultures'''

"An invaluable lens into the world of toolcrafting. Teaches multiple recipes for these advanced tools: Weapon Whetstones, Jeweler's Maintenance Kits, Reweighting Kits, and Weapons-Grade Sandpaper." Requires Toolcrafting 75


'''Horse Riding'''

"I know that you of all people don't need a course on this, but it's a ruse, you see: we're still boarding your horse. She's gotten a bit strange since you left, but still quite healthy! This lesson is just a pretext to let you take possession of her. You'll need a free stable slot, of course."
