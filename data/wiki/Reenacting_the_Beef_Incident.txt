==Summary==
[[Farsight Flemmings]] wants you to re-enact the events in Vidaria surrounding the sentient steak. (NOTE: you MUST use the downstairs fireplace in the inn to cook the Burnt Steak. The upstairs will complete the first step but will NOT trigger the second step)


===Requirements===
* Cook [[Burnt Steak]] at the Inn in Vidaria 
* Defend yourself (0/3)
