{{Quote|source=[[Sem]]|
As a replacement for my myconian pod allies, you have certain strengths, but you continue to fall short in categories such as soil preparation, spore singing, and, perhaps most critically, not-moving-around-all-the-damned-time. Please pick and area to work on, and try to improve a little bit with each moon phase.

[[Sem]] is a trainer in one of [[Gazluk]]'s caves. He will teach you the basics of mushroom teleportation, with high enough favor. 

Note: Sem requires you to be more fluent in [[Myconic]] compared to the other friendly Myconians . {{Hint|You need 15 Myconic to speak to Sem}}
