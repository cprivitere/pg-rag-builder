==Summary==
Moxie said, "Hey there! We recently joined the caravan to explore new methods of jewelry crafting. How about we get started with you? Craft me 5 Great Magic Rings and 5 Great Enchanted Necklaces. Bring them to me to study, and in return I will share some of my knowledge with you. Sound like a fair trade?"

This Quest can be repeated after 20 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Moxie]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. This quest is available at [[Jewelry Crafting]] level 66. This quest is only available during a Crafting Caravan event.

===Preface===
Hey there! We recently joined the caravan to explore new methods of jewelry crafting. How about we get started with you? Craft me 5 Great Magic Rings and 5 Great Enchanted Necklaces. Bring them to me to study, and in return I will share some of my knowledge with you. Sound like a fair trade?

===Requirements===
* Make 5 Great Magic Rings
* Obtain 5 Great Magic Rings
* Make 5 Great Enchanted Necklaces
* Obtain 5 Great Enchanted Necklaces
* Talk to Moxie
