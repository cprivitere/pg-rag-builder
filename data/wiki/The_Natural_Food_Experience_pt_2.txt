==Summary==
Tavilak in Red Wing Casino said, "Thank you for aiding me. If you have time to continue the Natural Food Experience, please grow several beets and mention that it is a joy to do so."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Tavilak]]''' in the '''[[Red Wing Casino]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. You must have previously completed [[The Natural Food Experience]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
Thank you for aiding me. If you have time to continue the Natural Food Experience, please grow several beets and mention that it is a joy to do so.

===Requirements===
* Grow Beets x6
* Say aloud 'Growing beets is my favorite casino activity!'
* Talk to Tavilak
