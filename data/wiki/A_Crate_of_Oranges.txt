==Summary==
Umarak in Povus said, "All our supplies are gone, so I've had lots of trouble feeding Tryaka and myself. We're making due, but I really miss having an orange for breakfast each morning. And our gums are starting to bleed... I fear that's somehow related."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Umarak]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
All our supplies are gone, so I've had lots of trouble feeding Tryaka and myself. We're making due, but I really miss having an orange for breakfast each morning. And our gums are starting to bleed... I fear that's somehow related.

===Midway===
I wish oranges grew around here...

===Requirements===
* Obtain Oranges x24
* Talk to Umarak
