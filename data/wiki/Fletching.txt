== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Fletching''' is the art of crafting [[arrow]]s and their components.  Currently, fletching is rather expensive and time consuming making [[Archery]] somewhat less attractive than other combat skills.
=== In-Game Description ===
The craft of arrow-making. Most fletching recipes require elements obtained from Carpentry, so it's often handy to know both skills well. At higher level, fletchers also need various Alchemy components.
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Elahil]] in [[Serbule]] will offer to train you in fletching up to skill level 50.  He also sells various components needed for fletching.
* [[Bellema Deftwhisper]] at [[New Prestonbule Cave]] in [[Gazluk]] unlocks level 51-60.
* [[Floxie]] in [[Fae Realm]] unlocks levels 61-70 and 71-80.
* [[Mara Woodwright]] in [[Statehelm]] unlocks levels 81-91.

== Connected Skills ==

===== Related Skills: =====
* [[Carpentry]] - Using this, you can create the dowels used in fletching.
* [[Archery]] - Once the arrows are crafted, archery uses them.
* [[Crossbow]] -Arrows from this can be used for the crossbow sidebar skill.
* [[Alchemy]]

==Mechanics==
===Drying Boxes===

Drying boxes can only be used in sunny weather during the day from 6:00am-5:59pm.

Be wary of placing drying boxes on inclines, as they can be placed into world geometry and can't be further interacted with (preventing you from placing further drying boxes.)  If you are stuck unable to place further drying boxes because of an inaccessible previous box, you can remove your currently placed drying boxes with the command "/zapresources fletching".  This will give you a code that must be added to the end of that command to confirm deletion.  When you run the zapresources along with the code, all of your current boxes will be deleted from the world, allowing you to place more again.

====Number of Boxes====
*'''1''' box at a time may be dried starting at Fletching Level '''1'''.
*'''2''' boxes at level '''10'''.
*'''3''' boxes at level '''20'''.
*'''4''' boxes at level '''30'''.
*'''5''' boxes at level '''40'''.
*'''6''' boxes at level '''50'''.
*'''7''' boxes at level '''60'''.
