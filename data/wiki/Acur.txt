[[Acur]], the dark dimension of Angst, was once home to [[Zek]], a former god of death.
Before his defeat during the God War at the hands of his brother [[Vol]], [[Zek]] poured his power into a soul-catching chalice and hurled it into Acur, knowing that most other gods would be unable to perceive the entrance to the realm.

Towards the end of the [[God War]], [[Ilth Hale]] found her way into [[Acur]].
