[[Harry the Wolf]] is a quest NPC and vendor for animal form players, and players seeking [[Lycanthropy]]. [[Harry the Wolf|Harry]] holds a distaste for most humanoids, and will refuse to speak to them unless he receives assurances that they are trustworthy from an existing friend. 
== Location ==
; [[Serbule]]
: In the Serbule Keep garden, near to [[Therese]]
== Shopkeeper ==
[[Harry the Wolf]] sells [[First Aid]] and [[Armor Patching]] kits, as well as various potions and armor for players who can't avail themselves of [[Joeh]]'s and [[Marna]]'s services. He also sells belts for [[Lycanthropy]].

{{Spoiler| Befriending [[Harry the Wolf]]|
