==Summary==
Paul Vaughn in Serbule Hills said, "This is gonna sound silly. But you know that watery beer Durstin sells? I found a way to improve it: strawberry juice! I just need a few strawberries for each cup. If you have some, I'll buy 'em from you for... I dunno, ten times their value!"

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Paul Vaughn]]''' in '''[[Serbule Hills]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.
