==Summary==
Joeh in Serbule asked you to kill a tiger called "Old Fangsworth", rumored to live somewhere north of Serbule.

==Prerequisites==
To start this quest, talk to [[Joeh]] in [[Serbule]].  The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.

==Preface==
{{Quote|Damn it, he's still out there! Mocking me! The only tiger to escape my blade! Old Fangsworth is a crafty beast; I admit he bested me when I was younger. I could kill him now, but I no longer have the time to stalk him and take him out.}}

==Requirements==
* Kill [[Old Fangsworth]] {{Hint| Old Fangsworth can be found in the northeastern part of Serbule, close to the road that leads to the port.|}}
* Talk to [[Joeh]]
