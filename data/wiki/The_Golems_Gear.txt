==Summary==
Leonard Allenson believes there are golems inside Borghild, and asked you to find a Cranial Gear from the head of one.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Leonard Allenson]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|An old tome I found said there was an entire system of automata in Borghild: robots! Lots of 'em! They'd be sealed off away from the rest of the place now, I suppose. And probably all broken. But if you find a broken one, can you see if it has a large gear in its head? The book talks about something called a Cranial Gear. I'd love to have one.}}

===Requirements===
* Obtain {{Item|Cranial Gear}}
* Talk to Leonard Allenson
