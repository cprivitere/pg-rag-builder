You can learn the skill from Rappanel in South Serbule. (He's in the inn.) == {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Bard''' is a skill intended for Adventurers looking to provide support to their fellow travelers. The Bard skillset can only be used by humanoid races at this time, and travelers confined to [[Beast Forms]] will find it difficult, even impossible to use.
=== In-Game Description ===
Bardin'
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
<!-- A list of methods to learn and level the skill -->
* [[Rappanel]] is a teacher for beginner [[Bard]]s, and can be found along the old trade-route trail in [[Serbule Hills]], inside the Tapestry Inn. 
* [[Percussa]] can raise the level cap of [[Bard]] from 51-60, and from 61-70. She can be found in Rahu. During the day she performs in front of the restaurant and in the evening she returns home to the hostile part of the city. 
* [[Raina]] unlocks 71-80.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Emersil the Ever-Gloating]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Ormond Neth]] unlocks 81-90 and 91-100. You'll need complete the trial from [[Rebeccah the Whisperer]] first

== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Mechanics ==
Bards can play with a lute and/or horn. Horn abilities focus on dealing damage, lute abilities are songs (see below).

=== Songs ===
Bards using a lute can play a song for constant effects like healing or damage. When moving, the song is interrupted so it is a good idea to pull groups of enemies or have a high range attack to pull. Switching to a different song will interrupt the current song.
=== Weapons ===
Lute instruments are all MainHand, while everything else is OffHand. Even though only the Lutes are tagged as Wooden, [[Druid]]s may use all instruments.'''

=== Hymn of Resurrection ===
[[Bard]]s have access to the [[Revive|revival]] ability [[Hymn of Resurrection]]. To unlock and use this ability, a [[Bard]] must be wearing a Main Hand item with the proper treasure effect. The required treasure only appears on {{ItemRarity|Epic}} or {{ItemRarity|Legendary}} equipment at Levels 50 and 70. Although the ability can be learned from any Main Hand item with the treasure, a Lute with the treasure must be held to actually use the ability.

'''of Hymnals''' - ''Your Bard Songs cost -20% Power. In addition, you can use the ability Hymn of Resurrection.''
