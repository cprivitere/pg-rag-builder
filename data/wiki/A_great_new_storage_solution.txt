==Summary==
Lincha in Povus said, "Back before the orc attacks, my home was beautiful. Even my storage chests were beautiful pieces of art! They sell similar chests in Rahu, but I know those are made by goblin-owned slaves. I need to know my furniture is made by someone free!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lincha]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
Back before the orc attacks, my home was beautiful. Even my storage chests were beautiful pieces of art! They sell similar chests in Rahu, but I know those are made by goblin-owned slaves. I need to know my furniture is made by someone free!

===Midway===
I wish I had a great new storage crate...

===Requirements===
* Craft Great Storage Crate
* (Keep Great Storage Crate)
* Talk to Lincha
