==Summary==
Fazzi in the Winter Nexus asked you to free some Summer fae.

===Prerequisites===

To start this quest, talk to '''[[Fazzi]]''' in '''[[Winter Nexus]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

===Preface===
A Summer fairy named Fazzi taught you a ritual that you can use to free some Summer fae that are being tortured in the Winter Corridors.

===Requirements===
* Free Tortured Fairies (3)
* Talk to Fazzi

Freeing the fairies requires [[Winterprize]]. The objective is shared between your group, so with 3 Winterprize you can complete the objective for 6 people at once.

===Rewards===
*50 favor
*Unlocks the ability to create new fairy characters on the player's current server.
