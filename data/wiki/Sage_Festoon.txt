[[Sage Festoon]] appears to be a human who lives in the city of [[Statehelm]]. He can be found at the Vicious Bat during the day. Around 6pm, [[Sage Festoon]] leaves the shop to return home.
== Location ==
; [[Statehelm]]
: [[Sage Festoon]] may be found wandering the city of Statehelm during the day.
== Favor ==
=== Small Talk ===
[[Sage Festoon]] uses the favor system, but does not offer any favor or conversation options to individuals with '''{{favor|Neutral}}''' favor.

[[Sage Festoon]] gains favor from completing [[Sagiso]]'s daily graffiti quest.

[[Sage Festoon]] has the same favor as [[Sagiso]], so gifting [[Sagiso]] [[Mentalism]] skill books will increase his favor.
