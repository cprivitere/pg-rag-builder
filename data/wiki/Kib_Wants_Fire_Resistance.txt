==Summary==
Kib the Unkillable in Red Wing Casino said, "I learned something fascinating yesterday: there are relatively low-cost fire resistance potions on this continent. Those are quite rare in my homeland. Sadly they don't see much use here in the casino. I bought Ragabir's stock, but I have more tests to run. Can you bring me several doses?"
