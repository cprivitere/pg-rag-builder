== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview ==

=== In-Game Description ===
The ability to smith and shape raw materials into aesthetically-pleasing rings and necklace, requiring a forge to make. For a list of forge locations, see [[Blacksmithing]].

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Solgribue]] in the [[Fae Realm]] teaches Jewelry Crafting, and also provides the unlocks for 51-60, 61-70, and 71-80.  Learning Jewelry Crafting requires 50 [[Blacksmithing]].
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* [[Elune]] in [[Statehelm]] unlocks 81-90 and 91-100.
