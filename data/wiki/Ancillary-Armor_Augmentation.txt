== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
'''Ancillary-Armor Augmentation''' is an [[Augmentation]] skill that focuses on Helm Augments and Shoe Augments.
=== In-Game Description ===
Knowledge of the techniques for creating Helm Augments and Shoes Augments. (Augments for other types of equipment are covered by other skills).
=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* After speaking with [[Nightshade]] in [[Kur Mountains|Kur]] and completing a task for him, '''Ancillary-Armor-Augmentation''' can be learned from [[Gribburn]].<br>It requires 25 [[Alchemy]] and 25 [[Tailoring]] to learn.
* [[Kohan]] or [[Ufkar]] will unlock levels 51-60, and 61-70.
* Levels 71-80 and 81-90 are unlocked by [[Dunahain Broadarm]] deep beneath [[Povus]].
* Levels 81-90 and 91-100 are available to unlock from [[Corinth]].
