==Summary==
Gerrux in Povus said, "I like to refine weapons by giving them an artistic touch. I'm working on a combat beaker -- I haven't done one of these since my sister died! It seems I'm out of blue glass, though. Do you have any? Oh, I need a bit of pyrite too."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Gerrux]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor. 

===Preface===
I like to refine weapons by giving them an artistic touch. I'm working on a combat beaker -- I haven't done one of these since my sister died! It seems I'm out of blue glass, though. Do you have any? Oh, I need a bit of pyrite too.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Pieces of Blue Glass x4
* Obtain Pyrite x3
* Talk to Gerrux
