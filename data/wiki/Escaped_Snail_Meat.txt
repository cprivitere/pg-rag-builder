==Summary==
[[Lady Windsong]] in [[Rahu]] said, "The sewers connect to a cave system, and last year I noticed that some of the ratkins' snails had escaped and wandered into the caves. The [[ratkin]] didn't seem interested in recovering them, and I really want to know why. In this case I think the death of a few innocent snails is justified. Bring me a sample so that I can examine it."

===Prerequisites===

To start this quest, talk to '''[[Lady Windsong]]''' in '''[[Rahu]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor. This quest is available at [[Druid]] level 1. This quest is only available after a speech interaction. Also, you need to have already unlocked the upper level shortcut access door next to the Weird Leaves in the '''[[Rahu Sewers]]''' by defeating the '''[[Treant Guardian]]''' and looting his key.  You also have to travel the Weird Leaves and read the a book at the other end. Until then, '''[[Lady Windsong]]''' will not offer this quest.

===Preface===
The sewers connect to a cave system, and last year I noticed that some of the ratkins' snails had escaped and wandered into the caves.
