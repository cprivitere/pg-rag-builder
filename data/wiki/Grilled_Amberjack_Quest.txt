==Summary==
Ailred in Povus said, "I've had a VERY long time to look at the menu, so yes, I'm ready to place my food order! I saw that you have fresh amberjack on the fish list. How is that even possible?! I guess amberjack is native to here? Well, I simply must try this dish."

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Ailred]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
I've had a VERY long time to look at the menu, so yes, I'm ready to place my food order! I saw that you have fresh amberjack on the fish list. How is that even possible?! I guess amberjack is native to here? Well, I simply must try this dish.

===Midway===
Where's my grilled amberjack? And can I get a refill on my water?

===Requirements===
* Craft Grilled Amberjack
* (Keep Grilled Amberjack)
* Talk to Ailred
