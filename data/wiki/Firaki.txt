[[Firaki]] is a female [[Rakshasa]] living in the walled town of [[Amulna]] in the [[Ilmari]] Desert. She was fed up with the great numbers of merchants and craftsmen in the city of [[Rahu]], and moved to Amulna to find new customers. 
== Location ==
; [[Ilmari]]
: Found in the central market of the town of [[Amulna]].
