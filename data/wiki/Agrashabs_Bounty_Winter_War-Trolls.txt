==Summary==
Agrashab in the Fae Realm said, "I do not give commandments to my children. Trolls can form alliances with anyone they want! But that doesn't mean I will stand idly by when a pack of potent war-trolls comes here and starts wreaking havoc! These winter trolls are extremely powerful and need to be stopped. Remember: I only want you to kill the giant blue trolls, not the green ones."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to [[Agrashab/Fae Realm|Agrashab]] in the [[Fae Realm]]. The quest is available at {{Favor|Despised}} favor.
