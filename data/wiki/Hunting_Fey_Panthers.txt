You're killing fey panthers in Eltibule.

===Preface===
<b>Hunting: Fey Panthers</b>
These big green panthers come from the fairy realm. Semi-intelligent, they seek to cause mischief and destruction. Regular panthers seem to instinctively treat them as royalty, following their lead and protecting their flank.
