== Appearances ==
* First spotted between July 14th and July 20th 2023 as part of the '''Going Batty''' event.
* May appear for one week throughout the year.

== Training ==
While in [[Serbule]], [[Happy-Go-Lucky Bat]] offers interested adventurers the chance to transform into a [[Giant Bat]].
{{Quote |
You are not a giant bat? How can you live with yourself?!

Hah! I'm joking. We bats are the best, but not everyone can see that. But ... I can help. For a limited time, I can help you achieve your best life - as a giant bat!
; Yes! I want to be a giant bat!
Yes! Now you are a beautiful, bountiful, brilliant giant bat! Now you can start living your best life - or, you know, killing with your great new batty body!
:; Wait! I changed my mind. Can you change me back?
:: Oh no! You don't like being a bat? Well, clearly you are doing it wrong!

If you insist ... yeah, I can un-bat you. But you'll be missing out on the wonders of being a giant bat!

Okay then. Fine. You are no longer a bat. Now why don't you get out of here before you bring down the neighborhood.
::; Nevermind. Can you just make me a better giant bat?
