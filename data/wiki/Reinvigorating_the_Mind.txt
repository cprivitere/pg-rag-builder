==Summary==
[[Truxartis]] asked me to find a Tuvillic Rune in [[Povus]] beneath a giant mushroom and retrieve the Mnemonic Index. This quest becomes available after completing [[Khyrulek's Planar Planner]] and can run in parallel with the rest of Chain A.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Truxartis]]''' in '''[[Vidaria]]'''. The quest is available after completing [[Khyrulek's Planar Planner]].

===Preface===
{{Quote|My mind is... Fuzzy. I need to realign it. Yes! I know what to do.

You should find a Tuvillic Rune in Povus, beneath a giant mushroom. Touch the rune and retrieve the Mnemonix Index. When you enter the caves, turn left and you should see it.}}

===Requirements===
* Touch Vampire Rune beneath a Giant Mushroom in [[Povus]]
* Obtain {{Item|Mnemonic Index}}
* Talk to [[Truxartis]]

===Notes===
{{Spoiler|Notes|
The Mnemonic Index is found in [[Fungal Fortress]], located beneath the giant mushroom in southeastern [[Povus]]. The fungoid NPC [[Mope]], who sits on top of the mushroom, will teleport you into [[Fungal Fortress]] if you have at least Friend-level favor with him.
