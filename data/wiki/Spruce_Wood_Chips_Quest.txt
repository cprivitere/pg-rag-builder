==Summary==
Umarak in Povus said, "It'll soon be time for outdoor grilling again! And my grill was destroyed by orcs. But I can fix it! What I can't fix is the lack of fuel. How can I make perfect food without perfect fuel? It's impossible! I need spruce wood chips!"

This Quest can be repeated after 1 hour.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Umarak]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. This quest is only available during daytime. 

===Preface===
It'll soon be time for outdoor grilling again! And my grill was destroyed by orcs. But I can fix it! What I can't fix is the lack of fuel. How can I make perfect food without perfect fuel? It's impossible! I need spruce wood chips!

===Midway===
I sure could use a huge pile of spruce wood chips.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Spruce Wood Chips x6
* Talk to Umarak
