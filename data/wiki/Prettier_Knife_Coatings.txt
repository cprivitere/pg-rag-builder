==Summary==
Gerrux in Povus said, "I'm working on artistically improving a dagger right now, but I need something very special: blue glass. But not any old blue glass. Only the glass from a pair of ratkin goggles will do! I also need several sets of hexapus teeth. I'm afraid this dagger will not be subtle."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Gerrux]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Comfortable}} favor. 

===Preface===
I'm working on artistically improving a dagger right now, but I need something very special: blue glass. But not any old blue glass. Only the glass from a pair of ratkin goggles will do! I also need several sets of hexapus teeth. I'm afraid this dagger will not be subtle.

* Obtain Ratkin Glare Protectors
* Obtain Hexapus Mouth Rings x4
