== {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} Overview==
[[Fairy Magic]] is a [[Mentalism]] sub-skill used by Fae adventurers.
=== In-Game Description ===
A sub-skill of Mentalism that focuses on psychic links to the fae realms.

=== Training {{msg:BASEPAGENAME}} ===
* [[Preta]] in [[Sun Vale]] offers the Level 51-60 and Level 61-70 unlocks.
* [[Raina]] in the [[Fae Realm]] offers the Level 71-80 unlock.
* Levels 81-90 are unlocked by [[Reinath]] deep beneath [[Povus]].

===Equipment===
Crafters producing equipment for use by [[Fairy Magic]] users should obtain the [[Sigil Scripting]] recipe '''Fairy Magic Inscription,''' which allows for the creation of {{Item|Fairy Magic Topaz}}. 

This attuned gemstone can be used in any recipe which calls for the use of [[:Category:Items/Crystal|Crystals]] to create equipment with [[Fairy Magic]] Treasure Effects.
