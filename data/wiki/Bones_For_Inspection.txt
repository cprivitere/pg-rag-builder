==Summary==
Brugo in Povus said, "If I'm to quickly become a master of paleontology, I need a constant supply of bones to study! But I can't go collect the bones, since that would require me to stop studying. Perhaps you're for hire? I will pay you for 10 good old fossils."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Brugo the Wise]]''' in '''[[Povus]]'''. 

===Preface===
If I'm to quickly become a master of paleontology, I need a constant supply of bones to study! But I can't go collect the bones, since that would require me to stop studying. Perhaps you're for hire? I will pay you for 10 good old fossils.

===Requirements===
* Obtain Paleontology Bones x10
* Talk to Brugo
