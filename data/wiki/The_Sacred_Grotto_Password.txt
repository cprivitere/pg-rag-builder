==Summary==
An animal friend has shared the secret password to the Sacred Grotto hidden in the Kur Mountains. The entrance is in the center of Kur, near a river. You were instructed to look for a huge stone arch attached to a large hill; there is a door embedded in the hillside a small distance away from the arch. As another reference point, the entrance is northwest of "Fox Outpost".

Once there, you need to provide the secret pass phrase, "Horse-Rabbit-Maelstrom".



===Requirements===
* Knock on the door and give the pass phrase
