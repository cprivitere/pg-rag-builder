==Summary==
Glortok in the Rahu Sewers said, "Go to the caves and destroy the living ratkin miners. With only undead miners left, their necromancers will be overwhelmed, and we can strike!"

This Quest can be repeated after 66 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chief Glortok]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Goblin Avenger: Off-Duty Ratkin Miners]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
Go to the caves and destroy the living ratkin miners. With only undead miners left, their necromancers will be overwhelmed, and we can strike!

===Requirements===
* Kill Ratkin Miners x15
* Talk to Glortok
