==Summary==
Fainor in Serbule wishes to know the secret of Ur-Bacon, but the recipe is known only to the undead.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Fainor]]''' in '''[[Serbule]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Friends}} favor.

===Preface===
{{Quote|If you were a REAL friend, you would help me master the ancient recipe for forbidden bacon. Ur-Bacon is infused with the spirits of the dead. What a thrill it'd be to serve that! But of course, only the dead know the secret. You might find it written down somewhere, but it'd be incredibly rare. Sigh.}}

* Obtain the Recipe for {{Item|Ur-Bacon}}
