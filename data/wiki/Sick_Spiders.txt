==Summary==
[[Silvia]] in [[Sun Vale]] said, "I'm getting reports that some of the spiders near here are developing some sort of spinneret-related disease. If that's true, I may be able to identify the problem if I can study enough of their spiderwebs."

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Silvia]]''' in '''[[Sun Vale]]'''. You must have previously completed [[Rabid Deer]] in order to undertake this quest. This quest is only available to [[Fairy|Fae]].
