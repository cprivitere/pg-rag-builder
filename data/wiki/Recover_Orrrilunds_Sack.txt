==Summary==
Orrrilund in the Ranalon Den said, "You found what? You mean they weren't torn completely to shreds? That's great! They were supposed to collect something for me. The next time you're in there, look for a sack that they had and grab it for me."

===Prerequisites===
To begin this quest, speak with [[Orrrilund]] in the [[Ranalon Den]]. The quest is available after completing [[Kill the Ranalon Den Mother]].
