==Summary==
Glortok in the Rahu Sewers said, "The enemy lounges and relaxes as if they were not at war! Fix it! By murdering every lazy ratkin you see!"

This Quest can be repeated after 66 hours.

===Prerequisites===
To start this quest, talk to '''[[Chief Glortok]]''' in the '''[[Rahu Sewer]]'''. The quest is available at {{Favor|Neutral}} favor.

You must have previously completed [[Goblin Avenger: Ratkin Fishtenders]] in order to undertake this quest.

===Preface===
The enemy lounges and relaxes as if they were not at war! Fix it! By murdering every lazy ratkin you see!

===Requirements===
* Kill Relaxing Ratkin Miners x15
* Talk to Glortok
