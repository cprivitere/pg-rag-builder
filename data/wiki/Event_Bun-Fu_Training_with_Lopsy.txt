==Summary==
Hi! Would you be interested in learning the ways of the carrot? I can help you get started!

First off, you need to know how to seek out your carrots. I have placed several Carrots of Power across the landscape and in some dungeons here in the Serbule and Serbule Hills area. Seek out seven of these Carrots of Power and then return to me. Also, you must interact with the Carrots of Power as a rabbit! You cannot become a Bun-Fu Master if you're not a rabbit!

If you need to bunny up, just ask! Good hunting!

This Quest can be repeated after 15 days.

This quest is only available during a BunFu event. 

===Requirements===
* Touch 7 Carrots of Power
* Speak to Lopsy
